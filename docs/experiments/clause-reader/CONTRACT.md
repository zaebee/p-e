# Contract

Set by **bee.zae**. Reproduced verbatim; nothing in it was written by anyone whose
work you will be compared against.

1. Purpose
Implement an independent reading of the frozen catalogue clauses, not an audit of our existing clause.ts and not a certification of the suite. The reader must derive its interpretation from the frozen clause text itself.

2. Inputs
- frozen CATALOGUE.md / frozen catalogue revision;
- the published corpus needed to evaluate the clauses;
- no OBS findings, prior verdicts, relay commentary about expected answers, or our interpretation of the clauses;
- no access to the existing clause.ts during the reading phase, except later as a comparison target.

3. Output for each clause
- exact clause identifier;
- quoted/located falsifier text;
- executable predicate or explicit decision procedure derived from that text;
- required evidence fields/files;
- treatment of missing evidence, including EXCLUDED vs UNDECIDABLE where applicable;
- verdict and the minimal evidence supporting it;
- any ambiguity in the clause that prevents a unique execution, stated as ambiguity rather than silently resolved.

4. Independence rule
The reader must not optimize for agreement with the existing reader, suite, or prior human/agent conclusions. If its reading disagrees, preserve the disagreement and point to the exact clause text that caused it.

7. No trusted auditor
The independent reader is not a trusted authority. Its independence is itself a property to be examined. A disagreement is a finding candidate, not an automatic fault of either implementation.

## A note on what is not here, written by the party preparing this bundle

The contract has nine numbered sections. Five are reproduced above — the ones
addressed to you. Four are addressed to the people running the experiment and
describe what they will do with your reading afterwards: how to compare it, which
pairs are comparable, an unrelated repair, and what would count as success.

They were held back for one reason, stated so you can judge it: one of them names
which clauses an existing implementation covers, and knowing that would tell you
where somebody else's attention has already been. Nothing else in them is withheld
for any other purpose, and this note exists so the withholding is visible rather
than silent.

If that seems like the wrong call, say so in your output. It was made by the party
whose implementation your reading will be compared against, which is exactly the
position that should not go unexamined.
