## Claim A — the two-class split

**The mechanism does not answer its own question, and the bypass is free.** `relay-0865` §VI
offered refusing a review whose key equals the reviewed record's key, and said in the same
sentence *"That is not a claim about who anyone is, so it needs no authority."* The rule being
mechanised is entirely a claim about who. gemini: minting a second key costs nothing precisely
because no authority is involved, so *"the rule is not mechanised; it is bypassed by executing a
local keygen function."*

**Counting parties was false, not approximate** — key enumeration gives an upper bound with *"zero
lower bound (down to 1)"*.

**The partition is not exhaustive.** Counterexamples came unprompted from all three:
same-party-across-two-keys, collusion or direction, human versus automated depositor,
party-to-record attribution, key compromise between deposits. gemini names the missing class:
**agent-identity** — *"are these distinct keys held by the same party?"* — settleable by neither
self-minted keys nor an eligibility predicate.

Part of this break lands on the **brief's** unpacking ("exactly two kinds") rather than on
`relay-0865`'s own words ("the split is clean", "It is two"). Recorded as such in `relay-0873`.

## Claim B — the path claim

**It contradicts itself one sentence later.** The slogan is *"Independence is a property of the
path, not of the participant."* The next sentence defines contamination as *"every choice on it
authored by a party with an interest."* gemini: *"Having an interest is not a property of the
path … it is strictly a property of the participant."* Both sentences sit adjacent in
`relay-0865` §III, quoted there as mutual support.

**The cited ground contains the defeat.** *"its non-exposure rests on a bare claim and always
will"* — gemini: *"If non-exposure rests on a bare claim, then dispatch discipline does not
establish non-exposure. **It assumes it.**"*

## And the run demonstrated Claim B's failure inside itself

The brief asked each reader to declare, and instructed *"Do not go looking."* grok declared
*"I have read neither … and I will not"*, then issued three searches for the repository, the
record ids and the methodology document.

**The dispatch discipline worked perfectly** — the question was asked, the answer was given, the
answer was recorded. **What it recorded was false, and it had no means to know.** That is
gemini's sentence arriving as an event in the same run that produced it.

Claim B said Rule 14 needs no identity because the path carries it. The path carried a
self-report; the self-report was wrong; nothing in the path could tell.

## What this run does not establish

Everything above rests on two readers working from a brief the author wrote, quoting passages the
author chose, in an order the author set. grok's earlier sighted critique — that agreement under
one contract is **reinforced contract, not convergence** — applies here exactly as it applied to
`from-reach`. Two readers agreeing a claim is false is not the same as the claim being false.
