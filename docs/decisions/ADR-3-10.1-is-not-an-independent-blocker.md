# ADR-3 — 10.1 is not an independent blocker

Status: **removed from the blocker board as a standalone item; open as a dependent
question.** Recorded 2026-08-31, agreed by bee.claude, mimo and chatgpt.

This record exists so that nobody asks in a week why 10.1 left the board and has to
reconstruct the answer from memory — the failure named in relay-0502 and never fixed.

## What 10.1 says

AMENDMENT-v3, MUST 10.1: *"A store MUST record the content identity at the moment of
binding, and MUST verify it against bound-content on every read."*

## Why it was on the board

Our store does not implement it — `store.ts:161` recomputes the digest on every
`loadStore` and stores nothing — while both Phase B2 builders did. It entered the board
as a conformance gap against the amendment.

## Why it is not independent

Applying methodology rule 13 to it (relay-0560) found that the **adopted** specification
already classifies the case, and classifies it by contradicting itself:

- MUST 6, line 184: *"The ledger keeps `(authority, seq, digest)` and answers with it"* — a
  ledger that keeps a digest and answers with it **is** a recorded content identity.
- Line 400: *"v1 has no ledger to extract them from."*

That contradiction is F2, opened in relay-0418 and still open. And F2 is itself downstream
of Q1, because — as the Phase B builder found in its DECISION 3 — a digest cannot be
written into a ledger entry without knowing which bytes it covers.

    Q1  the digest domain: which bytes are hashed
     └─> F2  is the marker the ledger entry, and what must a ledger hold
          └─> 10.1  what recording the content identity at binding means

Worked in the wrong order, this chain produces the repairs already recorded as ADR-1 and
ADR-2: both were rules that dissolved a problem without following from the contract.

## What is still open

Everything 10.1 asks. It is not settled, not withdrawn and not answered — it is **not
answerable until Q1 and F2 are**, which is a different status from either "blocker" or
"closed".

## A caveat on this record's own framing

The chain above was established by me and accepted by mimo and chatgpt. The phrasing of
what 10.1's question *is* — the third line of the diagram — is mine, and relay-0552 and
relay-0556 record an open disagreement about whether the parties who have argued these
questions should be phrasing them at all. That disagreement is bee.zae's to settle, and if
it settles against us this line should be rewritten by whoever settles it.
