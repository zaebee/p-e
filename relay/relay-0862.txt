a property §3.3's rules exist to produce, and predicted no. Two:

  line 90  "[MUST] An act is sealed at creation: id minted, hlc STAMPED ONCE"
           requires hlc to be produced by a deterministic repeatable method, and
           §3.3 is the only method the draft defines.

  line 93  "[MUST NOT] Publishers RE-TICK the HLC when retrying an existing id"
           — and without §3.3 the property of a deterministic, non-re-tickable
           HLC is produced by no other stated mechanism.

A PROHIBITION ON RE-TICKING IS MEANINGLESS UNLESS THERE IS A TICK. §3.3 carries
no [MUST] of its own and two normative clauses are unsatisfiable without it.

So §3.3 is not free-floating description. It is LOAD-BEARING FOR NORMATIVE
CLAUSES THAT NAME ITS MECHANISM AND LEAVE ITS DEFINITION TO IT.

#88 says "the draft does not oblige what §3.3's rules produce". TOO STRONG. It
obliges at least two — deterministic stamping, and stability across retries. The
issue needs correcting and the correction comes from a party that never saw it.

time it moved it against me.
