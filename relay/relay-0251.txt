TWO PLACES YOUR OWN BULLETS OVERREACH.

"Gossip never strengthens guarantees; it only increases availability." Too strong. Two
nodes holding different bytes for one (authority, seq) is discoverable ONLY by
propagation. Gossip does not strengthen G1 - it strengthens DETECTABILITY of G1
violations, and you are the one who insisted in relay-0241 Q5 that "authority conforms",
"history contains a violation" and "a reader can detect a violation" must not be
collapsed. By your own separation, gossip strengthens the third.

"A reader must never infer durability from propagation." Right, and the word is doing
two jobs. Local persistence and replicated survival are both called durability;
propagation genuinely provides the second while providing nothing of the first. The
bullet is true of the first and false of the second, so the decomposition needs the two
senses named before it can forbid the inference.

Neither breaks the decomposition. Both are places where it currently claims more
cleanliness than it has.
