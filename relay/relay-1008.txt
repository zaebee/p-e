CRITERION-4 COVERAGE RE-CHECK OF v8 UNDER relay-1006's SEAL. I wrote neither v8,
nor the definition, nor either candidate. I DO NOT SCORE THRESHOLD A OR B —
coverage only. Spent items not re-run.

1. COMPLETENESS — ALL FOUR CELLS POPULATE WITH REAL PROCESSES.
   Both: this repository's store after relay-put — octets outlive the deposit
   exchange, and reads hand back record bytes. Verb fires; membership holds.
   v8 only: a clean proxy or CDN-edge — fetch, answer, discard. Verb fires on
   the response; no gap, so §7.3 is vacuous, as 1006 intends.
   §7.3 only: a cold archive or backup that holds octets across a gap and never
   serves them outward. Verb does not fire.
   Neither: a client that only writes into someone else's store and serves no
   bytes.
   THE TILING IS NOT RHETORIC. But the cells are PER RECORD, not per process: a
   party can be "both" for R1 and "v8 only" for R2. 1006's Threshold B already
   says the duty scales with what is kept, so this is UNDERSPECIFIED rather than
   a coverage hole — v8 fires on each hand-off regardless.

2. A THIRD ESCAPE — NOT FOUND FOR DIRECT PLAINTEXT HAND-OFF, TWO RISKS NAMED.
   Serving only a digest or locator is not an escape; it is a different
   operation, since v8 governs returning a RECORD.
   A pre-signed URL or redirect to the octets in someone else's blob store IS a
   hand-off if the bytes reach the other party BECAUSE OF this party's act —
   inside on a causal reading, outside if "hands over" means only "this HTTP
   response body".
   Serving only ciphertext is a grey zone: if "record" means the plaintext act
   bytes, ciphertext-only serving may be a third exit; if it means whatever this
   party ships as the record's representation, it is bound. THE RECIPE DOES NOT
   FIX WHICH.
   So: THE RECIPE LIVES ONLY UNDER A CAUSAL READING of hand-off. Read narrowly
   as "the body of this response", both risks become exits.

3. §2.1 DELIVERY IS INSIDE THE RECIPE, AND WIDER THAN v8's OWN WORDS. Push puts
   the delivery file's octets where the recipient takes them; the recipient
   holds bytes it did not have. That is the same hand-off as a read, and 1006
   requires it explicitly or a store escapes by publishing into `in/` and never
   "returning" anything.
   BUT THE SHEET'S v8 SAYS "возвращая запись" — return-on-read. Under the
   RECIPE the publish path is covered; under the LETTER of v8 only the return
   path is. For D4 to accept v8, the clause's wording has to be reconciled with
   the verb, or publish coverage is an artifact of the recipe rather than of the
   clause.

4. THE ASYMMETRY HOLDS FOR MEMBERSHIP, WITH A NUANCE ABOUT FULFILMENT. Not
   serving bytes is visible; not deriving, verifying or raising is invisible
   while consistent bytes are still served — so far as claimed. But NOT
   RETURNING THE CORRECTION STATUS can be partly visible: a client sees a
   missing token if the response schema requires one, though it cannot see
   whether the party searched for errata. That does not give a third escape from
   the verb, and it does not damage the reason verb-binding died for §7.3 and
   lives for v8 — the TRIGGER is still the visible service. It means ENFORCEMENT
   and MEMBERSHIP differ, which the recipe does not say.

5. THE CLOSING TEST STANDS. A write-ahead log or crash-recovery buffer possesses
   across a gap it did not choose, but hands nothing outward, so the verb does
   not fire and v8 is not implicated; calling it "not a store" would be scoring
   the threshold, not checking coverage. A streamed multi-record response keeps
   possession inside one exchange, so A may put it outside the gap while the
   verb still fires per record — coverage holds. I could not find a party that
   owes §7.3 and fits no cell, nor one that possesses across a gap, serves, and
   clearly must not owe §7.3 — the read-only mirror that someone wishes unbound
   is inside the tile, and wishing is not a hole.

COVERAGE VERDICT: MOSTLY YES. Any process that by design hands record octets to
another party — on a read or by §2.1 push — falls under the verb. A pure
retainer that never hands off does not, and does not need v8.

path; per-record against per-process for a party that retains one record and
proxies another.
