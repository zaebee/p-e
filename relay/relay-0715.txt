SIX STATES TESTED BLIND. TWO READERS. NEITHER RETURNED SIX.

SETUP. docs/experiments/citation-set. SYSTEM.md describes an append-only log
with predecessor references in two fields, either omittable, and readers holding
arbitrary subsets. No state names, no counts, no continuity.ts, no issue 5.
Both readers verified SYSTEM.md and CONTRACT.md against PIN.txt.
Run A: subagent, nine outcomes. Run C: mistral, eight.
Two runs were void before these: operator material reached the reader both
times, first because I put it in the bundle, then because it was handed over as
a file. Logged in OPERATOR.md. The fault was mine in both.

WHAT BOTH FOUND WITHOUT SEEING OUR CODE.
  - Not holding the predecessor is NOT an error. A: "the normal condition of a
    distributed log, not evidence about the log." C: "No error by the author.
    The reader's subset simply lacks the predecessor."
  - A predecessor named without a digest is not an error either.
  - A record referring to nothing is its own outcome.

That is UNCHECKABLE's load-bearing claim and LABEL_ONLY's, corroborated twice
by readers who could not have read them. This is the half I imported and could
not test from inside. It survives.

WHAT BOTH FOUND THAT WE DO NOT HAVE. Both split on whether the reader holds
matching material, in two places we do not:
  - LABEL_ONLY splits by held / not held.
  - UNANCHORED splits by whether any held record hashes to the declared digest.
Two readers, two methods, same two extra boundaries. Our six is not complete.

WHERE THE READERS DISAGREE WITH EACH OTHER, WHICH IS ALSO A RESULT.
  - DIVERGES. A returns one outcome and refuses to attribute it: "definite error
    with undetermined attribution -- the candidates are the author, whoever
    produced the reader's copy of the predecessor, and whoever produced the
    reader's copy of this record." C returns three outcomes and attributes every
    one to the author.
  - Not-held cases. A splits them by field shape and revised its method mid-run
    to do so. C merges them all and explicitly rejects the split: "the
    actionable conclusion is the same."
  - A has SELF_REFERENCE. C does not.

NOT ADOPTING EITHER. Our continuity.ts says DIVERGES is "a defect in the
record". A says that is a claim the reader cannot support. C agrees with us.
n=2 and they split, so the attribution question is open, not decided against
us. Acting on A alone would repeat exactly what relay-0710 corrected me for.

WHAT IS ESTABLISHED. The partition's core distinction is not an artefact of
this store -- two readers reached it from the system description alone. Its
boundary count is not determined by that description -- two readers reached
different counts, each with a stated method and a defended reason.

NOT REPAIRING. continuity.ts unchanged. The spec in PR 12 still says "author
defect" at DIVERGES and that is now a known open question, not a settled one.
