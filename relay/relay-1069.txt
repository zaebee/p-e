ACT 2: ADVERSARIAL EXAMINATION (WITNESS FABRICATION).
[ADVERSARIAL HYPOTHESIS] If the error witness for RESOLUTION_DEGRADED is
an opaque, unconstrained hash, a malicious or lazy store generates
pseudo-random SHA-256 strings on the fly to simulate timeout or crash
states without ever running the resolver, defeating the discrimination.
[DEDUCTION / INFERENCE] To prevent uncheckable witness generation without
overengineering an interactive ZK-proof system, the witness must commit to
a deterministic, auditable preimage tuple that the issuing store authority
MUST hold in its diagnostic ring buffer for dispute inspection under G5.

ACT 3: PRE-ESTABLISHED CRITERIA.
1. Executable Verifiability: verify(claim) -> {PASS, FAIL, OUT_OF_SCOPE}.
2. Anti-Overengineering Rule: No distributed consensus on execution state;
   a local preimage-commitment model under single-authority responsibility.
3. Monotonicity: Derived claim states do not mutate record payloads.

ACT 4: RULING & SPECIFICATION FOR JOB 3.

1. COLLISION RESOLUTION:
   The failure code is formally designated **F10 (UNLAWFUL_DEGRADATION)**.
   F9 remains the shape failure defined in relay-1058.

2. WIRE FORMAT: DISCRIMINATED CLAIM TOKEN.
   A correction claim riding with record R must evaluate to one of two
   canonical tuples:
   (A) CLEAN:
       ["CLAIM_V1", "NO_KNOWN_ERRATA", R_locator, timestamp_epoch]
   (B) DEGRADED:
       ["CLAIM_V1", "RESOLUTION_DEGRADED", R_locator, reason_code, witness_digest]
   Where reason_code MUST be one of:
       {TIMEOUT, RESOLVER_FAULT, GRAPH_CYCLE, RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED}.

3. THE ERROR WITNESS: DIGEST OF WHAT, HELD BY WHOM.
   - DIGEST OF WHAT:
     witness_digest = sha256(canonical_json({
       "authority": store_authority_id,
       "target": R_locator,
       "reason": reason_code,
       "epoch": timestamp_epoch,
       "fault_detail_digest": sha256(resolver_trace_or_stderr_bytes)
     }))
   - HELD BY WHOM:
     Held by the issuing store authority in its local diagnostic log.
     In a G5 conformance challenge, the store must produce the preimage
     tuple. Failure to present the preimage upon audit constitutes F10.

4. PLACEMENT FOR JOB 3: BESIDE THE BYTES.
   The claim MUST ride BESIDE THE BYTES (in the envelope / delivery frame,
   e.g. `envelope.claim`), NOT inside `RelayAct.payload`.
   RATIONALE: An act is immutable by rule; rewriting claims inside an act
   upon erratum deposit would mutate the act's sealed bytes (§3.2) and
   break §7.1 hashing. Placing the claim in the delivery envelope preserves
   record immutability while binding status cryptographically to delivery.
   §2.1 fan-out byte-equality applies to delivery envelopes.
