I THINK YOU ARE RIGHT. And here is what I checked and what would make it wrong.

THE SENTENCE STRUCTURE. Line 204: "An authority MUST declare the seq from which
it claims G1, and MUST NOT claim G1 below it." The "from which it claims G1" is
a defining clause — it specifies which authority the obligation attaches to. An
authority that claims no G1 has no seq to declare. The obligation is: if you
claim G1, declare where you start.

THE EXPLANATORY TEXT. Line 206: "Without this the contract has no vocabulary for
an authority with a history, and every authority acquires one the moment it
starts." This says why the clause exists, not that it applies universally.
"Every authority acquires one" refers to acquiring a history, not to declaring a
floor.

THE SUPPORTING TABLE. Lines 425-428: "legacy relay | makes no G1 claim" and
"MUST 2 exists for the third row [a future authority]". The spec consistently
treats G1 as something authorities may claim, and MUST 2 is the mechanism for
those that do.

WHAT WOULD MAKE ME WRONG. A sentence somewhere that says "all authorities must
declare their seq floor" regardless of G1 claim. I searched for "every authority
MUST", "all authority MUST", "authority MUST declare" without the G1 qualifier.
No such sentence exists. The closest is line 206 which says "every authority
acquires one [a history]" — but that is descriptive, not normative.

Also line 426: "a future authority | may declare a floor" — "may", not "must".
Even a future authority is permissive.

I CANNOT FIND A CONSEQUENCE OF VACUITY THAT MAKES IT WRONG. If the store claims
no G1, it has no seq floor. It cannot participate in cross-authority citation
that requires a seq floor. But that is a consequence of claiming no G1, not a
contradiction of the reading.

THE COMPETING READING. The unconditional one — every authority must declare a
seq, full stop — would require finding a sentence that says so. I cannot find
one. The clause says "from which it claims G1" and I cannot read past those
words to make the obligation unconditional.

So: I think you are right. The reading is: the obligation binds an authority
that claims G1. The store claims no G1. The store cannot have a floor. The real
gap was that nothing in src/relay/ said so.
