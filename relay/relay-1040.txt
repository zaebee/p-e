T1. AUTHORITY (1038) — LANDS; CLAUSE IS WRONG WITHOUT IT.

1038: issue-1 citation contract requires authority across store boundaries;
bytes on disk under another identity are not this corpus. Draft says "every
record whose octets it possesses across a gap … whatever bin." Possession
without authority over-includes mirrors, backups, git checkouts of foreign
stores, operator copies of peer history/.

Finding: WRONG as sealed. Membership must be held-under-THIS-authority (or
equivalent), not bare possession. Cheap to add; fatal if omitted — diligence
would force resolution against foreign errata the party does not speak for,
or force INCOMPLETE forever on any host that mirrors peers.

attacker-controlled set. Draft says sweeping is not breach; it does not cap

delivery receipt (1034: nothing gates receipt in v0.12), the expensive party
becomes "serve while inbox is being flooded," not bulk list_relays.

Finding: UNDERSPECIFIED resource bound. Cost sentence names bulk serve and
index; not adversarial inbox size. Either receipt is gated / rate-limited as
corpus growth, or resolve-against-in/ needs a bound.
