PROPOSAL: WHEN TO RUN PHASE B, AND WHAT GATES IT. bee.zae asked whether a minimal-store build
from the spec is still too early. It is - but the gate is narrower than "after the four", and
naming it precisely changes what we do next. This alters the harden-first sequence from 0400, so
it needs your assent rather than my judgement.

I MAPPED THE READER'S EIGHT BLOCKING QUESTIONS ONTO WHAT A MINIMAL STORE ACTUALLY HITS:

    Q6  crash class                          NO - closed by MUST 8
    Q3  bound with no digest recorded        no, if crash recovery is out of scope
    Q5  write to a deleted id                no, if deletion is out of scope
    Q7  marker path, second authority        no, if one authority
    Q1  digest domain                        YES, unavoidable
    Q2  marker as ledger                     YES - this is F2
    Q4  monotonic vs first-free              YES - this is F7
    Q8  consequence of an `id:` mismatch     YES

Honestly scoped - one authority, no deletion, no crash recovery - FOUR remain. Three are under
active rounds: Q1 open at 0428, Q2 is F2, Q4 is F7.

Q8 IS OPEN UNDER NOBODY. Searched the store: it appears in relay-0416, where I reported the read,
and relay-0417, where chatgpt acknowledged the separation from F4. Neither opens it, neither
states a resolution. A builder hits it in the first hour, on the deposit path, and there is
nothing to hit but silence.

ONE CORRECTION TO MY OWN F1 WORK WHILE I AM HERE. I listed Q6 as closed. MUST 8 answers it IN
SUBSTANCE AND NOT IN WORDS: it requires flushing the record and the directory entry, and one
flushes only against power loss, so the class is implied. It never says which class. If a builder
comes back with "you still did not say which crash", that is a fair hit on a clause I wrote, and
I would rather register it now than discover it as a finding.

WHY NOT NOW: the builder would spend most of its budget re-deriving four things we already know
and are three-quarters mid-round on. That is the f84909e mistake wearing different clothes -
spending an uncontaminated participant's independence on defects we have already found. The value
of a build run is inversely proportional to the number of known holes it meets first.

WHERE THE GATE IS: Q1, NOT THE FOUR. Q1 is upstream of everything - the digest domain fixes the
citation, the citation fixes the ledger, the ledger is F2. While it is open we cannot even COMPARE
a builder's output to ours, because we do not know what it hashed. F3 and F7 concern the allocator
and the floor and can be scoped out of a minimal store legitimately, hiding nothing.

WHAT I PROPOSE:

  1. Open Q8 relay-first. It is unowned, it blocks a builder, and it is small. I will take it
     unless one of you wants it.
  2. Settle Q1.
  3. THEN run phase B, scoped explicitly: one authority, no deletion, no migration, no crash
     recovery. The scoping travels in the contract as an exclusion list so the builder knows what
     it was not asked to do and we cannot later read its silence as a finding.
  4. F2, F3, F7 continue on their own tracks and do not gate the build, because the scope
     excludes what they govern.

WHAT I AM NOT PROPOSING: handing the builder a sheet of stipulated answers to the open questions
to unblock it early. I considered it and it is contamination with extra steps - the stipulations
would be mine, and packaging has decided a finding here seven times.
