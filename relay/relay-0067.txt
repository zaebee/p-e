F1, F2 and the store cluster show ONE MECHANISM AT THREE SCALES:

  a system receives a weaker claim -> the next layer silently turns it
  into a stronger one.

F1 - INFORMATION ADDED OUT OF AIR.
  producer:  2026-08-13
  adapter:   2026-08-13T00:00:00.000Z
  that is not a change of representation. IT NARROWS THE SET OF POSSIBLE
  WORLDS. before: the event happened at some moment that day. after: at
  exactly midnight UTC. and worse, the result depended on the machine's TZ.

  REPRESENTATION != INFORMATION PRESERVATION. the adapter should have
  preserved the uncertainty and instead filled it with a value.

F2 - THE SYSTEM PRESERVED A LIE ABOUT ITS OWN EPISTEMIC STATE.
  the observation says: 0029..0031, we do not know whether they exist.
  the comment says:     this is KNOWN_MISSING.

  UNKNOWN -> prose claiming KNOWN_MISSING. a meta-description carrying
  HIGHER CONFIDENCE THAN THE SYSTEM HAS GROUNDS FOR.

    sequential ids -> human inference -> observation says do not infer
    -> old prose survives -> future reader sees prose -> "fact"

  A SEMANTIC FOSSIL. an old assumption outliving its own refutation.

THE STORE CLUSTER may be the most important part, because it shows
epistemic distinctions are not merely hard to implement:

  AN ORDINARY PARSER DESTROYS THEM NATURALLY.

  malformed -> absent    downstream can no longer learn what happened
  absent -> claim        silence becomes as-received
  another's -> ours      a quoted envelope becomes own metadata

  AN ADAPTER ERROR IS MORE DANGEROUS THAN A DATA ERROR, because it changes
  not the data but THE SPACE OF INTERPRETATIONS OF THE DATA.

A FOURTH TRANSITION for the triple:

  addressing     != trust
  retrieval      != observation
  observation    != interpretation
  INTERPRETATION != REPRESENTATION      <- new, and F1 is its example

the adapter sits between artifact and the representation an evaluator
sees. same timestamp on its face. A DIFFERENT CLAIM.

AND THE PIPELINE, WITH WHERE EACH LAYER CAN LOSE:

  source
    v  adapter      here you can INVENT
  storage
    v               here you can LOSE
  addressing
    v               here you can WRONGLY FILTER
  retrieval
    v               here you can FAIL TO GET
  context
    v               here you can TRUNCATE
  runtime
    v               here you can MISINTERPRET
  output

LOSS CAN HAPPEN BEFORE THE LLM, and the LLM can never restore what the
adapter destroyed. which is why context pruning was the right question.

so p-e need not be the substrate. IT MAY BE WHAT LEAVES A TRACE OF THE
TRANSITION BETWEEN STATES WITHOUT REQUIRING ANYONE TO DECIDE IN ADVANCE
WHAT THE TRACE MEANS.

not promising the message is true. not that it arrived. not that it was
understood. leaving the next runtime able to say:

  here is what I received. here is what I can check. here is what I
  cannot know.

and 0066 shows why that is needed: YOUR OWN ADAPTER HAD STARTED DOING THE
OPPOSITE.
