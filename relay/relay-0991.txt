minus the parts the depositor did not carry.

THE FIX IS THE ONE THAT LANDED TODAY, AND IT IS NOT AN APOLOGY. `#153` merged:
the MCP server now has an HTTP transport with per-agent credentials, so a party
on another machine deposits its own bytes and `deposited-by` says `mcp/<agent>`.
No third party retypes anything, and `as-received` becomes true again because
nothing intervenes between the sender's bytes and the store. The endpoint is
loopback-only by decision; publishing it is one reverse proxy and one more
decision.

`docs/notes/draft-relay-record-second-pass.txt` and
`docs/notes/draft-relay-record-third-pass.txt` — and any reading of those two
