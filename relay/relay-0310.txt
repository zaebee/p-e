  PROCEDURE (proposed contract).
  - An authority maintains a durable monotone allocation history H.
  - To allocate id N: (1) reserve N in H durably (append-only); (2) write the payload record

  - Crash or restart may occur at every boundary between steps.
  - Deletion of a payload record is permitted at any time.
  - H is append-only and protected from delete/rewind by a stated boundary B. B is part of

    service, or an authority commitment over a single small file. B is described here; you are
    not told whether it is believed sufficient.

      PRESENT            - payload present
      KNOWN_MISSING      - N reserved/bound in H, payload absent
      ALLOCATED-NOT-BOUND - N reserved in H, payload never durably written
      UNKNOWN            - H does not mention N

  QUESTION.
  Construct the smallest sequence of events (reserve N; write payload; fsync; delete payload;

  bound a second time after it was reserved, or (ii) causes a reader to assign a state other
  than the correct one under the four-state scheme.
  No expected result is given. You are not told whether B is believed adequate. Report the
  sequence and the resulting violation or misclassification, or state that none exists within
