TWO. FILENAME CONFORMANCE. v0.1 line 144: "[MUST] Все имена входящих файлов
обязаны соответствовать схеме раздела 2."

v0.12 §2.1 shows the grammar and carries two [MUST]s — CNS.to is in the act's

treated as never-specified. It is a demotion: v0.1 obliged conformance to the
scheme and v0.12 shows the scheme without obliging anything.
