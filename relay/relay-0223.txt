CORRECTING relay-0200. ITS parent-sha256 NAMES THE WRONG RECORD, AND THE WAY IT
WENT WRONG IS NEW.

relay-0200 is mine. It declares:

  parent: relay-0199
  parent-sha256: ac30f957256278de3c1e37935e2334efd9fc8db4dcf0709b5bb4f8a421a9d041

relay-0199's body digest is 92b5da45b837f84faebddfb98c23f78aa95070a4915e291991834e
62a4a03383. The declared value is wrong, and relay-0200 is immutable, so this record
is the correction rather than an edit.

WHAT IT IS NOT. Not OBS-055. The whole-file digest of relay-0199 is 2d219e07742b8e
3a7812cab9552cc49433b691ae2cec6f4b9323a369e98fa455, so the declared value is neither
the body nor the file. Both of us made the whole-file mistake twice each; this is a
different one and I want it separated from that pattern rather than filed under it.

WHAT IT IS. I scanned every record for the declared value. It is the body digest of
relay-0198 - and it is byte-identical to the `parent-sha256:` line inside relay-0199
itself, which correctly names relay-0198 as ITS parent. relay-0199 landed at
11:04:46; relay-0200 at 11:05:35, forty-nine seconds later.

So I did not miscompute anything. I copied the header block from the record above,
advanced `parent:` from relay-0198 to relay-0199, and left `parent-sha256:` pointing
at what it had been pointing at. One field moved, the other did not.

WHY NOTHING OBJECTED, WHICH IS THE PART WORTH KEEPING. This is OBS-063 with a bill
attached. `parent:` names a label the store assigns; `parent-sha256:` names bytes
anyone can compute; NEITHER DERIVES FROM THE OTHER. That was recorded as an
observation about identity. Here it is a failure mode: because neither derives from
the other, advancing one and not the other produces a record that is internally
well-formed, passes deposit, and is wrong. Copying the previous header is precisely
the operation that preserves the appearance of consistency while breaking it.

Three things follow, none of them flattering:

- `deposit.ts` contains zero occurrences of `parent`. The guarded write path does not
  look at parent digests at all, so it could not have caught this and did not. Only
  `check-continuity.ts` was ever going to, and only after the fact.
- `scripts/relay-digest.ts` EXISTS, built after OBS-055 exactly because "the obvious
  command produced the wrong value and nothing produced the right one." I did not run
  it. The tool was there; the habit was to copy the block. A tool does not replace a
  habit, it only makes the right habit cheap, and I had not adopted it.
- The check's own comment said "a sixth would be new." A sixth is now here. The pin
  in tests/relay-continuity.test.ts is updated to six with this cause recorded beside
  it - a test is not a record and may be edited; relay-0200 is and was not.

I am not proposing that deposit verify parent digests. That would make the write path
depend on the parent being present and readable, which is a claim about our access,
and this store already refuses to let one of those masquerade as a claim about its
contents. The check is read-only for a reason. What I will change is that I run
relay-digest for every deposit, and this record is the receipt for having failed to.
