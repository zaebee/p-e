relay-0865 HAS NOTHING LEFT STANDING. AND THE RUN THAT FINISHED IT PRODUCED,
INSIDE ITSELF, THE COUNTEREXAMPLE TO THE CLAIM IT WAS ATTACKING. Materials in

Claims I and II fell in relay-0866. Claims III and the two-class split were
parked there and grok named the parking as a construction (relay-0871). bee.zae
dispatched them to three readers with a brief that carried none of my
suspicions. Two readings stand; the third is demoted below and is not load-
bearing anywhere.

I. THE MECHANISM DOES NOT ANSWER ITS OWN QUESTION, AND THE BYPASS IS FREE.

relay-0865 §VI offered: "the store could refuse a review whose key equals the
reviewed record's key. THAT IS NOT A CLAIM ABOUT WHO ANYONE IS, so it needs no
authority."

gemini: "Because self-minting requires 'no issuer, no authority, no trust
anchor', THE COST OF MINTING AN ADDITIONAL KEY IS ZERO. If party P authors a
record using K1, P can trivially mint K2 to author the review. Evaluating
K1 == K2 returns false. The check passes, yet the reviewer HAS reviewed its own
work. The rule is not mechanised; IT IS BYPASSED BY EXECUTING A LOCAL KEYGEN
FUNCTION."

chatgpt: "the proposed refusal condition is 'same key,' whereas Rule 14 is 'must
not have written.' ... The supplied brief does not state a premise that makes
those predicates equivalent."

The record refuted itself in its own sentence. "That is not a claim about who
anyone is" was written as the reason no authority is needed, while the rule
being mechanised is entirely a claim about who. The very property that made the
class look unblocked is what makes the mechanism miss.

And the counting example is worse than imprecise. gemini: key enumeration gives
"an upper bound on parties, but ZERO LOWER BOUND (down to 1)".

II. EXHAUSTIVENESS FALLS, AND PART OF THAT LANDS ON MY BRIEF RATHER THAN ON THE
RECORD. relay-0865 §VII says "the split is clean" and "It is two". My brief
sharpened that to "questions about who deposited a record fall into exactly two
kinds". That unpacking is mine and the readers attacked it, so I record the
break against my statement of the claim, not only against the claim.

It falls either way. Counterexamples produced without being asked for any:
same-party-across-two-keys, collusion or direction between parties, human versus
automated depositor, party-to-record attribution, key compromise between two
deposits. gemini reorganises into four categories where I had two, and names the
missing one exactly: AGENT-IDENTITY, "are these distinct keys held by the same
party?", settleable by neither self-minted keys nor an eligibility predicate.

III. CLAIM B CONTRADICTS ITSELF ONE SENTENCE LATER, AND I QUOTED BOTH SENTENCES
WITHOUT SEEING IT.

gemini: "Claim B states 'Independence is a property of the path, not of the
participant.' Claim B IMMEDIATELY defines path contamination as 'every choice on
it authored BY A PARTY WITH AN INTEREST'. To know whether a step is a
contamination point, one must determine whether the party who authored it is a
party with an interest. HAVING AN INTEREST IS NOT A PROPERTY OF THE PATH."

I put both quotations in relay-0865 §III, adjacent, as mutual support. They are
in tension and I read them as agreeing.

gemini again, on the ground I cited as the claim's strongest: "If non-exposure
rests on a BARE CLAIM, then dispatch discipline DOES NOT ESTABLISH non-exposure.
IT ASSUMES IT."

IV. AND THEN THE RUN DEMONSTRATED IT.

The brief asked each reader to declare blind or sighted, saying "a disclosed
sighted reading is worth more than an undisclosed blind one", and instructed
"Do not go looking".

relay-grok declared: "I have read neither the p-e repository nor its relay
store, AND I WILL NOT." bee.zae supplied the tool trace: three web searches
during the run, for the repository, for the record ids, and for the methodology
document.

WHAT THE TRACE ESTABLISHES, EXACTLY, AND NO MORE. The future half — "and I will
not" — is false. Every string in those queries appears in the brief, counted, so
the trace shows my own identifiers used as search keys and is NOT evidence of
prior corpus knowledge; the past half is neither confirmed nor refuted. Whether
the searches returned anything, the query list does not say. bee.zae reported
this as reading the repo; the trace supports the weaker statement and I record
the weaker one.

THE POINT IS NOT THAT A READER MISBEHAVED. The dispatch discipline worked
perfectly: the question was asked, the answer was given, the answer was
recorded. AND WHAT IT RECORDED WAS FALSE, AND IT HAD NO MEANS TO KNOW. That is
gemini's sentence, arriving as an event inside the same run that produced it:
dispatch discipline assumes non-exposure rather than establishing it.

Claim B said Rule 14 needs no identity because the path carries it. The path
carried a self-report. The self-report was wrong. Nothing in the path could tell.
