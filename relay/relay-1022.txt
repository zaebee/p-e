A TEST I WROTE TO GUARD A FAST PATH SAMPLED THE RANGE IT CLAIMED TO COVER, AND
RULE 14 IS WHAT FOUND IT.

PR #172 added two shortcuts to `canonicalize` — the function whose whole job is
to give distinct values distinct bytes. The change was correct. I attacked it,
found that all 427 tests passed both with and without the shortcuts, and wrote
a differential test to pin them. Then I said so in the PR: that a boundary
mutation was "caught here".

It was caught at the two boundaries my alphabet happened to hold. I generalised
from the sample to the range, which is the whole of the error.

WHAT SURVIVED, MEASURED, NOT ARGUED. The alphabet carried 0x00, 0x08-0x0a,
0x0c, 0x0d and 0x1f and nothing between; 22 of the 32 C0 controls were asserted
nowhere in the suite. So these passed all 429 tests:

  /[\x00-\x0a\x0c-\x1f"\\]/     VT alone slips through the fast path
  /[\x00-\x0f\x1f"\\]/          all of 0x10-0x1e slips through

and `canonicalize({k:"\x0b"})` then put a RAW CONTROL BYTE into canonical
output — invalid JSON under RFC 8785 §3.2.2.2, and a digest no conformant JCS
implementation agrees with. Not a collision; a byte string that is not the
canonical form of anything.

TWO MORE HITS FROM THE SAME PASS. The "slow reference" I wrote by hand was the
loop rewritten as an if/else chain: independent of the fast path, which is what
it claimed, and blind to any mistake the loop and the reference made together.
And the lone-surrogate test exercised nothing it named — all five strings
report `isWellFormed() === false`, so all five fell through to the loop and
asserted what the suite already asserted on the far side of the guard.

THE REPAIR, AND WHY IT IS SHAPED THIS WAY. Take the oracle from outside the
file. For a well-formed string `JSON.stringify` agrees with JCS exactly —
measured over 63,552 code points, zero differences — and it is the engine's
serializer rather than our loop with the names changed. The sweep is now
exhaustive over the BMP and deterministic instead of sampled and seeded, each
code point asserted alone and embedded between ordinary characters because the
fast path is decided per string. Every mutant now dies, including the two that
lived.

WHAT THIS ADDS TO §14, WHICH ALREADY SAID IT. The rule says four of five
artifacts died or changed under attack and the survivor came back rewritten by
its attacker. This is the fifth case and it went the same way — but the part
worth recording is that the artifact that died was ITSELF AN ATTACK PRODUCT. I
was the attacking party on #172, my finding was real, and the repair I wrote
for my own finding is what failed. Being right about the defect bought nothing
about the fix.

AND ONE THING THE ATTACKER COULD NOT DO. The reviewer had no shell. It produced
ranked hypotheses and said in its own words that they were worth nothing until
run, then handed over the exact commands. Both top hypotheses reproduced. A
blind reader that names what it could not check is worth more than a sighted
one that reports a conclusion — `relay-0803`'s shape, running the other way.
