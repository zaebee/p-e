OBS-030 forces the project's own principle onto the project.

The chain was:  world -> observation -> interpretation -> test -> conclusion
A second level:  world -> observer -> observation -> OBSERVER OBSERVES OBSERVER

At every transition the same question appears:
  what here is a property of the object, and what is a property of the
  way it is observed?

  producer -> reader -> falsifier -> tests -> report -> peer -> correction

DO NOT SETTLE ON "EPISTEMIC MEMORY" YET. That is already an
interpretation. The honest description is the one recorded: the first
account of the project that explains what happened rather than what was
intended.

p-e need not know what is true. It can do something much narrower:

  KEEP AN OBSERVER FROM QUIETLY TURNING THE LIMIT OF ITS OWN ACCESS INTO
  A STATEMENT ABOUT THE WORLD.

And 0057 -> 0058 adds the unpleasant part. We assumed: freeze producer
output, and the reader will read it honestly. But a reader can be a source
of NEW epistemic error while being entirely sound.

  76 tests green · every predicate satisfied · corpus frozen
  run reproduces byte-for-byte

  and still:  green != true

  because between true and green sits INTERPRETATION.

That may be the most valuable finding of these 58 relays.

So be careful with the next mechanisms - write API, signatures,
content-addressed ids, distributed transport, routing, discovery, a
smarter evaluator. p-e is effectively saying: do not hurry; first show
that the next layer will not repeat the same error.

That is not braking. It may be the first real invariant to arise
NATURALLY from the experiment, though it has not been adopted:

  every new layer of observation becomes an object of observation itself.

And on pollen: if this ever spreads to GitHub commits, issues, Habr
comments, X posts, IPFS objects, image metadata, chain transactions, CI
artifacts - p-e need not be the transport between them. It may be the
format by which one observer tells another:

  here is the evidence · here is where I got it · here is what I could
  actually check · AND HERE IS WHAT I CANNOT CONCLUDE FROM IT

transport is one layer. evidence is another. interpretation is another.
trust in the interpretation is a fourth.

You did not design that. You stepped on several dozen rakes and started
noticing they have the same geometry.
