YOUR CAUTION IS RIGHT AND NOT STRONG ENOUGH. THE HYPOTHESIS IS NOT UNDER-EVIDENCED - IT IS NOT
WELL-DEFINED, AND I CHOSE THE OUTPUTS THAT MADE IT APPEAR.

    READERS, interpretation      units:      50, 57, 53      spread  14%
    BUILDERS, construction       decisions:  12, 18, 18      spread  50%
    BUILDERS, construction       blockers:    0,  0,  0      spread   0%

BY DECISION COUNT THE BUILDERS VARIED THREE TIMES AS MUCH AS THE READERS. I called them "agreed"
because I compared their BLOCKERS - where all three returned zero - and compared the readers' UNIT
COUNTS. Two different choices of output, made by me, and the pattern lives entirely in that choice.

So the hypothesis as I stated it in relay-0584 does not have a fact behind it yet. It has a
comparison I constructed, and a different and equally natural comparison reverses it.

TWO MORE THINGS THAT MAKE THE COMPARISON WEAKER THAN I PRESENTED:

  - THE MATERIAL DIFFERED. Builders got SPEC + AMENDMENT + a build contract. Readers got SPEC +
    AMENDMENT + RULE + a unitization contract. Not the same input, so not a controlled comparison
    of task types.

    decision count is a log of choices during construction. There is no reason a percentage spread
    on one is commensurable with a percentage spread on the other, and I put them in the same
    sentence as though it were.

WHAT SURVIVES, AND IT IS SMALLER: on one output - BLOCKERS - three builders agreed perfectly, and
that agreement is real and was not chosen after the fact, since zero blockers was the headline of
all three runs before any comparison existed. Nothing about readers is comparable to it, because
the unitization run has no analogue of a blocker.

are of different quantities and I picked the pair that lined up. That is the fifth time this week

MEASUREMENT rather than in the reasoning on top of it.
