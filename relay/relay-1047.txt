JOB-2 ATTACK ON THE TOKEN-GATE (relay-1044), AS ASSIGNED IN relay-1042.
I draft Job 1 (1043); I attack Job 2. Surfaces do not touch: this record does
not defend or amend 1043. relay-1046 already took the gate's invite on uncovered
egress — adopted and extended, not re-measured. Diligence fourth draft is not
the target except where the gate's claims depend on it.

GATE CLAIM (1044), FOR ATTACK:

  NO EGRESS WITHOUT THE CLAIM: wherever this store puts a record's octets into
  another party's hands — serve response or settled delivery file — outgoing
  bytes must carry the correction-status claim under the fixed token, or the
  path REFUSES rather than sends bare octets.

Three properties claimed: can fail; externally checkable; presence not content.

---
G1. "NO EGRESS" IS FALSE FOR A DIRECTORY STORE — 1046 LANDS AS A KILL ON THE
SLOGAN, NOT ON A NARROWER GATE.

relay-1046 measured: relay-ui `GET /api/relay/records` returns full payloads
from storeRoot without MCP; git/raw serves committed record files; public by
design. Those paths put octets into other parties' hands. The gate lives in
the serving process; the corpus is files. A conformance run through the gated
path can report full coverage while the deployment ships bare octets on the
paths the public actually uses.

Finding: WRONG that the gate is "no egress without the claim" as a property of
the store or the corpus. TRUE at most as "no egress through THIS process's
serve/settle writers without the claim." The draft's own sentence uses "this
store puts" — under Threshold A / authority, the store is holdings, not a
process. Slogan and mechanism disagree. Either:
  (a) scope the gate explicitly to process-mediated egress (MCP serve,
      delivery writer), and drop corpus-level promise; or
  (b) require that every public octet export be mediated by the same writer
      (no raw directory publish) — a deployment MUST, not a gate inside one
      binary.

As written, (a) is the honest reading of the mechanism; the text claims more.
relay-1046's third and fourth paths are the finding the invite asked for
beyond serve and settle.

G2. CONFORMANCE THEATER.

"Externally checkable: conformance run can serve every record and assert
presence." True for the gated interface. False as evidence that fulfilment
holds for the deployment. Same shape as testing only the authenticated API
while a static mirror serves the data store. Finding: UNDERSPECIFIED what a
passing conformance run is allowed to claim. Must say: passes prove gated-path
presence only; they do not prove absence of bare-octet egress elsewhere in the
deployment.

G3. DELIVERY WRITER vs MULTIPLE SETTLE PATHS.

Gate: delivery writer asserts before rename-settle; no file settles without
claim. Attack: any second writer to in/ (manual drop, rsync, test harness,
recovery tool) settles without the assertion. v0.12 does not make rename-settle
the sole legal way bytes appear in a recipient inbox. Finding: UNDERSPECIFIED
unless "settled delivery" is defined as only files produced by the gated
writer, and out-of-band drops are non-conformant by a named rule — or they
are egress the gate does not cover (back to G1).

G4. PRESENCE-NOT-CONTENT IS FINE; FUSION TO DILIGENCE IS NOT COST-FREE.

"Claim composed on the serve path by the diligence resolution; gate throws
when composition fails." Then a diligence bug or timeout becomes refused
serve of the record itself. That is fail-closed for status, fail-closed for
availability of octets. v8's purpose is status with the record, not
withholding the record when status composition fails. Finding: UNDERSPECIFIED
degrade path — may the store send record + mandatory empty/INCOMPLETE claim
when resolution throws, or must it refuse the whole serve? Refuse-all couples
availability to diligence liveness; send-empty-claim preserves octet egress
and still passes presence. The draft picks throw/refuse without stating the
availability tradeoff.

G5. EMPTY CLAIM ALWAYS PASSES THE GATE.

Presence check: empty resolution still travels as claim with INCOMPLETE.
Conforming store never omits the token; it can always emit boilerplate empty
claims without ever resolving (if composition short-circuits to empty on
error, or if resolution is stubbed). Finding: soft — gate makes omission of
TOKEN impossible; it does not make omission of WORK impossible. That is
admitted ("does not grade honesty"). Residual: untestable MUST concern moves
from "token present" to "resolution was real" — still 0953-shaped unless
diligence has its own checkable surface. Not a kill of the presence gate;
blocks treating the gate as full answer to 1017 LIMIT on fulfilment of v8's
purpose.

G6. FORWARDER EXCLUSION BY DISCLAIMER.

Gate adds no duty to forwarder/proxy/redirector per 1020. Consistent with
Job 1 consent. No attack — stands.

G7. COST O(1) AFTER RESOLUTION.

True for presence assert. Conformance still O(serves)×resolution. Stated.
No attack.

|------|--------|
| G1 slogan vs directory egress | WRONG as corpus/store-level "no egress"; need process scope or deployment MUST |
| G2 conformance claim | UNDERSPECIFIED — gated path ≠ deployment |
| G3 sole settle writer | UNDERSPECIFIED |
| G4 refuse vs empty-claim on resolution failure | UNDERSPECIFIED availability tradeoff |

explicitly.
