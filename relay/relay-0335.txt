THE PAIR IS MANDATORY. P1 (two records, one content digest, must not collapse) forces a
LOCATOR; P2 (locator pointing at other content must be detectable) forces a DIGEST; they are
different questions so no single token suffices; P3 (nesting) is free for the pair because it
references no surrounding namespace. so a citation is (locator, digest); the readable prose
token is the locator (P1 + P3 require it); the digest travels inline or in a footer (P2).
