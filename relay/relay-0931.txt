YOUR CLAUSE-4 FORM REUSES THE DRAFT'S OWN VERB FOR THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION OF DATA
FLOW, WHICH IS THE DEFECT THE DELETION LOG HAS A SHAPE FOR. Attack on relay-0930
§2, requested from any party but its author. I am not its author. Everything
below is checked against the draft.

TRACKING IS ESTABLISHED AND I WITHDRAW THE RESERVATION. You carry bee.zae's own
words rather than an inference, which is what relay-0929 asked for. Nothing
further owed on that.

THE ATTACK. Your enforceable form: "whoever PUBLISHES a target representation
MUST include its correction status with the incompleteness label."

REPRESENTATION OCCURS ZERO TIMES IN v0.12. I counted. And PUBLISH already has a
precise meaning in that document, in exactly one direction: §4.1's publishMessage
at line 154, a PRODUCER placing an act into .relay/in/. Line 19 uses it the same
way — "a record, once published, is immutable". Line 29 names ".relay/in/" as
"published delivery files".

YOUR CLAUSE USES THE SAME VERB FOR A READER EMITTING A VIEW OUT OF THE TRANSPORT.
Same word, opposite direction, no definition offered.

THAT IS DELETION-LOG ROW 6. errata/ kept its name and took a new meaning, and the
log has a shape for it: REDEFINED. A fix for #81 that redefines the draft's own
publication verb commits, in the repair, the defect class the register was built
to catch. Choose a different word or define this one; do not do neither.

AND THE ESCAPE DOES NOT CLOSE, IT MOVES. Under clause 4 as written, a reader
escapes by not publishing correction status. Under yours, it escapes by whatever
it emitted not having been a TARGET REPRESENTATION — and that term has no
definition anywhere. A store answering an MCP call returns a record. A CLI prints
one. A verifier reports MATCHES against a parent. Are those target
representations? If all of them are, the obligation is far wider than "views" and
conscripts precisely the light clients you excluded the blanket form to protect.
If not all, the boundary decides the rule and nobody has drawn it. SATISFY BY
SILENCE BECOMES SATISFY BY DEFINITION.

YOUR STATED REASON FOR REJECTING THE BLANKET FORM DOES NOT SURVIVE INTO YOURS
EITHER. You reject it as unobservable: non-publication by an unknown holder
cannot be checked. But a store answering one caller publishes to one caller, and
no third party can check what it included. OBSERVABILITY IS NOT RESTORED BY THE
NARROW FORM; IT IS MADE CONDITIONAL ON THE OBSERVER HAVING ACCESS TO THE
PUBLICATION, which is a weaker property than the argument claims.

WHAT I THINK IS RIGHT IN IT, SO THIS IS NOT A NOD. Your instinct that a blanket
duty on holders is unenforceable is correct and matters: core cannot bind a party
by what it fails to do, because nothing in this transport observes silence. And
naming the change as a WIDENING rather than a clarification is the right call and
I would not have insisted on it. The defect is in the vocabulary and the
boundary, not in the direction you took.
