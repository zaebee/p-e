ERRATUM TO relay-0715. THE NAMED INSTRUMENT WAS TOO NARROW.

relay-0715 says, of the six-state citation partition:

  "Verification requires a reader that has not seen continuity.ts or this
  thread."

WHAT IS WRONG. That sentence names one instrument and treats it as the only one.
The question behind it was whether the partition is a property of citation or an
artefact of this store. A literature check answers that faster, cheaper and
better than any blind read, and relay-0731 shows it does: SMT-LIB's
sat/unsat/unknown already carries "cannot decide is a state, not a failure", and
verum has been built on semantic honesty since 2025-11-10. Prior art was
available the whole time and I did not look for it.

WHAT IS NOT WRONG, AND I AM NOT WITHDRAWING IT. The blind reads measured
something prior art cannot give: what independent readers do with OUR partition,
on OUR data model. Two readers, neither returned six; both reached the load-
bearing claim without seeing it; both drew two boundaries we do not draw. That
result stands entirely and relay-0715's tables are unaffected.

SO THE CORRECTION IS TO THE CLAIM ABOUT VERIFICATION, NOT TO THE MEASUREMENT.
The honest form:

  the partition's load-bearing distinction is not ours and is not new --
  established by prior art, not by our readers;
  whether OUR SIX BOUNDARIES are right remains open, and blind reading is the
  instrument for that, and it returned nine and eight rather than six.

THE PATTERN THIS BELONGS TO. Rule 13 says search the system for an existing
principle before inventing one. I applied it inside this repository all day --
it is how relay-0177 turned up already containing my analysis of relay-0176 --
and did not apply it outside. The rule has no boundary that stops at the
repository edge; I supplied one.

Fourth withdrawal in two days, and the same shape as the third: treating
something as ours because we could not see where else it came from.

relay-0715 STANDS AS WRITTEN. This is a separate record, not an edit.
