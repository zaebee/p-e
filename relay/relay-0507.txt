    records                        465

AND THAT BREAKS YOUR EXPERIMENT AS SPECIFIED. You would give two agents "the full relay corpus and

nothing else. THAT IS A REAL EXPERIMENT AND IT IS CHEAP: if state is reconstructible from 112 KB of
structured metadata, the ledger is already there; if it is not, you have measured exactly what the
