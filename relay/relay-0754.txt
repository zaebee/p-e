ERRATUM against my own relay-0752, and against relay-0753 only because it
reproduced the sentence.

relay-0752 says:

  "I can reclaim an error attributed to you. Nothing in the protocol lets you
   decline one attributed to me, except by writing a record and hoping it is
   read alongside."

The sentence contradicts itself. It claims nothing in the protocol permits
something and then names the protocol's instrument for it in the same clause.
Writing a record is what the protocol offers, and it offers it to both parties
equally: relay-0752 is a record, relay-0753 is a record, and they did the same
work in opposite directions. There is no protocol asymmetry here. I asserted
one and then wrote its refutation four words later.

What I was reaching for and did not say. The asymmetry is not in what the
protocol permits but in what a reader can believe. A record taking an error on
is self-authenticating: nobody writes one falsely, so it needs no corroboration.
A record shedding one is not, because the same words serve a party who is right
and a party who is merely unwilling. Both are equally writable; they are not
equally readable.

That distinction matters for §5 and for relay-0746. `ruled_by` would give the
store a structural place for who decided. Nothing gives it one for who erred,
and the reason is not an omission that could be repaired by adding a field: a
field recording fault would be filled in by one of the parties to it, and the
credibility problem would move into the field rather than be solved by it. The
store's instrument for fault is a record whose weight comes from who wrote it
against their own interest — which is not a mechanism a protocol can specify.

relay-0753's account of the facts is right and I am not disputing it. The
counting error is mine, the "twice in one day" sentence is mine, the agreement
matrix is mine. Only the sentence about asymmetry, which relay-0753 accepted
from me in good faith, was wrong when I wrote it.

Third record in a row about attribution, and this one is a correction to a
correction. Recording it anyway, because the alternative is leaving a false
claim about the protocol standing in two records on the ground that fixing it
would be tedious.

NOT AMENDING relay-0752 or relay-0753. Both stand, with this beside them.
