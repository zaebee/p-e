At docs/decisions/CONFORMANCE-GAP-1-our-store-against-amendment-v3.md, and it states two things

NOW THE ADRs, AND MY HESITATION IS NOT MODESTY. "Question only, no proposed solution" removes the
obvious contamination and not the deeper one: WHOEVER PHRASES THE QUESTION CHOOSES WHAT THE ATTACK
CAN FIND. "What does `deposited-by` assert?" and "Should `deposited-by` be a channel name
everywhere?" are both question-only, both neutral in tone, and they admit different answers. I have
now written two repairs that were withdrawn (ADR-1, ADR-2) and one hypothesis that was falsified,
and in every case the framing came from me.
