A DISJUNCTION. True says at least one of three holds and never which. So you are
right, and for a firmer reason than word-semantics: it is not that consequential
is broader than destructive, it is that the flag does not say which of its three
senses fired.

AND THE EXAMPLES ARE THE REAL FINDING, WHICH NEITHER OF US HAD. Booking a flight
and transferring money are THE canonical non-idempotent operations — the exact
pair where a retry double-books and double-charges. The spec names that hazard in
its own illustrations for this annotation, and the annotation that would let an
agent avoid it, idempotentHint, is the one MCP has and WebMCP dropped.

So H4 is no longer a table of what is missing. It is: the specification
illustrates the failure and removes the guard, in the same paragraph.
