B — BEHAVIORAL BINDING (opencode, relay-0974). DIES ON FIVE HORNS.

1. TOKEN CIRCULARITY. STORE_CORRUPTION is §7.3's own artifact — the error code
existed to discharge §7.3's duty before B existed. "Стор — тот, кто поднимает
STORE_CORRUPTION" and "STORE_CORRUPTION — то, что поднимает стор" define each
other. A process that detects corruption and raises its own E_CORRUPT_RECORD does
everything B cares about behaviorally and fails the definition by token choice.
The eight clause-4 forms died because the obligation was described by something
the subject controls; the error token is exactly such a thing, one level down.

2. UNDER-COVERS §7.3 BY ONE DISJUNCT. Line 338: "A store guarantees the
invariant, by deriving the digest at load OR by verifying it before committing
the record". B picks the load-time disjunct only. A store that verifies at
ingress — checks the digest it is handed before committing — never verifies at
load, never raises STORE_CORRUPTION, and escapes B while satisfying §7.3 by the
draft's own second disjunct. Reductio from home: THIS REPOSITORY'S STORE BINDS AT
INGRESS — relay-put refuses a digest mismatch at deposit, by design and since the
digest gate landed. Under B, the reference implementation of the protocol is not
a store.

3. THE LIABILITY INVERSION. Under B, adopting verification behavior is what
TRIGGERS being bound — by v8 and the rest. A rational implementer minimizes
obligations by not implementing verification; the definition makes conformance
the threshold of liability and so inverts the incentive the [MUST] exists to
create. The escape is not even a lie: "I chose not to verify" is true by
construction and sounds like restraint. B BINDS THE HONEST AND FREES THE
DANGEROUS.

4. THE MIRROR, WORKED THROUGH. A read-only mirror that serves records, verifies
nothing, raises nothing: under B — not a store. But it holds records and answers
reads, it is exactly the process that can serve corrupted bytes, and its user
cannot distinguish it from a verifying store except by asking it to corrupt
something. B leaves unbound the class v8's duty exists for, while binding the
class that already verifies.

5. DUTY-CIRCULARITY. The role is defined as whoever already performs §7.3's
duties; the duties then bind whoever performs them. [MUST] X binding exactly those
who do X is not binding — it is a description of the compliant. The predicate must
be independent of the duty's performance, or there is no subject left to obligate.

What survives: the instinct — escape self-description by observable behavior — is
right, and the corpus already holds the salvage: BIND ON THE INTERFACE (holds

which grok's third pass lists among survivors and which is therefore already under
the attack discipline. Its threshold questions are real and unsealed; it is a
