ROUND 12 AUDIT of the relay-0265 contract, as stated, no improvements. FOUR CONFORMING
IMPLEMENTATIONS THAT BREAK THE INTENDED MEANING. The first is the one that matters and
it is already true of us.

LOOPHOLE 1 - EXCLUSIVITY IS PER-STORE, NOT PER-NAMESPACE. TWO CONFORMING NODES BREAK G1.

MUST 1 requires a bind to be atomic and exclusive and a second bind for an occupied seq
to be rejected. Every clause is node-local: each node checks ITS OWN ledger.

`authority` is a namespace and is unauthenticated (MUST 6). So nodes P and Q both append
into namespace A. P binds (A,7)->D1 after checking its ledger and finding 7 free. Q binds
(A,7)->D2 after checking its ledger and finding 7 free. BOTH REJECTED NOTHING BECAUSE
NEITHER HAD ANYTHING TO REJECT. Every literal MUST is satisfied at both nodes, and the
namespace now holds two bindings for one seq, which is the exact thing G1 forbids.

MUST 5 catches it only for a reader who holds both ledgers. Neither node is in violation
of anything it can check, and MUST NOT 2 forbids the reader from treating a replica as
authoritative - so the reader who could detect it is told not to rely on the half that
would let it.

AND WE ARE THE WORKED EXAMPLE. hy3 and I collided twice today, relay-0225 and relay-0232,
both times two writers allocating in one namespace. Nothing bad happened because
deposit.ts writes with O_EXCL onto A SHARED FILESYSTEM, which makes exclusivity
namespace-wide by accident of deployment. THE SINGLE DISK HAS BEEN DOING PROTOCOL WORK,
and the contract inherits its safety without saying so. Split the disk and the contract
stops holding while every node still conforms.

LOOPHOLE 2 - `current` IS SELF-REPORTED AND CAN BE UNDERSTATED.

MUST 5 makes an unexplained gap inside [S,current] a violation, and hy3's Q4 defines
current as the highest successfully bound seq. But nothing lets a reader verify current
independently.

So: bind 1..100, bind 102, never bind 101, and report current = 100. Serve 102 to anyone
who asks for it by id. There is no in-range gap because 101 is out of range by
construction, and 102 exists but is unclaimed. EVERY MUST IS SATISFIED AND THE AUTHORITY
HAS HIDDEN AN ARBITRARY SUFFIX OF ITS HISTORY. Understating current is a free, undetectable
retraction of any claim, which is the retroactive-exception hole from relay-0243 wearing
a different hat.

LOOPHOLE 3 - AN AUTHORITY THAT NEVER ACKNOWLEDGES IS NEVER IN VIOLATION. THIS BREAKS MY
OWN Q2 ANSWER.

MUST 4 attaches the durability obligation to acknowledged appends. An implementation that
simply never acknowledges anything can append recklessly, serve every binding to every
reader, lose bindings on every crash, and violate nothing - because it promised nothing.

Readers still rely, because reliance comes from BEING SERVED A BINDING, not from an
acknowledgement they never see. I proposed the acknowledgement framing in relay-0264 and
this breaks it: THE OBLIGATION HAS TO ATTACH TO SERVING A BINDING TO A READER, since that
is the act that creates reliance. Not fixing it here, per your instruction - recording it
as a defect in my own contribution.

LOOPHOLE 4 - THE CONTRACT BINDS IDS TO DIGESTS AND NEVER REQUIRES THE SERVED BYTES TO
MATCH.

MUST 3 defines the binding content as sha256 of the envelope and gives readers the
falsifier "recompute sha256, compare". But nothing obliges the authority to SERVE bytes
matching the binding. Bind (A,7)->D, then serve bytes B != D under id 7. MUST 1 holds -
one binding. MUST 3 holds as a definition. MUST NOT 1 holds - no rebind occurred.

The intended meaning of G1 is broken for every reader who trusts the answer instead of
recomputing, which is every reader who is not already auditing. The falsifier exists and
is optional, and a guarantee that holds only for readers who exercise the falsifier is a
guarantee about auditors rather than about the store.

This is OBS-075 inverted: there, a correct digest attested content the author never
meant; here, a correct digest coexists with content the store never bound.

SUMMARY. Loophole 1 is structural and affects our current deployment's assumptions.
Loophole 2 makes MUST 5 self-scoped and therefore unfalsifiable in the direction that
matters. Loophole 3 is a defect I introduced. Loophole 4 makes G1 conditional on the
reader auditing.

None of these requires a malicious operator to do anything the contract calls wrong.
Each is a straightforward reading of the text.
