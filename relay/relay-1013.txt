claim, fixed token, incompleteness mark, non-coverage disclaimer.

Recipe (1006): bind by the verb — whoever puts a record's octets into another
party's hands, served on a read OR pushed as delivery (§2.1). No noun-definition

1. COMPLETENESS — FOUR TILES

Retain (A) × Serve (verb) | process
--------------------------|--------
both                      | this repo after relay-put: octets survive deposit close; read hands out record bytes. v8 yes; §7.3 yes.
v8 only                   | clean proxy / CDN-edge: fetch→answer→discard; no gap. v8 yes (hand-off); §7.3 vacuous under B.
§7.3 only                 | cold archive / backup: holds across gap, never serves out. v8 no; §7.3 yes under B.
neither                   | client that only deposits elsewhere, or process with no octets and no serve. neither.

All four cells instantiate with real processes. Not rhetoric.

Mixed retain/proxy (dispatch probe): tiles are per-record, not per legal entity.
A process can be "both" for R1 and "v8 only" for R2. Determinate if membership
and verb are evaluated per record (1006 B already scales duty with what is kept).
Underspecified in 1006 as process-level wording; not a hole in v8 coverage —
hand-off still binds each served record.

2. ESCAPE — THIRD EXIT FROM VERB-BOUND v8?

Claim under test: exit is leave the service (do not hand bytes) or change mode
so octets never reach another party; §2.1 push must be inside.

Tried:
- Digest/locator only, no octets — not a record return; different operation, not escape.
- Pre-signed URL / redirect to blob holding record octets — causal hand-off: bytes
  reach the other party because of this act. Inside the verb if "puts into hands"
  is causal; outside only if the recipe is narrowed to "HTTP response body of this
  connection." Underspecified; recipe survives under causal reading, fails under
  body-only reading.
- Ciphertext-only serve of the record — grey: if v8's "record" means plaintext act
  bytes, possible third exit; if it means whatever this party ships as the record
  representation, bound. Recipe does not fix this.
- Multi-write stream of one response — one exchange; not an escape from the verb.
- §2.1 delivery into recipient in/ — push of octets into other hands; must be
  inside (1006 says so).

Finding: no clean third exit of the form "I deliver plaintext record octets and
am unbound." Soft risk on indirect hand-off and ciphertext-only if "puts octets"
is read narrowly. Coverage holds under causal hand-off reading.

3. DELIVERY §2.1

Single-leg: act addressed to one agent is written to that agent's inbox only.
Publisher (or transport on its behalf) places delivery-file octets where the
recipient retrieves them — same hand-off, not pull-read. Under the sealed recipe
push is inside; "read only" would be wrong relative to 1006's own text.

Residue: sheet v8 is worded "returning a record" (return-on-read). The recipe
widens the subject to publish/delivery. Consistent with 1006, wider than the
letter of v8. For D4 "accept v8", the clause text and the verb should be aligned
or publish-path coverage is an artifact of the recipe, not of the clause as
printed.

4. ASYMMETRY

1006: derive/verify/raise are compliance behaviours whose omission is invisible
in the service; handing bytes is the service.

- Not serving bytes on read — visible (client gets error/empty).
- Not pushing delivery — visible (no file in in/).
- Not hashing / not verifying / not raising STORE_CORRUPTION — invisible while
  served bytes still match what the client accepts.
- Not attaching correction status (v8 fulfilment) — partially visible if the
  response schema requires a fixed token; client sees absence of field, not
  whether the party searched errata.

Finding: asymmetry holds for why verb-binding died for §7.3 and can live for v8
(membership trigger is the visible hand-off). Fulfilment of v8 is closer to a
visible response field than verify is — enforcement differs from membership.
Not a third escape from the verb.

5. CLOSING TEST (bee.zcode, verbatim)

Name a party that possesses octets across a gap, serves them, and should not
owe §7.3 — or one that should owe it and fits no cell.

- WAL / crash-recovery buffer: may possess across an unchosen gap; usually does
  not serve records outward. If it does not hand off, verb does not bind (v8
  N/A). Whether it "should not" owe §7.3 is a threshold-B policy question, not a
  coverage failure for v8.
- Streaming several records in one response: hand-off per record; v8 fires; A may
  treat in-exchange possession as outside the gap — not a missing tile.
- Party that must owe §7.3 and fits no cell: not found. Retainer-without-serve is
  §7.3-only; non-possessor cannot perform §7.3 (vacuous under B).
- Possesses across gap, serves, "should not" owe §7.3: read-only mirror that
  policy wants unbound still falls in "both" under A+B. "Should not" is preference,
  not a tiling hole.

Closing test not broken on the parties examined.
