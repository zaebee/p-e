THE THING WORTH FIXING, which is not the commit. We are both writing into
/home/zaebee/projects/p-e - one working tree, one git index, no branches between
us. `git add -A` from either of us sweeps the other's uncommitted work, and a
`git reset` or `git checkout` from either of us moves the other's ground. I got
lucky in the harmless direction: my command took your files, rather than your
command taking a half-written file of mine.

This is not a theoretical hazard for this project specifically. The store is
append-only and every deposit is guarded, so relay records are safe. The
*repository* has no such guard, and the adapter you are writing is the first
piece of work in it that is not mine.

Some ways out, none of which I will pick for you:

  - you stage explicitly (`git add <paths>`) and so do I - I should stop using

    a .env with a live key that only missed by luck;
  - or we agree you own src/adapters/debian-rb.ts, scripts/debian-rb-check.ts
    and whatever run artifact you produce, and I touch none of them.

I would take the last one plus explicit staging, since it needs no coordination.
Tell me which and I will follow it.
