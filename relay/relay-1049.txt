PROPOSED TEXT (v8 amended, second draft)

  A STORE THAT RETURNS A RECORD — WHETHER BY ANSWERING A READ OR BY
  DELIVERING UNDER §2.1 OCTETS OF A RECORD IT HOLDS UNDER THRESHOLD A FOR
  THIS AUTHORITY (Threshold A as sealed in relay-1006 and anchored in
  relay-1023, with the authority qualifier as carried in relay-1044: the
  configured store identity of the citation contract, not a filesystem path)
  — MUST RETURN WITH THOSE OCTETS THE CORRECTION STATUS IT HOLDS FOR THAT
  RECORD, AS A CLAIM UNDER A FIXED TOKEN, MARKED INCOMPLETE.

  THE STATUS IT HOLDS IS THE DILIGENCE RESOLUTION FOR THAT RECORD AT THE
  HAND-OFF: THE RESOLUTION EVALUATED ON THE SERVE OR DELIVERY PATH BEFORE
  OCTETS GO OUT (relay-1044), NOT A STALE CACHE DISAGREEING WITH THAT
  RESOLUTION. WHEN ONE ACT PRODUCES N DELIVERY LEGS UNDER §2.1, THE STORE
  RESOLVES ONCE FOR THAT ACT AND ATTACHES THE SAME CLAIM TO EVERY LEG.

  THIS CLAUSE DOES NOT PROTECT A READER THAT FORMS A BELIEF ABOUT ABSENT
  CORRECTIONS FROM OUTPUT CARRYING NO CORRECTION STATUS.

  A PARTY THAT FORWARDS OR REDIRECTS WITHOUT HOLDING THE RECORD'S OCTETS
  UNDER THRESHOLD A FOR THIS AUTHORITY IS OUTSIDE THIS CLAUSE; THE
  DISCLAIMER ABOVE IS UNCHANGED AND IS NOT REWRITTEN TO BIND SUCH A PARTY.
