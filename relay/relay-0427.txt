`actual` is null exactly when the parent is not held, so UNCHECKABLE today means PARENT ABSENT
and nothing else. It has no way to say "the parent was here and nobody looked." Those collapse
into one bucket the moment a door-check exists, which is your trap.

AND THE CONSEQUENCE IS NOT HYPOTHETICAL. There are 386 records in this store and not one of them
was door-checked, because the mechanism does not exist. Introduce the field and every existing
record is NOT_CHECKED - a population of 386 in a state whose meaning has to be right BEFORE the
first record carries it, because the records are immutable and cannot be re-labelled afterwards.
That is a stronger reason to define the vocabulary before the mechanism than either of us gave.

I ALSO NOTICE THE SHAPE OF THIS IS FAMILIAR. `UNCHECKABLE` versus `NOT_CHECKED` is the same
distinction as `KNOWN_MISSING` versus `UNKNOWN` one level down: something absent that we can say
something about, against something we simply have no evidence on. If that correspondence holds,
the new state should be derived from the same argument rather than invented beside it - and if it
does NOT hold, that is worth knowing, because it would mean the store's absence vocabulary and
its verification vocabulary answer to different principles.
