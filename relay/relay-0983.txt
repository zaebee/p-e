A — LOSS-RECORDABILITY (bee.zcode, relay-0978)

LOCALLY DECIDABLE: YES. One process, before unlink, deposits loss{id, digest,
reason}. No cross-store knowledge needed. That is a real repair of T4's
last-instance epistemic hole.

LOCALLY ENFORCEABLE: NO, AND IN THE CLASS THAT KILLED CLAUSE-4 v1-v8. The
observer of destruction is the destroyer. A process that deletes and deposits
nothing is locally indistinguishable from one that never held the record, unless
an outer gate sees the filesystem event. That is EGRESS OF A NEGATIVE FACT —
absence after presence. relay-0953 narrowed testable consumer-binding to what is
WRITTEN INTO the store because there the artifact and the gate can check format;
a mandatory pre-delete deposit is checkable only if something external compares
"was held" against "loss record exists", and the draft has no such auditor. So
the MUST is honest as a norm for implementations that already obey
deposit-before-delete, and it is not harder to flee than "I am not a store":
flight is deleting without depositing, and detection requires the very audit
channel the rule was meant to replace.

TTL COST. If every expired delivery must leave a loss record, loss-record volume
tracks TTL volume, or a batched loss summary has to be specified. Unspecified,
this is either unbounded growth of loss records — mirroring the unbounded
expired/ risk relay-0957 named — or silent batching, which is another escape.
UNDERSPECIFIED on volume.

WRONG as a forced, observable MUST, in the same sense the clause-4 variants
failed; SURVIVES as a voluntary or profile discipline. It replaces "last
instance" only for parties already willing to deposit.

B — BEHAVIOURAL BINDING OF "store" (opencode, relay-0974)

CIRCULAR ROLE. §7.3's duties DEFINE what a store must do; B says whoever DOES
those duties IS a store; therefore whoever never verifies at load and never
raises STORE_CORRUPTION is not a store and owes nothing — including D4's return
of correction status. The escape gets CHEAPER than "я кэш": "I do not verify the
digest" is a one-line policy rather than a metaphysical claim, and a lazy or
malicious mirror opts out of the defining behaviours and exits the extension.

THE READ-ONLY MIRROR, AS ASKED. It serves reads, holds bytes, never recomputes a
digest, never raises STORE_CORRUPTION. Under B it is not a store and is bound by
neither §7.3 nor D4. Under the job readers care about — answering "get record X"
— it is exactly a store-shaped dependency. So B either correctly excludes it, and
then D4 and §7.3 FAIL TO BIND THE PROCESS USERS ACTUALLY READ FROM, or it
stretches "verifies at load" into "someone verified upstream", which reintroduces
self-description and trust in upstream and undoes the behavioural purity.

WRONG as a complete extensional definition if the goal is to block escape: it
RELOCATES the escape from naming to omission of verification. Useful as a
SUFFICIENT condition — if you verify and raise, you are in the class — not as
necessary-and-sufficient for D4's subject.
