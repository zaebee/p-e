HOLE 1 - THE ROOT-CAUSE FIX IS MISSING FROM MIGRATION.
the draft names "delete" as "the case we currently fail," which is relay-0183. but the
Migration section lists only reference.ts:94/:105, nextFree(), and check-continuity. the
guard that actually permitted the rebind is deposit.ts:68 - `held.has(proposedId)`, which
asks whether the id is CURRENTLY HELD, not whether it was EVER BOUND. the non-rewindable
ledger is what MUST answer that question, and the guard does not consult it. until
deposit.ts:68 consults the ever-bound ledger, MUST 1 / G1 is not enforceable even for the
single authority - the incident recurs on the next delete-and-rebind. add to Migration:
"deposit.ts:68 - the bind check must consult the non-rewindable ledger (ever-bound), not
the live held set." this is the one fix the incident was filed for; leaving it out leaves
the incident open.

HOLE 2 - MUST 1 IS ABSOLUTE BUT THE RECORDED EXCEPTION IS A REUSE.
MUST 1: "never reuses a seq." but the legacy authority, under exceptions-allowed, records
relay-0183 as a reused seq INSIDE its G1 claim. that is a reuse, permanently. the two
statements contradict unless MUST 1 is qualified by the guarantee-start clause: "an
authority never reuses a seq FROM ITS DECLARED GUARANTEE-START, except as a permanently
recorded exception." without that qualification the contract both embraces the 183
exception (Migration) and forbids it (MUST 1). qualify it.

HOLE 3 - NO MINIMUM WITNESS OBLIGATION.
MUST 7 says the ABSENCE of a witness is reported as absence. nothing says what a PRESENT
witness attests, so the "witnessed" capability is undefined in obligation. "What a witness
does" lists (a) saw digest, (b) saw binding, (c) covers head - but as options. for G2b
("binding survives the author") to mean anything, a witness attesting a record MUST attest
the binding (b): saw (id, digest). publishing leaves (c) is what makes inclusion provable
and is the strongest. add: "a witness that attests a record MUST attest its binding
(id, digest); it MAY attest coverage under a head by publishing leaves." otherwise "witnessed"
can be satisfied by (a), which says nothing about a record.

HOLE 4 - EQUIVOCATION DETECTION RULE IS UNSTATED.
MUST NOT says equivocation by a non-conforming authority is "detected, not prevented," but
the draft never states the detection condition. it is: two distinct digests observed for one
(authority, seq) is the finding. a witness that sees a second head for an already-seen
(authority, seq) MUST surface it, not hide it - hiding it would be the OBS-072 substitution
at the witness layer. add the detection rule next to the MUST NOT, so "detected" is
operational rather than asserted.
