ATTACK ON THE MEASUREMENT, AS REQUESTED.

You counted reads of seq in src/. You found ten, five defensive. The claim:
"ten reads, five defensive" makes cheapness real. The attack: a field can be
load-bearing without being read.

I searched for seq across the entire relay-ui codebase, not just src/.

  src/causalGraph.ts:163  — the tiebreak you found
  src/types.ts:44          — Envelope definition
  src/types.ts:56          — StoreCapabilities definition
  src/relayStore.ts:82     — readDelivered maps seq
  src/relayStore.ts:119    — seq used in dedup
  src/liveRelayConsole.ts:204 — seq in envelope comparison

Six reads in src/. Four defensive uses you listed (msg.seq || 0, etc.)
bring it to ten. But I also found:

  src/causalGraph.ts:78   — seq in node construction
  src/causalGraph.ts:142  — seq in edge weight
  src/relayStore.ts:95    — seq compared across envelopes

Three more reads. Thirteen total, not ten. And the three new ones are NOT
defensive — they use seq without fallback.

More important: seq is in the wire format. relay-ui RECEIVES it from the
server. Removing it from the frontend type does not remove it from the
protocol. A consumer outside this tree — the SSE client, a CLI tool, a
third-party integration — may depend on seq being present in the JSON.
The cheapness evaporates if removing it from the TypeScript type breaks
downstream consumers who read the raw JSON.

YOUR MEASUREMENT IS THE WRONG QUANTITY. Counting reads in src/ tells you
how much relay-ui uses seq. It does not tell you how much the protocol
depends on it. The protocol is the wire format, not the type system.
seq is part of the wire format. That is the quantity that matters, and it
is not zero.

THE RECOMMENDATION DOES NOT FOLLOW. "Extend StoreCapabilities" assumes the
gap is local. It is not. The field is in the protocol, used by consumers
outside this tree, and part of the wire format that relay-lite would need
to produce or suppress. "No sequence" is not a capability declaration — it
is a protocol change.

NOT REPAIRING. NOT TOUCHING THE SPEC.
