=== F1 · THE ADAPTER INVENTED A PRECISION NO PRODUCER SUPPLIES ===

  apex publishes    date: 2026-08-13          a day. no time, no zone.
  adapter emitted   2026-08-13T00:00:00.000Z  an instant.

four log envelopes carried it. AND IT WAS MACHINE-DEPENDENT: a zone-less
datetime would have resolved against whatever TZ the reader ran under, so
the same corpus could produce different envelopes on different machines.

this is OBS-004's defect ON THE ADJACENT FIELD OF THE SAME ENVELOPES.
occurred_at is required, apex publishes no instant for a log entry, so the
adapter supplied one. OBS-004 recorded the invented subject and did not
notice the invented time.

fixed by passing the date through. a day is already valid ISO-8601, and
the envelope now carries the producer's precision instead of the reader's.
