`npx tsc --noEmit` fails the repository:

  src/adapters/debian-rb.ts(67,10): error TS2532: Object is possibly 'undefined'

That is `m[1]` in `rustEnumVariants`, under `noUncheckedIndexedAccess: true` in
tsconfig.json, which types every index access as possibly undefined. It is a
type-level complaint only: group 1 of `enum\s+NAME\s*\{([\s\S]*?)\}` always
captures when the regex matches, and you already return early on `!m`, so it
cannot be undefined at runtime. `const body = m[1] ?? ""` or destructuring
settles it without changing behaviour. Mentioning it because the repository's
typecheck is currently red and mine is the only other work in it.

WHAT I CHECKED RATHER THAN ASSUMED, because it bears on your I-1 verdict and I
would rather have found a real problem than a lint. I suspected
`rustEnumVariants` might be reading the wrong file: `Status` does not exist in
v1build.rs, and a miss returns `[]` silently rather than raising, so an I-1
finding could have rested on an empty vocabulary. It does not. You read `Status`
from v0Src and `BuildStatus` from v1Src, and running your function over the
frozen bytes gives:

  v0 Status        ["Good","Bad","Unknown"]
  v0 BuildStatus   ["Good","Bad","Fail"]
  v1 BuildStatus   ["Good","Bad","Fail","Unknown"]

All three correct, including the non-greedy `[\s\S]*?` surviving the serde and
clap attributes between variants, which I expected to break it. The substance is
sound; only the diagnostics are outstanding.

The silent `[]` on a miss is still worth a thought, though nothing currently
triggers it. An empty vocabulary and a vocabulary of nothing are the same value
in that return, and one of them is a fact about the source while the other is a
fact about the regex — which is the invariant your adapter is checking, one level
in.
