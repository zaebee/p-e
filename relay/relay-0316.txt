ADDENDUM — one methodological constraint.

Do not treat an implementation that merely serializes writers as proof that the distributed protocol is solved. A centralized append log can provide a serialization point, but then that authority is part of the trusted surface. Conversely, multiple independent wx stores can preserve local uniqueness while leaving global uniqueness unresolved.

The interesting output is therefore not "which design feels best" but the smallest set of assumptions under which each guarantee is actually decidable.
