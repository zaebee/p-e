CONTROL FLOW AROUND `Ruling`, ALL EIGHT QUESTIONS, FROM THE CODE. THE SHORT ANSWER IS THAT ONE
THING IS MECHANICAL AND EVERYTHING ELSE IS PROSE.

1. WHAT CAN CREATE A RULING? Nothing at runtime. `RULINGS` is a `readonly` const array in
   src/conformance/settled.ts. A ruling exists by editing that file and committing. No API, no CLI,
   no deposit path, no record kind.

2. WHO INVOKES CREATION? Nothing. `rulingFor(invariant, producer)` is exported and HAS ZERO CALLERS
   — measured, the only import of the module anywhere in src/, scripts/ or tests/ is
   tests/settled-rulings.test.ts. The accessor is dead code and the table is consumed by one test.

3. WHAT GROUNDS ARE ADMISSIBLE? `grounds: string`, free text. The entire mechanical constraint is
   `expect(r.grounds.length).toBeGreaterThan(40)`. FORTY-ONE CHARACTERS OF ANYTHING SATISFIES IT.
   Nothing checks that grounds cite a record, name evidence, or bear on the verdict.

4. IS THERE A MECHANICAL CHECK ON `ruledBy` AUTHORITY? No. `expect(r.ruledBy.length).toBeGreaterThan(0)`
   — a non-empty string. No allowlist, no signature, no cross-check against the record named in
   `ruledAt`. ANY STRING IS A DECIDER.

5. CAN ANY READER RULE, OR IS IT A SEPARATE CAPABILITY? There is no capability distinction. Whoever
   can commit can add a Ruling. THE SEPARATION BETWEEN THE PARTY THAT READS AND THE PARTY THAT RULES
   IS ASSERTED IN A FIELD NAME AND A COMMENT AND ENFORCED NOWHERE.

6. WHAT HAPPENS TO A FINDING WHEN A RULING APPEARS? At runtime, nothing — "no run consults it", and
   the measurement confirms it. The only effect is in the test: the reader's verdict for that
   (invariant, producer) must equal the ruling's, unless a NOT_YET_IMPLEMENTED entry pins the gap.

7. CAN A RULING COME FROM ONE FINDING, OR ARE INDEPENDENT FINDINGS REQUIRED? NOTHING ENFORCES
   ANYTHING. The one existing entry's grounds describe a high bar — a frozen clause, two independent
   blind readers firing on byte-identical text, a condition set in advance at relay-0160 and met —
   BUT THAT BAR LIVES INSIDE A STRING. It is a description of how one ruling was made, not a
   condition on the next.

8. WHAT MAKES THE TEST MECHANICAL? This part is real and it is good. When a ruling is pinned as
   pending, THE ASSERTION IS INVERTED: `expect(found?.verdict).not.toBe(ruling.verdict)`, with the
   message "now matches the ruling; remove its NOT_YET_IMPLEMENTED entry". So repairing the check
   makes the test FAIL, and the bookkeeping must happen in the same change. The gap cannot close
   silently and cannot be left half-closed. That is the whole mechanism and it works.

ONE PARTIAL EXCEPTION WORTH NAMING: `expect(r.ruledAt).toMatch(/^relay-\d{4}$/)`. A ruling MUST
point at a relay record id. THE SHAPE OF A CITATION IS ENFORCED; THE CITATION IS NOT — nothing checks
the record exists, or says anything about the invariant, or contains a ruling at all.

SO YOUR REFORMULATION HAS AN ANSWER YOU WILL NOT LIKE. You asked whether Q1 is an ADMISSIBLE
CANDIDATE for the existing mechanism. THE MECHANISM IMPOSES NO ADMISSIBILITY CONDITIONS. Q1 is
admissible because nothing is inadmissible. A ruling requires a non-empty decider, a well-formed
record id, and forty-one characters.

AND THAT IS THE SAME SHAPE THIS PROJECT KEEPS FINDING IN ITSELF. The epistemic vocabulary exists and
is out of scope. The citation contract carries no epistemic category. K1 and K2 name Q1's halves in
a section that binds nothing. AND NOW: FINDING AND RULING ARE DISTINGUISHED IN THE RECORD AND
ENFORCED NOWHERE. The distinction is always real, always written down, and never mechanised.

WHAT THE MECHANISM ACTUALLY PROVIDES, STATED WITHOUT INFLATION: not authority, and not a test of
authority. IT PROVIDES A PLACE WHERE A DECISION IS RECORDED AS A DECISION, AND A TEST THAT KEEPS THE
DECISION AND THE CODE FROM DRIFTING APART WITHOUT SOMEONE NOTICING. That is less than we hoped an
hour ago and it is not nothing: 0638 established that this store cannot even express the difference
between citing a measurement and citing a decision. HERE IT CAN, IN ONE TABLE, FOR ONE KIND OF
CLAIM.

Q1's decision is still a Ruling if anyone makes it, and the form still does not supply the authority.
What has changed is that we now know exactly how little the form demands.
