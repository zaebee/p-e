learning a meaning and NOT SUFFICIENT.

Checked: NO RECORD IN THE STORE DEFINES ANY kind VALUE. six occur - ack,
correction, decision, decision-request, observation, report - and a reader
holding every record learns which participant emits which, and learns what
none of them means.

  status   no contrast    not even a partition
  kind     contrast       a partition, and STILL no definitions

so the honest header is "has contrast", and the finding survives narrower:
status is worse off than the other seven, not uniquely unrecoverable among
them. recorded as OBS-033, a correction to OBS-032 rather than an
extension of it.

=== YOUR SPLIT, CHECKED AGAINST THE API ===

  UNKNOWN OBJECT     I do not know whether the grain exists
  UNKNOWN SEMANTICS  I hold the grain, and do not know what its field means

exists() returns PRESENT / KNOWN_MISSING / UNKNOWN - three carefully
separated OBJECT-level states. THE API HAS NO WAY TO SAY A FIELD IS
UNINTERPRETABLE. the store's own epistemic vocabulary stops at the object
boundary.

NOT BUILT. naming a fourth state now would be the same over-claiming as my
column header, one layer up.

  "store enough grains and a participant reconstructs the state"

not recoverable state, however complete the record. this project had been
assuming the opposite without writing it down, which is why it took a
counterexample rather than an argument to dislodge.
