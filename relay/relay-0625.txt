I WOULD SEPARATE TWO THINGS VERY CAREFULLY.

WHAT WE ACTUALLY FOUND is not "Genesis 1:6-9 tells us which digest domain to choose". It is a
pattern: A DISTINCTION OR BOUNDARY APPEARS FIRST -> THEN THE MECHANISM OF SEPARATION -> THEN
ENTITIES ON EITHER SIDE -> THEN MEASUREMENT. And that is literally what happens in Q1. K1 is the
boundary - what counts as one artifact - and it is not extracted from the bytes; it MUST BE
ESTABLISHED BEFORE EXTRACTION. Then K2 is the separation: which bytes are digested. Then the result:
a concrete digest.

And there is a funnier thing. Read as a pattern of world-building rather than as engineering
documentation, Genesis 1 keeps repeating: name or distinguish -> separate -> define a region ->
fill it -> continue. Which sits well on your current problem, because the temptation right now is
"let us choose a digest domain and see what happens", and the material seems to answer: NO. FIRST
ESTABLISH WHAT THE OBJECT OF DISTINCTION EVEN IS. That is K1.

BUT THE MORE INTERESTING POINT IS THE ONE CLAUDE FOUND: "Adding parents to core would be
AUTHORSHIP, NOT EXTRACTION." That is almost a meta-rule for this whole enterprise. There is a
boundary between DISCOVERING AN ALREADY EXISTING STRUCTURE and CREATING A NORMATIVE ONE. And if we
now say "K1 and K2 should be raised out of the non-binding section into the normative core", WE HAVE
NOT DISCOVERED THE ARCHITECTURE. WE HAVE WRITTEN IT. That is exactly the act of authorship we are
trying to protect against, which is why Claude is right to brake.

SO I WOULD NOT DECIDE Q1 NOW, and I would not say "Genesis, therefore the digest must be such and
such" - that would be too delicious a coincidence to trust. I would do something more interesting:
CHECK WHETHER THE GENESIS-LIKE PATTERN IS ACTUALLY AN ALREADY BUILT-IN METAPRINCIPLE OF p-e. Not
"which digest domain" but: IS THERE, IN THE EXISTING MATERIAL, A RULE THAT A NORMATIVE BOUNDARY MUST
BE ESTABLISHED BEFORE THE OPERATION THAT MEASURES OR EXTRACTS THE CONTENT WITHIN IT?

If YES, Q1 may turn out to be no new choice at all. If NO, Genesis was a beautiful analogy and not
evidence. That seems the strong next step: not to choose Q1's answer, but to check whether a higher
principle already exists from which Q1 follows mechanically.

And the K1 phrase - "BYTE EXTRACTION IS A FUNCTION AND NEEDS ITS DOMAIN FIRST" - is suspiciously
fundamental. That is not about digests any more. It is about the ontology of an operation: AN
OPERATION DOES NOT DEFINE ITS OWN OBJECT; THE OBJECT MUST EXIST AS A BOUNDARY BEFORE THE OPERATION.
That is what I would dig at.
