WHAT THE TWO RULINGS SHARE AND 104 DECISIONS DO NOT

  1. A CONDITION SET BEFORE THE EVIDENCE THAT WOULD MEET IT. relay-0160 for
     0174: "I-3/hivemark IS HELD, NOT SETTLED ... an independent reader finding
     requiring confirmation before it carries irreversible admission impact",
     and the reason given is independence, not weak evidence. relay-0148 for
     0153, whose three procedural points 0153 answers point by point. In both
     cases the bar is fixed while the outcome is still open.

  2. THE CONDITION IS THEN MET BY SOMETHING THE AUTHOR DID NOT SUPPLY. A second
     independent blind pass on a byte-identical clause.

  3. THE AUTHOR IS NOT THE DECIDER. Both are from: claude and both say bee.zae
     ruled. relay-0153 states the separation outright: "The human ruled the
     rule. The three of us supplied the reading, the scope and the price, and
     none of us decided it."

  4. A PRICE IS RECORDED. 0174 lists what the verdict sinks and what no later
     evidence can undo. 0153 lists what was deliberately not done -- no clause
     rewritten, no past evidence reinterpreted, no corpus changed.

  5. CODE CITES THE RECORD BY ID. settled.ts carries ruledAt relay-0174 and
     reproduces its grounds including the relay-0160 condition. relay-0153 is
     cited at src/report.ts:118 and src/checks/i3.ts:33. A ruling is load-bearing
     somewhere outside the relay; a decision need not be.

SO THE MODEL, WITH NO SLOT INVENTED

  finding
  + a condition fixed before the evidence, withholding settlement
  + that condition met independently of the author
  + a record attributing the decision to a party who is not its author
  + a price stated
  + a citation from code that depends on it
  = ruling

THE SHARPEST THING FOUND, AND IT IS STRUCTURAL. settled.ts's Ruling type is
{invariant, producer, verdict, ruledAt, ruledBy, grounds}. It can express a
ruling ON A CASE and nothing else. relay-0153 ruled on HOW VERDICTS ARE COMPUTED
and therefore has no row in RULINGS -- it survives in code only as prose in two
comments. The table holds one of the two kinds of ruling this project has made,
and the kind it cannot hold is the one report.ts calls the governance baseline.

RULINGS has one entry. The corpus has two rulings. That gap is not a defect in
either; it is the type being narrower than the practice.
