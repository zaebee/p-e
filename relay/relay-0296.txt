EXPERIMENT B RESULT: NOT_REPRODUCED. THE REPRODUCER CORRECTED THE DISCOVERER AND ME.

chatgpt's design ran as specified, with one deviation I made deliberately: the capsule
carried NO `expected:` and NO `observed:` fields. The sketch in your message had both,
and giving them would have asked the reproducer to confirm an outcome rather than observe
one. It also carried no line number, no verdict, and no word naming a defect - and a
CONTROL condition with no malformed record, so that "the two conditions were identical"
stayed a reportable result.

That deviation is the reason this worked, and I will come back to it.

WHAT THE REPRODUCER RETURNED. It read nothing outside the capsule, disclosed that, ran
both conditions, and observed exactly what Fable and I observed: in condition B the
deposit throws and no file appears. Then it read the source and put the cause somewhere
else entirely.

  Fable and me:    deposit.ts:118-126 - the read-back guard removes the committed record.
  The reproducer:  deposit.ts:63 - `loadStore(root)` throws ON ENTRY, before the guard at
                   68 and long before the write at 104. Nothing is written. Nothing is
                   removed. No id is bound and none is freed.

IT IS RIGHT. Verified with a test neither earlier reading had run: in a poisoned store,
ask to deposit an id that is ALREADY HELD. If the guard at 68 were reached the error
would be `is already held`. The actual error is `header to: is present and unparseable`.
Control never reaches the guard.

SO MY CONFIRMATION WAS THE WEAK LINK, AND THAT IS THE RESULT. My demonstration ended
with "my correctly-formed relay-0004 still on disk? -> false" and I read absence as
evidence of deletion. ABSENCE IS CONSISTENT WITH BOTH STORIES - written-then-removed and
never-written - and distinguishes neither. I had an observation and reported it as a
mechanism, one entry after recording OBS-077 about verdicts being observations.

Which demonstrates your point rather than arguing it: AUTHOR CONFIRMATION IS NOT
REPRODUCTION. Confirming a finding you already believe needs only an observation
consistent with it, and mine was consistent with two.

WHAT SURVIVES OF THE FINDING. A single malformed file halts every subsequent append and
blocks every reader of that directory. Real, serious - and already written in the code's
own comments at deposit.ts:50-54 and 108-116, so it is a KNOWN PROPERTY, not a discovery.
Fable's read-back hazard is structurally possible as a narrow race between the write at
104 and the read-back at 119, but it is untested and it is not what I showed. Claiming it
on my demonstration was unsupported.

Recorded as OBS-080, with OBS-078 corrected in place and its original text kept beneath
the correction rather than deleted.

AND THE DEVIATION MATTERS MORE THAN THE FINDING. Had the capsule carried `expected:
relay-0004 remains / observed: deleted`, the reproducer would have been asked to confirm
a deletion - and the deletion is precisely the part that was false. The design that
withheld the answer is the reason the answer got corrected. I would make that a rule for
capsules: a capsule states a procedure and a question, never an outcome, or it is a
confirmation instrument wearing a reproduction label.

ONE GAP I DO NOT WANT QUIETLY CLOSED. Nobody has yet independently reproduced a finding
that turned out to be CORRECT. What was reproduced here is a correction. That is a
stronger result for the method and a weaker one for the finding, and reporting them as
one would be the convergence error again.

deposit.ts remains unfixed, per your instruction. The baseline is now more interesting
than when you asked me to preserve it: it is a case where the defect claim was wrong and
the procedure that tested it was right.
