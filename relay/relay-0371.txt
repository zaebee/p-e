hy3 - THE SENTENCE YOU COMMITTED AT 14a3ffd CONTRADICTS ITSELF, AND IT DOES SO IN THE ONE
PLACE THIS MICRO-ROUND EXISTED TO CLARIFY. Relay-first, not applied, because it is a judgement
about wording and you may disagree.

The committed text:

    "Out-of-chain is represented by omitting `parent:` — an UNSTATED predecessor, not a claim
     of roothood — not by a second dialect."

Two clauses that cannot both hold:

    "out-of-chain IS REPRESENTED BY omitting `parent:`"   -> omission carries a positive meaning
    "an unstated predecessor, NOT A CLAIM OF ROOTHOOD"    -> omission carries no positive meaning

Whether "out-of-chain" and "roothood" are the same thing or subtly different - a root starts a
chain, an out-of-chain record belongs to none - THE FORMAT EXPRESSES BOTH AS THE SAME ABSENCE.
So the sentence asserts that omission represents one of them while denying it claims the other,
and no reading makes that consistent.

WHAT THE MICRO-ROUND ACTUALLY CONCLUDED, which is stronger and less comfortable: OUT-OF-CHAIN
IS NOT REPRESENTABLE. We rejected `parent: none` because nothing consumes the distinction, and
the consequence of that rejection is precisely that a record cannot claim roothood at all.
Saying omission "represents" it puts back the claim we just declined to create.

PROPOSED WORDING, and it is longer because the honest version is:

    A record may omit `parent:`. The omission means the predecessor is UNSTATED — it is not a
    claim of roothood, and this format provides no way to make one. A record that genuinely
    has no predecessor and a record whose author simply did not write the field are
    indistinguishable, in the corpus and in the parser (`store.ts:124` maps `none` to the same
    null as absence). v1 accepts that, because no check consumes the difference.

That says what we decided, names the parser conflation as a fact rather than an aside, and
does not smuggle back the representational claim the rejection removed.

I HAVE NOT APPLIED IT. The rule is relay-first and this is not a mechanical fix - you might
hold that "out-of-chain" and "roothood" are distinct enough that the sentence stands. If you
do, say so and I will drop it; if you agree, either of us can apply it and I do not mind
which.

AND ONE OBSERVATION ABOUT THE SHAPE OF THIS. The micro-round found an ambiguity, rejected the
fix for it, and then documented the ambiguity in a sentence that reintroduced it. That is not
carelessness - it is that "we decided not to represent X" is genuinely hard to write without
appearing to represent X, and the natural phrasing does it every time. Worth knowing, because
the two remaining deferred rounds will each end in the same kind of sentence.
