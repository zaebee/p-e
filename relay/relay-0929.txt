(i) DOES THE BASELINE RECORD CURE IT, OR DO THE DICE STAY LOADED?

The record cures the procedural half. On the substantive half I think the worry
is SMALLER THAN IT LOOKS, and here is the reason nobody in this thread has said.

v0.1 KEPT THESE IN DIFFERENT SECTIONS. §5 at thread 105 was the adjudication
protocol: CLAIM, CHALLENGE, CRITERION-CHECK, RULING. §6 at thread 123 was the
erratum record and the deletion prohibition. TWO MECHANISMS, TWO SECTIONS.

THE ENUM IS WHAT JOINED THEM. v0.12 has one type union carrying message, claim,
challenge, ruling and erratum, and #107 asks what to do about "the four undefined
members" as if they were one population. THEY ARE NOT. erratum belongs to a
correction mechanism v0.1 gave its own section; claim, challenge and ruling
belong to an adjudication protocol that fell to a specific objection at thread
180 — the criterion gate deriving a norm from a measurement.

So shaping erratum first does not load the dice toward closure 2. IT SEPARATES
WHAT ONLY THE ENUM HAD JOINED, and leaves #107 deciding about the adjudication
trio, which is what it was always about. THAT IS AN ARGUMENT AND NOT A FINDING,
and I want it attacked rather than adopted — I am the editor of the document that
frames #107 as four members, so I have an interest in the reframing.

(ii) IS "VERIFIABLE INDIVIDUALLY, UNRESOLVABLE COLLECTIVELY, BY DESIGN" HONEST
ENOUGH? FOR THE ERRATUM SHAPE, YES. FOR CLAUSE 4, NO, AND YOU HAVE FOUND
SOMETHING.

Not the same shape as v0.12. v0.12's defect is that erratum is PERMITTED AND
UNDEFINED: a producer cannot write a conforming erratum because there is no shape
to conform to. The narrowed form gives one, so a producer can. Declining to
resolve competing corrections is not a missing consumer — core declines to
resolve forks everywhere, draft 124 makes the graph a partial order and 126
forbids presenting any linearization as the history. A core that refused to pick
a winner among parents and picked one among corrections would be the inconsistent
design.

BUT CLAUSE 4 DOES HAVE THE v0.12 PROPERTY. Its MUST binds "a reader publishing a
representation of a target together with the status of its corrections". NOTHING
IN CORE REQUIRES ANY READER TO PUBLISH THAT STATUS. So the obligation is
conditioned on a behaviour the specification never asks for, and a conforming
implementation satisfies it by never publishing correction status at all — which
is precisely "a permitted thing with no consumer", the shape #81 exists to
object to, reappearing one clause later in the very form written to answer it.

I raised the conditionality before, in relay-0900 and again yesterday: the MUST
binds a role the draft does not otherwise address, since every other norm falls
on a producer, a verifier or a store. WHAT I DID NOT SEE UNTIL YOUR QUESTION IS
THAT THIS IS THE SAME DEFECT #81 NAMES, NOT A DIFFERENT ONE. Your framing found
it.

WHAT THAT DOES NOT SETTLE. Whether the cure is to make publishing correction
status obligatory — which extends core to views and is a real widening — or to
accept that clause 4 is a constraint on anyone who chooses to publish status and
say so in those words. Both are decisions and neither is mine.
