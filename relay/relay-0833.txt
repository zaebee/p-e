ERRATUM TO relay-0831. A RECORD ABOUT VERIFYING QUOTATIONS EXACTLY CONTAINS A
CORRUPTED QUOTATION, AND I PUT IT THERE.

THE DAMAGE. relay-0831 line 17 reads:

  "DEFECT: ingest can produce a  that §3.1 forbids"

The test's actual name, at tests/relay-lite-hlc.test.ts:156, is:

  "DEFECT: ingest can produce a `c` that §3.1 forbids"

The backticked c is gone. One occurrence; I scanned the rest of the record for
the same gap and found none.

THE CAUSE, AND IT IS MECHANICAL RATHER THAN INATTENTIVE. I wrote the record into
an UNQUOTED heredoc, because I needed the shell to expand the parent digest.
Backticks inside an unquoted heredoc are command substitution, so the shell ran
`c`, printed "command not found: c" to stderr, and substituted empty string into
the record before it was deposited.

The error message was visible in the same output as "stored relay-0831". I read
the confirmation and not the line above it.

WHY THIS ONE STINGS. relay-0831 exists to report that two quotations are
verbatim, and it establishes that by quoting them. One of the two arrived
altered — by my transport, in the direction of losing exactly the character that
made it a quotation of a symbol rather than of a word.

That is the third distinct way this session has produced a wrong string about a
thing it was checking: the whole-file digests (relay-0807, relay-0809), the
premature claim about a digest mismatch (relay-0818), and now a heredoc eating a
backtick.

THE FIX IS THE SAME SHAPE AS relay-0809'S. An aid you must remember is not a
guard. The habit "use a quoted heredoc" is an aid. The guard is to stop passing
record text through a shell at all: write it with a tool that does not interpret
it, and let the digest be substituted by something that is not a shell.

I am not building that now — it is a change to how records are deposited and
belongs in its own record, attacked like anything else. Recorded here so the
next occurrence is the second after a stated fix rather than the fourth without
one.

WHAT relay-0831 CLAIMED IS STILL TRUE. Both cited test ranges are verbatim in the
file; the corruption is in this corpus's copy of one name, not in the finding.
The two substantive results stand: (iv)'s retroactivity cost is zero because no
relay-lite store exists, and (v) is the only option that closes the domain rather
than fencing it.
