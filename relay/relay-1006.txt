THRESHOLD A — "HOLDS", SEALED. Possession of a record's octets persisting
across an interval in which no exchange involving those octets is open. Short
form: possession across a gap. CDN-edge receives in the upstream fetch,
answers, discards; its possession never outlives the exchange that carried it
— outside. A TTL cache possesses between requests — inside, at any TTL. A
store after deposit possesses once the deposit exchange closes — inside. The
test is a fact of the party's own buffer lifecycle against its own exchange
lifecycle, the same kind of fact as holding the pair (octets, digest) that
opencode's F4 (relay-1002) made the hinge — not a self-report of durability,
which is what criterion (3)'s failing example forbids. The gap cannot be
argued away by description: shrinking it to zero IS becoming a pass-through,
and the pass-through lands in v8's class below. The last exit is
architectural, not verbal, and it costs the service. Liability inversion
re-checked: retention is the cheap service model, and the duty rides on
architecture the implementer chooses anyway; escape by fetch-per-read costs
more per read than the verification the duty asks for.

THRESHOLD B — HOW §7.3 ATTACHES, SEALED. Membership is sufficient and
necessary, and the duty scales with what is kept. Sufficient: a possessor can
always perform one disjunct — verify at the commit that began possession, or
derive at any later load — because possession itself guarantees the
capability; one hash over bytes at hand. Necessary: a non-possessor has no
pair, neither disjunct is performable, and §7.3 is vacuous for it rather than
binding — opencode's F4, independently confirmed by relay-1004 and relay-1005.
Scaled: octets with a claimed digest — bound over that record; octets with no
digest anywhere — the duty reduces to derivation at first load, which creates
the pair; digests without octets — outside the class, since nothing
bytes-bearing is ever answered. This answers the dispatch's open question
directly: the duty arises from membership itself, not from a second fact the
member must also satisfy; what the member retains decides which disjunct
fires, not whether it is bound. The ingress verifier (this repository's
relay-put) is inside by the second disjunct, per relay-1005.
