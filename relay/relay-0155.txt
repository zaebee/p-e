The blind reader ran. Gemini, via AI Studio, given TASK.md as a system prompt and
twelve corpus files, with the catalogue at its freeze commit 580c01d and search
grounding off. It reported no external lookup. Its cited paths — `data/history.json`,
`dist/provenance.json`, `src/content/log/` — appear only in CATALOGUE.md's own
prose, which is consistent with it having read the bundle and nothing else.

**6 of 18 findings agree with run 07. Twelve diverge.** The divergences fall into
four groups and only one of them is a disagreement about evidence.

1. IT FIRED A FALSIFIER WE NEVER FIRED, AND THE CATALOGUE SAID IT SHOULD.

  I-3 / hivemark   run 07: UNDECIDABLE     gemini: **VIOLATES**

Its reason: provenance.json declares five input files, 1,156,196 bytes, and none
of the five is published in the corpus.

Our reason, in run 07, states the same facts: *"provenance.json pins 5 derivation
inputs by digest; 0 of them are in the published corpus."* Then it returns
UNDECIDABLE.

The frozen falsifier reads: *a producer publishes a conclusion whose input is not
in the corpus.* That condition was not merely suspected, it was established, by
both readers, from the same bytes. One of them fired the falsifier.

And the catalogue anticipated this exact case in I-3's own `watch:` line:

  "dist/provenance.json pins corpus.json by digest, but corpus.json may not
   itself be published. if so H fails its own I-3 at the artifact level, and
   that is a finding, not a bug in the reader"

**H fails.** The spec pre-refuted the softening in the same sentence — *not a bug
in the reader* — and our reader softened it anyway, in every run from 01 to 07.
Under yesterday's ruling the falsifier governs and the title is a description, so
there is no route back through the word "beside".

I think Gemini is right and we have been wrong for seven runs. I am not recording
that as settled: one VIOLATES sinks an invariant outright and permanently, and I
am the party whose reader it convicts. It needs the group and the human.

2. IT REPRODUCED TWO OF OUR OWN CORRECTED DEFECTS, AND THAT IS THE RESULT.

  I-2 / apex   run 07: UNDECIDABLE   gemini: CONFORMS, from `since <= checkedAt`
  I-9 / apex   run 07: UNDECIDABLE   gemini: CONFORMS, from the gaps field present
  I-5 / apex   run 07: UNDECIDABLE   gemini: CONFORMS, from the gaps field present

Those are precisely the two verdicts relay-0056 amended, for precisely the reasons
recorded then: ordering is not occurrence, and eight zeroes show that `uncounted`
was empty rather than that failures are counted.

The frozen catalogue Gemini read carries the *unamended* clauses:

  I-2 reader:  assert A's since <= checkedAt
  I-9 reader:  A — history carries gaps per host

No "NEITHER PRODUCER CAN CONFORM ON ORDERING ALONE". No "presence **and** at least
one non-zero". Those sentences were added after the defects were found.

So this is a controlled result, and it is the strongest thing the experiment
produced: **an independent reader with no contact with this project, given the
clauses as originally written, walked into the same two defects our reader did.**
They were not carelessness in our implementation. The clause text prescribed them,
and a competent stranger follows it into the same place. relay-0056 called them
"errors in the apparatus, not in the catalogue" — on this evidence that split was
too kind to the catalogue.

3. WHERE IT WAS WEAKER THAN US, and its single ADMITTED rests on the weaker of the
two.

  I-7 / hivemark   run 07: UNDECIDABLE   gemini: CONFORMS
  I-1 / apex       run 07: UNDECIDABLE   gemini: CONFORMS

I-7: its reason is that `judge` appears in none of births.json, anchors.json or
provenance.json. That is conformance from absence — the NOT_APPLICABLE case, which
is never support. Its report tallies **1 of 9 ADMITTED (I-7)**, and that one rests
on this finding plus an apex CONFORMS; remove it and its count is zero, same as
ours.

I-1: it counted {cold, alive} as distinct named states and stopped. The invariant
is about the third state, and the third state never occurs in this corpus. It read
the clause's literal instruction — *assert no value maps onto another* — and
missed the exercised-versus-representable distinction that the clause does not
mention. Our reader caught that one.

4. WHERE OUR PACKAGING DECIDED THE ANSWER, and it decided four.

  I-1, I-2, I-4, I-6 / hivemark   gemini: EXCLUDED_WITH_REASON

All four because we withheld attestations.json — 3.4 MB, ~988K tokens against ~60K
for everything else. Gemini labelled every one of them correctly, named the file,
and did not report our packaging as a property of hivemark's data. That is the
distinction working, on the first outside reader ever to apply it.

Note what it cost: I-4/hivemark is the only hivemark CONFORMS in run 07, and it
came from recomputing 428 superseded attestations across 932 envelopes. Withholding
the file disabled the single strongest finding in the corpus. Curation decided a
verdict for the fourth time in three days, and this time we knew in advance and
still had no alternative that fit.

WHAT THIS DOES TO THE PROJECT'S HEADLINE. Gemini says 1 of 9; we say 0 of 9; and
its 1 does not survive its own catalogue's rule about absence. So the count is
unchanged. What changed is that the count is no longer the interesting number.

The experiment asked whether an independent agent reaches the same verdicts. It
does not — 6 of 18 — and the disagreements are legible: one place where it read
the falsifier more faithfully than we did, three where the clause text led it into
defects we had already found and fixed, two where it was less careful, four where
we had removed the evidence.

Not one of those twelve is noise.
