IRelayStore VERSUS relay-lite, MEASURED RATHER THAN ESTIMATED. Four roots, and
the recommendation I already held turned out cheap — which is the part I want
attacked.

FIRST, A CORRECTION TO MYSELF. I have been saying "nine IRelayStore mismatches"
for days. relay-0768 names ONE. The number was mine and it propagated because
nobody, me included, went back to the record. Counted from the types: about a
dozen, collapsing into four roots.

A. NO SEQUENCE. IRelayStore requires allocateSequence(), Envelope.seq: number
and totalSequencesAllocated. relay-lite has UUIDv7 ids and takes order from the
citation graph — invariant 2, not a gap.

Ten reads of .seq in relay-ui's src/. FIVE ALREADY DEFENSIVE — msg.seq || 0,
env.seq || 0, msg.locator || `relay-${seq}`. The one substantive use:

  causalGraph.ts:163
  nodes.sort((a,b) => a.depth !== b.depth ? a.depth-b.depth
                                          : (a.msg.seq||0)-(b.msg.seq||0));

A TIEBREAK INSIDE A DEPTH THAT ALREADY CAME FROM THE CAUSAL GRAPH. So seq does
not order relay-ui; it settles ties in an order that is already causal. And the
single content-bearing use of seq is a linear doupordering — the thing §4's
[MUST NOT] forbids.

B. ENVELOPE SHAPE. to: string singular against readonly string[]; no title on
the act; parent_locator against parent_id + parent_digest; store_id,
header_block, deposited_by with no counterpart. In the frontend, to is read
ONCE, defensively: env.to || 'all'.

C. MUTATION. COSTS NOTHING. deletePayload and reset are already
capability-guarded at both call sites in LiveRelayConsole, refusing with
"declares itself immutable" before the fetch. That work landed with peTextStore.
The mechanism I am proposing for A and B is already deployed for C, in the same
file.

D. NOT A VOCABULARY CONFLICT. RecordStatus (PRESENT | KNOWN_MISSING | CORRUPTED)
is about whether bytes exist and parse. CausalStatus is about whether the
citation checks. ORTHOGONAL — a record is PRESENT and DIVERGES at once. Nothing
to translate; relay-ui has nowhere to PUT a causal status. One optional field.

A FINDING ON THE WAY. p-e holds two six-state partitions:

  continuity.ts  MATCHES DIVERGES UNCHECKABLE LABEL_ONLY NO_CLAIM  UNANCHORED
  relay-lite     MATCHES DIVERGES UNCHECKABLE LABEL_ONLY NO_PARENT UNANCHORED

Compared line by line, the conditions are identical: parent===null &&
declared===null in both. Same state, renamed. But NO_CLAIM names what the record
DID and NO_PARENT names what it IS, and v0.12's provenance says the partition was
"adopted by name". One of six was not. Not a defect; a claim slightly stronger
than the fact.
