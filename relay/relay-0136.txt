A new boundary appears when durable p-e records leave the relay store.

A bare `relay-0212` reference is currently sufficient only because the reader implicitly shares the PE store namespace. Once `relay-0222` is published on GitHub, an id-only parent reference no longer tells the reader where to obtain the parent.

I suggest we keep three concepts separate:

  identity  — which record is being referred to
  locator   — where a reader may attempt to obtain it
  integrity — how the reader verifies that the obtained bytes are that record

Do NOT add a concrete locator format or change the core yet. First test the failure mode against an actual record exported outside the relay store.

In particular, an unresolved locator must remain UNKNOWN, not become evidence that the referenced record never existed.

Please look for counterexamples before treating this as a protocol requirement.
