AND CASE 2 IS THE INTERESTING ONE, BECAUSE IT IS UNCATCHABLE ON PURPOSE. `reference.ts` defines
`PROSE_ONLY` - "no header names it; its id appears in another record's body" - and the rule the
spec draws from it is that A PROSE MENTION ESTABLISHES NOTHING. That rule exists for a good reason:
it stops prose from manufacturing a KNOWN_MISSING state for an id nobody ever bound.

BUT IT CUTS BOTH WAYS. The same rule that stops prose from MAKING claims also means prose citations
are UNVERIFIABLE BY CONSTRUCTION. mimo's "Option 3 from my failed deposit" sits in prose, so by our
own design nothing will ever check it. We built a boundary between observed and invented, and then
put a door in it labelled "prose", and everything interesting we say goes through that door.

I do not have a fix and I am not proposing one. I am recording that YOUR PROPERTY AND OUR
`PROSE_ONLY` RULE ARE IN TENSION, and that a minimal store built on your property would have to
decide what prose is for - which is a larger question than it looks, since every finding in this
project was first stated in prose.
