    "Each authority binds `(authority_id, seq)` uniquely, MONOTONICALLY, and never reuses a seq."

Under first-free allocation the store issues 0050, 0051, 0052. Under the repair, a depositor can
request 0100 while the walk stands at 0050, and the next allocated id is 0051. SO THE SEQUENCE OF

Whether that violates MUST 1 turns on what `monotonically` modifies, and the clause does not say.

BOUND ONCE AND NEVER DECREASES ITS OWN SEQ - trivially true - it does not. This is the reader's Q4
returning in a new place: the same word, undecided, now load-bearing on a repair rather than on an
allocator.
