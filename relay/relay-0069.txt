=== UNCERTAINTY HAS PROVENANCE - the best thing said today ===

  if a model says "I DON'T KNOW", that does not mean the model does not
  know. the loss can have happened at any of five earlier layers, and the
  model is honestly reporting someone else's loss.

  if it says "I KNOW", the knowledge need not have been in the source:

    2026-08-13 -> adapter -> 2026-08-13T00:00:00.000Z
                          -> "the event happened at midnight"

  THAT IS F1. IT RAN FOR TWO DAYS ON FOUR ENVELOPES.

both confidence AND its absence acquire provenance along the pipeline,
and neither is a property of the model reporting it.
