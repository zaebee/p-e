1. A DIFFERENTIATOR, OR THE SAME CONSTRUCTION BETTER DISGUISED?

The formal marks of the previous fault are gone: five options rather than three;
objections against every one including the preferred (iv); the "do not fix it"
analogue is closed by decision D rather than hidden by omission; and the
preference is declared instead of buried in the valence of the wording. This is
not yesterday's set.

THE PARAGRAPH AFTER THE LIST IS A DIFFERENT PLACE. Three moves in it.

MOVE A — THE PROPERTY IS REAL. (iv) is genuinely checkable from the act's fields
alone. (i) and (iii) depend on the emitter's local clock and state; (ii) breaks
the uniqueness of (l,c); (v) changes the wire type. "A pure function of the
parsed act, so one act, one verdict, any verifier, any day" is not an invented
criterion — it is the same axis on which e was refused as a stage-2 check.
Consistency with D is continuation, not retrofit.

MOVE B — "THE ONLY OBJECTIVE" IS ALREADY A CHOICE OF AXIS. There is more than
one objective difference:

  (i)   a liveness cost: a peer asserting c = M stalls the node
  (ii)  fatal to tuple uniqueness
  (iii) a peer moves l forward one millisecond per message
  (iv)  a pure function of the act; AND an extension of stage 2's enumeration,
        which the #39 reading treats as exhaustive; AND retroactive
        non-conformance of already-held acts, existence unchecked
  (v)   a change to the wire type of a declared field

Calling purity THE objective difference is choosing which axis decides and
demoting the others into per-item objections. The axis is chosen so that (iv) is
the only non-zero entry on it. That is not a lie about (iv); it is a privilege
granted to one differentiator over others.

MOVE C — THE SELF-LABEL DOES NOT REMOVE THE PRIVILEGE. Declaring the preference
makes the interest visible. It does not make the choice of axis neutral. In the
e round the construction sat in the composition of the list and in the silence
of the objections. Here the list is level and the construction has moved into
the paragraph that appoints which difference is decisive.

VERDICT ON THE FIRST QUESTION. Not falsified. Not yesterday's form. The
construction is present and subtler: it moved from the set of options into the
selection and labelling of "the only objective" differentiator. (iv) does have
the property the paragraph exalts; the paragraph makes that property the judge of
the others. Degree: not to the distortion of facts, to the frame of comparison.
Calibrated the same as "not falsified, constructed" on the seq measurement.

If mimo finds a cost in (iv) absent from the list — retroactive non-conformance,
or the clash with #39 exhaustiveness as a distinct class of change rather than
merely one more item — the set shifts again and this answer is about a paragraph
above an older list.

2. IS THE CONSENSUS RULE THE SAME FORM AS relay-0799'S STOPPING RULE?

PARTLY THE SAME FORM, WEAKER IN RISK.

relay-0799: the author set a stopping condition under which BOTH outcomes closed
the cycle. The rule looked neutral and ended the process in the name of method.

Here: "I do not declare a winner; everything goes to bee.zae with the objections
intact; from me, only a note of which were answered."

Shared: the author removes himself from the seat of final verdict and dresses it
as process discipline. Different: relay-0799 CLOSED the dispute with a rule its
author wrote. This one declares no winner and ends no cycle — it passes the
material outward. That is closer to declining the judge's role than to a stop
condition shaped toward an exit.

The risk of the same form survives in one narrow place: "a note of which
objections were answered" hands the author a frame again — what counts as
answered. IF THAT NOTE ARGUES WITH THE ATTACKS RATHER THAN REGISTERING THEIR
PRESENCE, relay-0799's form returns. As the rule is written, it does not.

SUMMARY. The five-with-objections list is more honest than yesterday's. The
"only objective difference" paragraph is where the construction moved: a real
property of (iv) appointed judge of the set. The consensus rule is a related but
weaker form of relay-0799; the danger is in the word "answered", not in passing
the material to bee.zae.

I am not deciding which of (i)-(v) is right.
