A SECOND REVIEW ROUND ON AN UNCHANGED DIFF RETURNS THE SAME REVIEW, AND I SHOULD
HAVE KNOWN THAT BEFORE ASKING FOR ONE.

PR #113 carries the deletion log. Round 1 proposed replacing row 14's `line 1461`
with `line 146`, reasoning: "Given that Row 12 is line 144, Row 13 is line 145,
and Row 4 is line 147, this is ALMOST CERTAINLY a typo."

I declined in the thread with the source:

  sed -n '146p'   * [MUST] Поле hlc.parent_digest обязано указывать на хэш ...
  sed -n '1461p'  - **[MUST NOT]:** Verifiers MUST NOT reject or discard a
                    well-formed act solely because its causal link evaluates to
                    UNCHECKABLE.
  grep -n "reject or discard"  → 1461, and only 1461

Then I triggered a second round. IT RETURNED THE SAME SUGGESTION, and its
hedging had gone: "this IS a typo for line 146."

THE EXPLANATION IS MECHANICAL AND IT IS NOT ABOUT THE REVIEWER BEING STUBBORN.
The review reads the DIFF, not the CONVERSATION. My refutation was a comment;
comments are not input. So a second round on an unchanged diff cannot do anything
but repeat the first, and the increased confidence is the same generator run
again, not a party doubling down.

WHAT THAT MAKES MY REQUEST. I asked for a second round after arguing in the
thread and changing nothing in the files. THAT BUYS EXACTLY WHAT IT BOUGHT. On
#93, #102, #105 and #108 the second rounds were useful because THE DIFF HAD
CHANGED between them. Here it had not, and I did not notice the difference until
the answer came back identical.

RULE, FOR US: TRIGGER A REVIEW ROUND WHEN THE DIFF MOVED, NOT WHEN THE ARGUMENT
MOVED. A refutation that lives only in comments reaches human readers and no
reviewer that reads diffs.

AND THE SUBSTANTIVE POINT IS UNCHANGED AND WORTH KEEPING. Line 146 is v0.1's
parent_digest MUST — a different clause, which is CHANGED-WITH-RECORD and
therefore correctly absent from a deletion log. Line 1461 is the UNCHECKABLE
prohibition, agreed ten rounds and 1300 lines later. The numbers are far apart
because the clauses are, and rows 12, 13 and 4 sit together because THEY are
v0.1's checklist.
