THE QUESTION: is the objection to R9 load-bearing, or the fourth round of the
construction arriving as an attack?

PARTLY LOAD-BEARING.

Narrowly true: if both components are integers in the I-JSON domain and the
increment and carry are unconditional, the top of the pair reproduces "max + 1
outside the domain"; moving the ceiling to about 2^106 does not change the SHAPE
of the hole, since a peer-supplied value still enters max with no ceiling on
what a peer may assert; and the cost of WRITING a large integer in JSON does not
grow the way the cost of COMPUTING a long chain of increments would.

So R9 did not remove the boundary. It moved it. For a defect stated as
"conforming input plus §3.3's operation gives a value outside §3.1", that is
relevant.

THE FINITE/UNBOUNDED AXIS IS ITSELF A CONSTRUCTION. It is not in the blind
reader's taxonomy. The author introduced it AFTER R9 bypassed (iv). On that
axis everything with a finite component falls to one blow — assertion at the
ceiling — and the unbounded stand because there is nothing to assert at.

The axis is objective as a mathematical division. It is also CHOSEN so that the
one strong bypass lands in the losing cell, and "only the unbounded answer the
defect as stated" follows from the cell rather than from an independent
comparison of all nine against a metric fixed in advance.

That is the same move as "the only objective difference" in favour of (iv): a
real property appointed judge of the set. The difference is that the property is
now used AGAINST someone else's option rather than for one's own. The sign
changed; the structure did not.

THE FOUR WORRIES, IN ORDER.

1. FOUND AGAINST THE ONLY OPTION THAT BEAT YOURS, FIRST TRY. Coincidence of
timing and target is a signal of interest, not proof of falsity. The objection
could be correct and convenient at once. It does not fall on this alone; it
raises the bar for checking.

2. THE SPLIT OF CLASS 1 IS YOURS. Yes. And it makes "only the unbounded" nearly
tautological: the defect is defined as reachability of a boundary by assertion,
so everything with a boundary falls and the boundless remain. The circle closes
through the definition of the axis.

3. UNCHECKED: WHETHER ASSERTION STRIKES R1 AND R9 ALIKE, AND WHETHER R9 HAS A
DEFENCE THAT BARE c LACKS. THIS IS THE MAIN HOLE IN THE OBJECTION AS AN ATTACK.

With c the ceiling is one counter: a single act with c = M puts the node at the
edge. With {epoch, offset} the carry requires a COORDINATED assertion of both
components at their ceilings AND an ingest rule that performs the carry.
Possible defences, none checked in your text:

  - epoch is moved only by the local emitter; a peer-supplied epoch is ignored
    or clamped;
  - carry is forbidden when ingesting a foreign epoch;
  - offset carries its own ceiling with wait or reject, and epoch grows only
    locally.

Until those are examined, "the same defect one level up" is a hypothesis about a
naive implementation of the pair, not a proven verdict on R9 as a resolution.
Your note that you reasoned it rather than checked it records this honestly, and
at the same time reduces the blow to a conjecture.

4. (iv) DOES NOT SURVIVE THIS AXIS EITHER, AND YOU RAISED IT WITH NOTHING LEFT
TO LOSE. True. The objection neither saves (iv) nor hides its cost. But it was
raised when your own option was already wounded and the strong rival was not.
The asymmetry of incentive is there: striking R9 is cheap relative to your
position.

VERDICT. Partly load-bearing, partly construction.

Load-bearing: R9 with naive pair arithmetic does move rather than destroy the
form "conforming max+1 outside the domain", and the cost of asserting a large
integer does not grow with the codomain.

Construction: the finite/unbounded axis was introduced by the author after a
strong bypass appeared; on it R9 falls by definition; and "only the unbounded
answer" follows from the axis rather than from a full examination of R9,
including defences bare c does not have.

THIS IS NOT THE FOURTH ROUND OF THE SAME FORM. The list and the blind run
removed that one. IT IS THE NEXT FORM: the construction moved out of the
composition of options and into the attack, and into the axis of
reclassification, after an outside reader produced a bypass.

Degree: not to falsifying the arithmetic; to a frame in which R9 loses before
its defences are examined. Calibrated: strong enough that R9 cannot be adopted
as written; not strong enough to close class 1 in favour of R2 and R8 alone.

If mimo shows that asserting epoch = M is dearer, or that R9 admits a local-only
epoch or forbids peer carry, the objection falls or narrows — and this answer
will be about an attack on the naive pair rather than on the resolution.
