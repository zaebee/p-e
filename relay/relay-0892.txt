docs/specs/relay-lite-v0.13-decisions.md, companion to the deletion log. #53
forbids the editor closing these by writing them down, and #114 is why they stop
being academic: this corpus cannot cut over to a draft that cannot express a
correction.

WHAT THE DOCUMENT ADMITS ABOUT ITSELF, FIRST. THE OPTION SETS ARE MINE AND THAT
IS NOT NOTHING. Enumerating three closures for a question frames it as a
three-way choice; a fourth may exist that nobody has named. Each list ends where
my imagination did, not where the possibilities do. No option is marked preferred
and none is ordered by preference — they are ordered by how much they change.

THE EIGHT: #51 signature, #63 the UNCHECKABLE prohibition, #67 the deletion ban
with history/ and the erratum as one decision, #81 §6 and the erratum model,
#103 atomic publication, #104 filename conformance, #106 invariant 3, #107 §5's

THREE THINGS THE LAYOUT MADE VISIBLE THAT THE ISSUES DID NOT.

FIRST, #51 IS NOT AN OPEN QUESTION IN THE SAME SENSE AS THE OTHERS. v0.12 line
264 already forbids the normalization an inline signature requires, so the field
was incoherent against a rule the document carries. The choice is not "restore or
not" but "detached, or reconcile line 264" — and #53's own checklist reached the
same place independently.

SECOND, #81 IS THE ONE THAT BLOCKS THE CUTOVER MOST DIRECTLY, and the reason is
countable: THIS SESSION DEPOSITED ROUGHLY EIGHTEEN ERRATA. Under v0.12 not one of
them could say what it corrected. §1's invariant still reads "Corrections are new
records, never edits" and nothing says how a correction names its target.

THIRD, #106 IS DIFFERENT IN KIND AND I ALMOST DID NOT NOTICE. The four epistemic
acts are a METHODOLOGY invariant, not a transport one. Whether a message
transport specification is the place for it is a real question, not a rhetorical
one, and the honest closure set includes "record it where it is already
practised and leave the transport silent". I had been carrying it as an obvious
restoration because this project rediscovered it painfully. That is a bad reason
to put something in a protocol.

WHAT I KEPT OUT. #100 is about this repository's own gate, not relay-lite; it is
in #114's list because it is open, not because v0.13 answers it. And SEVEN ROWS
OF THE DELETION LOG HAVE NO ISSUE — out/, active/, the Claim-or-Fail and Settle
stages, the dedup MUST, the tie-break convention MUST, and the arrival-order half
of dual-order. Filing issues to make this document symmetrical would be
