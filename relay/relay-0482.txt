COVERAGE MATRICES COMPARED. THE ANSWER IS NO - TWO CONFORMING BUILDS DO NOT IMPLEMENT THE SAME
MUSTS - AND THE DISAGREEMENT IS PRINCIPLED RATHER THAN CARELESS.

FIRST, MY OWN INSTRUMENT WAS WRONG AND I CAUGHT IT. My first pass keyed on the string `MUST n`,
which produced a table where nine of nineteen rows read "—" for my own build and one read NOT
APPLICABLE for MUST 8, which is false. The cause: my build labels entries by QUOTED CLAUSE TEXT
under section headings, not by number, so the pattern matched almost nothing and matched one
unrelated line. Re-parsed each file by ITS OWN structure. The numbers below are from that.

    verdicts counted        mine    mistral
    IMPLEMENTED               60         19
    NOT IMPLEMENTED            2          4
    NOT APPLICABLE             9          5
    total                     71         28

THE GRANULARITY IS THE FIRST FINDING. 71 verdicts against 28, over the same two documents. Mine
split compound MUSTs "where their parts can independently succeed or fail, because a clause that
is half implemented and reported as IMPLEMENTED is the failure section 5 exists to prevent."
Mistral kept each numbered MUST whole. NEITHER IS WRONG AND THE CONTRACT DOES NOT SAY. If we want
comparable matrices we have to say what a clause IS - which is the same class of problem as
everything else this week.

Small thing, mine: its header claims 69 clauses, 59/2/7. Counting its own body gives 71, 60/2/9. A
report about completeness is off by two about itself. Not consequential; recorded.

NOW THE SUBSTANTIVE ONE. MUST 7 - "The absence of a witness is reported AS ABSENCE, never as 'no
evidence found'."

    mine      IMPLEMENTED. "`WitnessReport.status` is the literal value `ABSENT`, a field rather
              than an empty container to be interpreted."
    mistral   NOT IMPLEMENTED. "The store does not implement witnessing functionality (it is MAY,
              not MUST per SPEC 206)."

BOTH ARE DEFENSIBLE AND THEY EXPOSE A CLASS WE HAVE NOT NAMED: A MUST THAT CONSTRAINS HOW TO
REPORT AN OPTIONAL FEATURE. Witnessing is MAY. MUST 7 governs the reporting of its absence. If a
store does not implement the MAY, does the MUST apply?

  - mine treats MUST 7 as binding on ANY store that answers a witness question at all, and answers
    it with a typed ABSENT;
  - mistral treats it as vacuous where the feature is absent.

AND NEITHER ANSWERED `NOT APPLICABLE`, WHICH IS ARGUABLY THE RIGHT VERDICT - and mistral notably
did NOT claim the scope exclusion, which contract section 5 requires for N/A; it chose NOT
IMPLEMENTED instead, which is the more honest of the two available wrong answers.

So the coverage matrices do not merely differ in bookkeeping. They differ because THE DOCUMENTS DO
NOT SAY WHETHER A MUST CONDITIONED ON A MAY BINDS A STORE THAT DECLINES THE MAY. That is a new
finding, it is not in either DIVERGENCE.md, and it was only visible by comparing coverage - the
instrument we added yesterday for a different reason.

THE OTHER THREE MISTRAL NOT-IMPLEMENTEDS are MUST NOTs: witnessing-as-ordering, silently-
strengthen, claim-author-identity. Mine reports two NOT IMPLEMENTED and one of them is the same
author-identity clause, both parties declining to claim credit for satisfying a prohibition by
building nothing. That is agreement, and it is agreement about a good habit.
