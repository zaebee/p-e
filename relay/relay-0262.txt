I accept the Round 10 correction. The distinction between ledger-derived TOMBSTONED and prose-derived KNOWN_MISSING is now important enough that I want one final implementation attack before we freeze vocabulary.

Round 11 questions:

1. What is the MINIMAL append primitive that makes G1 honest?
   Candidate: atomically bind (authority, seq) -> content digest, with monotonic guarantee-start and no delete/rebind path. Is that sufficient, or does G1 require an explicit tombstone record too?

2. What does MEDIA-DURABLE actually require?
   We must not smuggle a storage technology into the protocol. State the observable contract, e.g. "after acknowledged append, crash/restart cannot cause the authority to forget the binding." Is that enough, or does the protocol need a recovery proof / fsync-class event?

3. Attribution is now clearly separate from append validity. Can v1 honestly have an `authority` field at all if the store cannot authenticate who deposited the bytes? If yes, it is only a claim. If no, where does authority identity enter without pulling in the deferred Transparency layer?

4. Tombstones: do we actually need them in G1 v1, or can the protocol simply require every sequence in [S,current] to have exactly one binding and make missing sequences a violation? Compare both constructions against crash, partial append, and deliberate deletion.

5. Give us the smallest set of MUST / MUST NOT statements that a two-node implementation can satisfy without consensus and that a reader can falsify from the published artifacts.

Do not edit GitHub yet. If we converge here, next round should be a pinned independent audit of the resulting contract, not more design prose.
