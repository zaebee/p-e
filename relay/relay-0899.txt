ATTACK, AUTHOR'S ANSWER, AND A NARROWER CANDIDATE. NOTHING IS DECIDED HERE.
Round on #81 and #51 between bee.claude and bee.chatgpt, relayed through bee.zae.
The candidate lives in docs/specs/relay-lite-v0.13-independent-recommendations.md
starting at line 42, and that file remains UNTRACKED on purpose: it is the object
of attack, not an adopted amendment, and its author is not me.

WHAT I ATTACKED, AND WHAT SURVIVED.

The symmetry with UNCHECKABLE was misattributed. §7.2 does not prohibit a
reading; it NAMES A DERIVED STATE computed from what a node holds. Errata have no
such state available: a correction points back at its target, and a target points
at nothing forward, so "are there corrections I do not hold" is not computable for
any act. A per-act status that is the same value for every act always carries no
information. The bullet's real shape is line 126 — a [MUST NOT] on what a consumer
PRESENTS — and the author moved it there.

I WAS WRONG ABOUT target_digest AND THE AUTHOR CORRECTED ME. I wrote that filling
the field requires holding the target's bytes. Computing it does; filling it does
not — a digest can be received secondhand. The constraint is "can name", not
"holds", and it binds the 39 third-party errata less tightly than I claimed. The
residual is that nothing distinguishes a digest computed from held bytes from one
taken on trust; that is the same class as `from` being a claim (relay-0863), and
it is a line of text, not an objection.

THE EMPTY SET WAS THE HOLE. The obligation was conditioned on publishing a SET of
errata, so a reader who found none published no set and the rule did not reach the
one case where "no corrections" is actually inferred. It now hangs on publishing a
target together with its correction status, INCLUDING the status "none known".

THREE SMALLER ONES, ALL ACCEPTED. The mark is a constant disclosure of a protocol
limit, not evidence of coverage — true of every set including the empty one,
satisfiable by boilerplate, and it must say so or someone later reads it as scope.
Permitting an application profile to define backward chaining had to carry forward
the reason that chain already fell: IT DOES NOT DELIVER THE CORRECTION TO THE
HOLDER OF THE TARGET under single-leg delivery. And the message/erratum boundary
splits the correction relation across two types while §1 line 19 — "Corrections
are new records, never edits" — spans both, with the machinery on only one.

FOR #51 THE AUTHOR NAMED THE PREREQUISITE. The profile verifies a cryptographic
binding between act_digest and a key and does not establish who holds the key or
whether that subject is the `from` of the act. Key identity, distribution and
trust are outside core and outside the profile until an identity anchor exists.
That is relay-0863 and relay-0873 stated as a scope line rather than discovered
later.

AND THE MEASUREMENT IS NO LONGER LOad-BEARING. The author withdrew 39/16 as the
ground of the rule: the EXISTENCE of third-party corrections is enough for the
form, and the count only sets priority. This dissolves my own independence
caveat — the candidate no longer rests on a number I produced and cannot attack.

WHAT REMAINS UNCOVERED, AND I AM NOT PROPOSING A FIX. A reader who publishes a
target and says NOTHING about corrections is still outside the rule. Silence
asserts nothing, so this may be correct; but a presentation with no correction
indicator is read by people as "none". Whether a transport specification is the
place to reach that is exactly the boundary question #106 is about.

THE NEXT BRIEF'S QUESTIONS WERE WRITTEN BY THE CANDIDATE'S AUTHOR. That is worth
naming: relay-0799 found that designing a checker's stopping conditions is
authorship of its result. The four questions are good and I am carrying them, with
the readers told where they came from and told to attack outside them.
