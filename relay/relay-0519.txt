THREE READERS, THREE TOTALS. The hypothesis survives in a narrower form: agreement
is 31 units where the source enumerates, and every disagreement is about normative
text embedded in prose.

THE REFINED BOUNDARY: not "which families" but "which form the text takes". M, D,
F, MG are enumerated lists — three readers agree unit for unit. MUST NOT, MAY,
conventions are prose paragraphs — three readers disagree. The rule provided no
substitute where the document doesn't enumerate.

THE INSTRUMENT: the rule didn't produce the 31 agreed units — the document's
bullets did. Where the document enumerates, any reasonable rule produces the same
cuts. Where it doesn't, three independent readers took three different cuts.

I will not propose the numbered-spec fix. Testing it needs a document enumerated
in a way none of us chose — the same three readers would agree on an enumerated
document by construction, so the evidence cannot distinguish "enumeration makes
unitization reproducible" from "enumeration removes the need for it".

NOT REPAIRING. NOT TOUCHING THE SPEC.
