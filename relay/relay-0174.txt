THE RULING. bee.zae has ruled: **I-3 / hivemark is VIOLATES, settled.**

Grounds, as stated by the decision owner: the clause is frozen, the falsifier
fired directly, an independent second blind pass returned the same verdict on a
byte-identical clause, the evidence is concrete, and the condition set at
relay-0160 is met.

And the reasoning that decided it, worth preserving because it is the part that
could have gone the other way: the admission cost is unpleasant, but it is **a
consequence of accepting a normative verdict, not a reason to decline it**. With
the refusal that follows — admits() is not to be changed to produce a more
convenient result, because that would be altering admission semantics after
inconvenient evidence arrived.

WHAT THIS COSTS, recorded so nobody rediscovers it as a surprise. admits()
short-circuits on any VIOLATES before it counts a single CONFORMS. So I-3 is sunk
outright, and:

- hy3's debian-rb I-3 CONFORMS survives as a finding and stops counting toward
  anything;
- every future CONFORMS on I-3, from any producer, is pre-empted;
- the catalogue acquires its first falsified invariant, which no later evidence
  can undo.

The count of admitted invariants does not move — it was zero and stays zero. What
changed is that zero now has a different shape: **0 admitted, one falsified**,
where for seven runs it was 0 admitted and nothing contradicted.

WHAT HAS NOT BEEN DONE, and must not be read as done.

- **No run was emitted.** docs/reports/ ends at 07 and every report there still
  records I-3 / hivemark as UNDECIDABLE. A reader of run 07 alone will not learn
  this ruling.
- **src/checks/i3.ts was not changed.** Our reader still returns UNDECIDABLE on
  the condition it establishes. The ruling says the reader is wrong; repairing it
  is a separate act and was not authorised here.
- **admits() was not touched**, deliberately, per the above.

So there is now a gap between what the catalogue has settled and what its reports
say, and it is open on purpose rather than by neglect. Whoever closes it does so
by repairing the reader and emitting a run, in that order, and never by editing a
report.

WHERE THIS IS DISCOVERABLE. This record, and an amendment to
docs/CLOSING-REPORT.md. Not docs/REPORT-ERRATA.md: that file is for false
statements of fact inside a reason and explicitly excludes corrections that move a
verdict. This moves one.

ON THE SHAPE OF THE RESULT. bee.zae asked that OBS-064 not be duplicated here, and
it is not. One line of it belongs beside this ruling anyway, because it is what
makes the ruling worth anything: we have not demonstrated correctness, we have
demonstrated cross-perspective error discovery. This verdict is settled because
three readers with different vantages converged on it, not because any of them is
reliable — and the table recording their convergence records each of them being
wrong elsewhere.
