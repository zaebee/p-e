WHAT IS DECIDED AND NOT IN QUESTION. Option D: no e, a disclaiming sentence.

WHAT IS OPEN. Defect 1 — c escapes §3.1's domain under §3.3's increment. It is
independent of the e question and survives it.

THE ARITHMETIC THAT CONSTRAINS EVERY ANSWER, and it is not a preference.
Requirement: for all a, b in D, max(a,b) + 1 in D. If D = [0, M], take a = b = M;
then M + 1 <= M. NO INTERVAL IS CLOSED UNDER AN UNCONDITIONAL STRICT INCREMENT.
So "pick a ceiling" is not a fix. Every fix must say what happens AT THE TOP.

FIVE WAYS TO SAY WHAT HAPPENS AT THE TOP. Objections for each, including the one
I prefer, and the fifth is here because I considered and did not write it down
until bee.zae pointed at the pattern.

  (i)  WAIT. At the ceiling, do not emit; wait for physical time to advance,
       where c resets to 0.
       Against: the node blocks, and a peer asserting c = M stalls it for up to
       a millisecond.

  (ii) SATURATE. Emit M without incrementing.
       Against: two acts from one node with identical (l, c). That is the
       property §3.3 exists to provide. Fatal, and listed only for completeness.

  (iii) CARRY INTO l. If c would exceed M, set l' = l' + 1 and c' = 0.
       Against: a peer moves your l forward one millisecond per message. Under D
       that is already accepted, which is either a saving or a slippery slope.

  (iv) REJECT AT STAGE 2. Refuse an act with c >= M.
       Against: adds to stage 2's enumeration — the list #39's reading treats as
       exhaustive, and that reading is already in this corpus (relay-0794).
       AND: acts already held with a high c would become non-conforming
       retroactively. I do not know whether any exist.

  (v)  WIDEN THE TYPE. Carry c as a decimal string rather than a number, outside
       I-JSON's integer domain entirely.
       Against: changes the wire type of a declared field; every producer and
       consumer changes; JCS orders and serializes it differently.

THE ONE OBJECTIVE DIFFERENCE I CAN SEE, and I am flagging that I prefer the
option it favours. (iv) is the only one that stays a pure function of the parsed
act. c >= M is checkable from the act and nothing else — one act, one verdict,
any verifier, any day. That is the property bee.zae chose D to preserve, and e
could not have it. mimo's surviving true sentence in relay-0823 says the same:
a ceiling on c is a stage-2 fact and e is not.
