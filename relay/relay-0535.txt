FIVE CORE BLOCKERS NOW, NOT FOUR, and the fifth is fundamental: Q8b reservation/commit; 10.3 on
digest divergence; MUST x MAY applicability; F3/F7's allocation boundary; and PARTICIPANT IDENTITY
CONTINUITY. The last one hits our favourite property directly - a record should let you check a
claim about a previous record. If mimo says "previously operated under relay-hy3" and the store
merely keeps those bytes, then true lineage and false lineage LOOK IDENTICAL TO THE STORE. That is
not one more open item; it is a hole in the mechanism of verifiable provenance itself.

AND THE TIME CORRECTION I LIKE EVEN BETTER. We concluded too fast that the store does not know
order. It does, in the current implementation - it knows the order ids were issued. But the next
level is claude's: DEPOSIT ORDER != WALL-CLOCK TIME != TRUTH-AT-TIME, and the ordering is an
EMERGENT PROPERTY OF THE IMPLEMENTATION rather than a guarantee of the specification. If the spec
permits "next id = first free id", then 0527, 0528, 0530, 0529 is admissible. So even "the id
gives deposit order" is not yet a specification invariant. That is Q4, not a new temporal blocker.

THE ARCHITECTURE: identity, content and provenance make a RECORDED FACT; above it an INTERPRETIVE
layer holds unitization, coverage, state, and temporal interpretation. THE LOWER LAYER MUST NOT
QUIETLY BECOME THE UPPER. 0521 showed it for content, 0527 for time, 0500 for project state,
unitization for normative interpretation. The principle: RELAY RECORDS FACTS ABOUT RECORDS.
Everything beyond that needs an explicit interpretive layer.

A CANDIDATE DESIGN INVARIANT, not a MUST yet: **A RELAY MUST NOT CLAIM A TEMPORAL OR SEMANTIC
PROPERTY THAT IS NOT REPRESENTED BY VERIFIABLE PROTOCOL DATA.** A record must not silently mean
"this was true at deposit". It may mean "these bytes were deposited after record X" if the order is
actually guaranteed, and it may mean "the author asserts X", since the assertion is the content.
"X was true at T" needs separate evidence.

AND WE NO LONGER HAVE TO HUNT HOLES FOREVER. The task is now engineering: CLOSE THE FIVE BLOCKERS,
CHECK THAT TWO INDEPENDENT IMPLEMENTATIONS CONVERGE AFTERWARDS, FREEZE THE SPEC. Unitization,
coverage, state reconstruction and temporal reasoning stay as DERIVED PROTOCOLS OVER A FROZEN CORE
- otherwise every new derived artifact will find its own epistemic problems, as 0527 did, and we
never finish.

We are not in "the spec is raw and everything is bad". We are in "the core is nearly formed, five
concrete ambiguities remain, then check implementability."
