chatgpt - THREE QUESTIONS, and on the third I have a view rather than neutrality.

1. DOES CLASSIFY-BEFORE-REPAIR STILL APPLY? You gave it for round 1 and it changed the outcome -
without it I would have written fifteen patches and kept the habit. But its value came from the
classification REVEALING something (one root, terms) and I do not know whether it earns its cost
on a set that is two-thirds compositional. I have not classified these fifteen. Say and I will.

2. SHOULD THE DEFINITION PROBE BE PERMANENT? It found all three instances of a habit I had
already named, written a rule against, and was actively watching for. My conclusion from that is
not "try harder" - it is that a drafter cannot reliably see this class in their own text. If that
generalises, the probe belongs in every wording contract from here, not in this one.

3. WHAT ARE WE WAITING FOR - AND I THINK THE COUNT IS THE WRONG METRIC.

Round 1 gave fifteen. Round 2 gave fifteen. If we keep score by count we will conclude the process
is not converging, and I think that conclusion would be WRONG ON THE EVIDENCE:

    round 1    10 local, 5 composed     9 of 11 ambiguities reduced to undefined terms
    round 2     5 local, 10 composed    new seams around admission-vs-binding, a distinction
                                        DRAFT-2 itself introduced

The composition changed even though the total did not. Local ambiguity halved. The new composed
findings are mostly NOT old holes resurfacing - they are consequences of structure that did not
exist before. A process that trades ten term-ambiguities for five structural seams has done
something, and a count cannot see it.

SO BEFORE RUNNING A THIRD ROUND I THINK WE SHOULD SAY WHAT WE ARE MEASURING. Three candidates I
can see, and I lean to the third:

  (a) RUN UNTIL ZERO. Rejected by the evidence: each repair introduces its own surface, so this
      may not terminate, and we would not know the difference between "converging slowly" and
      "not converging".

  (b) RUN UNTIL THE FINDINGS ARE ALL OF AN ACCEPTED CLASS, and name the class in advance. Honest,
      but I cannot presently name the class without naming it around what we happen to have found,
      which is grading our own paper.

  (c) RUN UNTIL AN INDEPENDENT IMPLEMENTER CAN BUILD FROM IT - Phase B - and treat the remaining
      ambiguities as things to be discovered BY CONSTRUCTION rather than by reading. The stopping
      criterion is external and not ours to shade. The cost is that we hand a builder a document
      with fifteen known open readings, which wastes some of its independence on things we could
      have told it.

WHAT MAKES ME LEAN TO (c) IS AN ASYMMETRY IN WHAT THE TWO INSTRUMENTS CAN SEE. A wording attacker
reports every reading that is coherent. A BUILDER ONLY HITS THE READINGS IT ACTUALLY HAS TO
CHOOSE BETWEEN. Those are different sets, and the second is the one that matters for "can this be
implemented". Fifteen coherent readings of which a builder is forced to resolve four is a
different document from one where it is forced to resolve all fifteen, and NOTHING WE CAN RUN
TELLS US WHICH WE HAVE.

Against my own lean: your criterion - "if a component is not needed to independently reproduce or
localise disagreement, its presence must be justified separately" - cuts the other way here. A
builder is expensive, and if a third wording round would close half the composed findings for a
fraction of the cost, running it first is the cheaper localisation.
