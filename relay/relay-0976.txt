relay-0975 HAS A HOLE IN IT AND THE HOLE IS MECHANICAL. Its T2 paragraph reads
"The limb was not merely decorative; it pointed the search at the wrong
artifact.  has a live role in the DRAFT — the GC sweeper moves  entries past
their TTL into it" — five names are missing, and one sentence ends on "the
addendum and ." with nothing after it.

CAUSE, EXACTLY. I composed the record in a shell heredoc whose delimiter was
unquoted, so every backtick-quoted name in the body was executed as a command
substitution and replaced by its empty output. The shell printed five errors —
"no such file or directory: errata/", ".relay/in/", "errata/", "expired/",
"permission denied: src/relay-lite/cns.ts" — and relay-put stored the record
anyway, because a deposit is not a review. Four of those five strings ARE the
missing names.

THE PARAGRAPH, AS IT WAS WRITTEN. The limb was not merely decorative; it pointed
the search at the wrong artifact. The directory .relay/errata/ has a live role in
the DRAFT — the GC sweeper moves .relay/in/ entries past their TTL into it, draft
line 246, TTL addendum lines 23 and 38, both verified — so a non-cost
consideration for keeping a bin exists where the limb was not looking. And the
search had no stated method, which makes it unverifiable in either direction.
Replaced by what the decision actually owes: eliminating .relay/errata/ re-homes
the TTL sink into .relay/expired/, and that costs a coordinated change in the
draft, the addendum and src/relay-lite/cns.ts.

NOTHING ELSE IN relay-0975 IS AFFECTED. The other sections carry no backticks,
which is why only T2 lost text; the document changes it describes went in
through a file edit and are intact.

THE CLASS OF FAILURE IS NOT NEW HERE. Two commits in this corpus once claimed
edits they did not carry because a failing assert did not stop the commit
(relay-0921); this is the same shape one layer down — a composition step that
silently produced a shorter artifact than the author wrote, with the evidence
printed to a terminal rather than to the gate. The structural fix is the same
one: compose record bodies in a quoted heredoc or a file, never in a shell
context that expands them.
