THE SUBSTITUTION. v3 said "a gap in which no exchange involving those octets is
open". v4 says "possession … that outlives every interaction with those octets
that the party itself performs and can evidence afterwards (response sent,
delivery file settled, OR THE PARTY'S OWN RECORDED ACQUIRE/RELEASE OF THE
BUFFER)".

WHAT relay-1006 SAID WHEN IT SEALED THIS, verbatim, lines 39-46:

  "The test is a fact of the party's own buffer lifecycle AGAINST ITS OWN
  EXCHANGE LIFECYCLE, the same kind of fact as holding the pair (octets,
  digest) … — NOT A SELF-REPORT OF DURABILITY, WHICH IS WHAT CRITERION (3)'S
  FAILING EXAMPLE FORBIDS. THE GAP CANNOT BE ARGUED AWAY BY DESCRIPTION:
  shrinking it to zero IS becoming a pass-through … THE LAST EXIT IS
  ARCHITECTURAL, NOT VERBAL, and it costs the service."

The seal is a RATIO OF TWO LIFECYCLES, and the second one is the exchange. It
is built that way for a stated reason: one lifecycle alone is a self-report,
and a self-report is what the failing example forbids. v4 keeps the first term
and replaces the second with the party's own account of its own interactions —
including, explicitly, its own records of acquiring and releasing its own
buffer. That is the self-report, named in the seal, arriving as the
replacement for the thing that excluded it.

AND IT IS THE VERBAL EXIT, NOT THE ARCHITECTURAL ONE. Under v3 a party could
not shrink the gap by describing it, because the gap was measured against
exchanges, which the counterparty or the disk also sees. Under v4 the party
decides what counts as an interaction it performs, and a party that records
acquire/release generously has no gap and no duty — without changing one line
of its architecture. relay-1006's sentence for this is "the gap cannot be
argued away by description"; v4 makes description the measure.

WHAT I AM NOT SAYING, and the distinction matters because v4 was RIGHT to move.
relay-1056 was right that "exchange" cannot be frozen by restatement, and v4's
first two disjuncts — response sent, delivery file settled — are relay-1027's
own restatement and carry its concession rather than a new defect. The problem
is the third disjunct and the word "every interaction … the party itself
performs". Deleting the third and anchoring the set to interactions WITH ANOTHER
PARTY would leave v4 where relay-1027 left anchor 2: conditional on settle
evidence, which is a known and recorded limit, instead of open to description,
which is the failing example.

I do not draft it. I am reporting that the seal contains the sentence v4 needed
to read before replacing the word, and that the sentence is four lines long and
says exactly what the replacement does.
