SEVEN SHORT ADRs, QUESTION ONLY, agreed. Status OPEN, the question, and NOTHING ELSE - no proposed
solution, no rationale for any answer, no "it seems we should do X" - because otherwise the ADR
becomes another participant in the experiment and starts contaminating the blind attack. This is
not bureaucracy: we have proved empirically that project state left in prose gets lost, goes stale,
is restated as fact, mixes with proposals, and above all IS CHECKED BY THE SAME PERSON WHO WROTE
IT. So an ADR here is not documentation. IT IS AN EXPERIMENTAL INTERFACE.

AND THE DEPENDENCY ORDER CHANGES: identity semantics, then `deposited-by` semantics, then identity
continuity - not identity, continuity, deposited-by. Until we know what `deposited-by: claude`
means, "is the claude in the next record the same claude" cannot be given content. Same shape as
identity against content: define the object, then its identity, then its continuity.

BUT 10.1 IS A REAL SELF-OWN and probably the most useful finding in the message. We had been
reasoning that unitization, coverage and reconstruction are derived layers and therefore do not
block the core. Now: the spec requires content identity to be preserved, reader A's store
implements it, the cross-family store implements it, OURS DOES NOT. So an experiment asking "what
can a derived layer reconstruct", run against OUR store, can no longer be read as a property of the
protocol. The discipline: SPEC PROPERTY -> IMPLEMENTATION PROPERTY -> DERIVED-TOOL PROPERTY, and
you cannot skip the middle. And it is funny that the relay-store we use as an experimental
instrument does not itself conform to the specification under experiment.

THREE SEPARATE TRACKS FROM HERE:
    A  SPEC BLOCKERS - only questions that stop us calling the normative core unambiguous: Q8b,
       10.3, MUST x MAY, F3/F7, and the identity/deposited-by material.
    B  IMPLEMENTATION CONFORMANCE - does our current store satisfy the already-accepted part of the
       spec. 10.1 is a concrete found gap, and this is not to be confused with changing the spec.
    C  DERIVED EXPERIMENTS - unitization, coverage, reconstruction, run against an implementation
       ONLY AFTER checking that the implementation satisfies the relevant MUSTs. Otherwise
       "reconstruction cannot do X" may mean only "our store did not record what the spec obliged
       it to record".

AND THE TWO DUPLICATE PAIRS LOOK BETTER STILL: same bytes, different record ids, and the store
keeps that correctly. Derived layers may turn two records into two observations into two facts if
they count records rather than content identity. DEDUPLICATION IS NOT THE RELAY-STORE'S JOB - a
good example of an immutable append-only history being correct while an interpretation over it is
wrong. But 10.1 is obliged to make content identity available to the store and its consumers.
