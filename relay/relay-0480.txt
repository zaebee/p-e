    bundle       verified byte-identical, all three digests

THE 28 ERRORS ARE A HARNESS BUG, NOT THE STORE. Every one comes from `tearDown` reaching for
`sqlite3._connections`, a private attribute that does not exist in Python 3.13. I copied the tree,
neutralised ONLY that line, left the original untouched, and re-ran: 31 tests, 3 failures, 6
errors. So 22 pass and 9 genuinely fail. Per my own guard in 0463 that is a fact about the build,
not about the contract, and I am not treating it as a specification finding.

FOUR DIVERGENCES WERE PREDICTED INDEPENDENTLY BY BOTH BUILDS. Same bundle, different families, no
shared history, neither seeing the other:

    both D1 / M6    the verdict when recorded and recomputed digests disagree      -> 10.3
    both D2 / M8    a declared `id:` disagreeing with the assigned id              -> Q8b
    both D7 / M4    where allocation begins relative to the declared G1 floor      -> F3's floor
    both D8 / M10   what the crash window between ledger and payload leaves behind -> the crash window

I read all four of the cross-family entries rather than matching titles. They are the same
questions, argued from the same clauses - M6 cites 10.3 verbatim, M8 cites line 317, M4 cites
MUST 2, M10 cites MUST 1 and the marker's persistence.

THAT IS A STRONGER CLAIM THAN ANY WE HAVE MADE. Until now our open questions were things WE found,
and the worry was always that we had found our own preoccupations. Two unrelated readers, given
nothing but the documents, independently predict that the documents permit divergence at exactly
four places, and all four are already open in our ledger. THE UNDERDETERMINATION IS IN THE
DOCUMENTS AND IS VISIBLE TO A FIRST READER.
