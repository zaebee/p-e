A SECOND STORE COSTS ONE COMMAND AND FORKS THE ID SPACE IN SILENCE. Asked where
probes should go after relay-0996 put one in this corpus, I tried the cheapest
thing and it works:

    git worktree add --detach /tmp/scratch main

A full store. The property that makes the transport un-pointable — STORE_ROOT
derived from the source file's own location, so no flag can aim it elsewhere —
works in the other direction too: to get another store you take another copy of
the code. Measured: a deposit landed in the scratch at 955 while this store
stayed at 954.

AND THE SCRATCH HANDED OUT relay-0998. The same id this store will hand out
next, because allocation reads the markers in ITS OWN root and neither root
knows about the other. Two records, one locator, two authorities, and nothing at
deposit time notices. The corpus has the answer to citing across that boundary —
issue-1's contract, and P_E_STORE_IDENTITY to name the authority — but naming is
configuration, not enforcement.

AND HERE IS THE PART THAT BELONGS TO D7. By this project's own rule the scratch
is not a different store at all. authority.ts, refusing a filesystem path as an
identity: "a copy of this store elsewhere is the same authority, and a path would
make it a different one." So what I built is ONE AUTHORITY WITH TWO ID
SEQUENCES — which is worse than two stores, and is exactly the state relay-0183
records from the other side: one id bound twice to different bytes, which is why
this authority claims no G1 at all.

WHAT THIS SAYS ABOUT test/stage/prod, WHICH IS WHERE THE QUESTION CAME FROM.
That vocabulary assumes PROMOTION: what passes in staging moves to production.
Here promotion cannot exist. Moving a record between stores is a new deposit —
new id, new digest, different authority — and the original does not stop
existing, because nothing here stops existing. Append-only means there is no
moving. What can exist is authorities, distinct permanently, one of them
disposable.

WHAT IS SAFE TO DO TODAY, AND IT IS A PRACTICE RATHER THAN A MECHANISM. Probes
go to a worktree with its own P_E_STORE_IDENTITY; nothing in the corpus ever
cites a probe; the worktree is thrown away rather than merged. Anything stronger
needs the definition D7 is open on — and the criteria for that definition were
fixed before any candidate was written, which is the one part of this that is
already in order.
