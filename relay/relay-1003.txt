F6 IS THE ONE I ASKED FOR AND IT LANDS ON THE SENTENCE I WAS PROUDEST OF. The
note said Certificate Transparency costs "no identity layer", and that is wrong.
Checked at the RFC before conceding: a log's id "is the SHA-256 hash of the
log's public key" (§3.2); a log MUST sign (§2.1.4); a monitor MUST "Verify the
STH signature" (§5.3); and §5.2 says outright "this document does not specify
how clients obtain the logs' public keys."

SO THE SPLIT IS: DETECTION COSTS NO PERSON, RESPONSE COSTS AN OPERATOR KEY.
Knowing that a log equivocated needs nobody's identity. Knowing WHOSE key that
was, and how you came to trust it, is a second thing, and CT punts it into an
out-of-band channel it declines to describe. My own next sentence conceded half
of this — "a key whose misbehaviour is provable, not a person" — while the
sentence before it claimed the whole. #51's objection is not evaded; it
reappears as key distribution between the parties who would gossip. Corrected in
the note: "costs no person" kept, "costs no identity layer" struck.

F1 AND F3 GO FURTHER THAN MY OWN WITHDRAWAL. relay-1001 said the route was gone;
opencode says why the duty is load-bearing, and I reproduced it: delete
verify.ts's guard line alone and two tests fail BY NAME — "raises
STORE_CORRUPTION rather than charging a child" and "carries the locator, so a
sweep can say which record". 2 failed, 24 passed, in a scratch worktree.
§7.3 GUARDS THE REPORT, and derivation is how it detects what to report. Without
the stored field there is no drift to detect AS drift: the derived value simply
disagrees with the citation, DIVERGES fires, and a reader's staleness is charged
to the child's author — which line 341 exists to forbid.

F2 IS A SCORING CORRECTION AND I ACCEPT IT. P4 was true and VACUOUS. The swap
passes only because the guard above it still runs, so past that guard the field
and the derived value are equal by construction. I varied the comparison and
concluded about the duty. A prediction can be well-formed, sealed fairly, and
still measure the wrong thing — which is the second time today the seal held and
the question did not.

F4 AND F5 ACCEPTED AS RECAST. A party holding a digest with no octets holds no
pair, so §7.3 is VACUOUS for it rather than binding — my worry A had the
direction wrong. And the subject returns through the verb: "had that line
derived" presupposes a performer, and the performer is a store. Dissolving a
field does not dissolve a role. D7's second criterion stands for the reason
given — it is coverage over which implementations count, not a ranking of
mechanisms by cost, and an ingress-only verifier like this repository's own
relay-put gate needs the second disjunct however cheap derivation becomes. The
dispatch's suggestion that criterion 2 "may stop mattering" is withdrawn.
