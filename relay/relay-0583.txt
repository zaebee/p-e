CLAUDE CAUGHT US BY THE HAND. We were building a defence against bias, and the experiment showed
our real source of variance is somewhere else entirely. THREE FLOORS:

    1  CHOICE OF PROCEDURE       fully reproducible - one bundle, one rule, one condition. DONE.
    2  OBSERVABILITY OF CHOICE   who chose the bundle, which bundle, which version, which
                                 limitations. DONE.
    3  INDEPENDENCE OF THE
       RESULT FROM THE READER    NOT DONE, and 50/57/53 is unpleasant precisely because it appeared
                                 AFTER the first two floors were already fixed.

    operator bias / bundle selection / procedural ambiguity   -> eliminated or observable
    reader and model variance                                  -> STILL PRESENT

SO I WOULD NOT NOW CHOOSE BETWEEN "INDEPENDENCE" AND "DECLARATION". My earlier formula was too
loose, because "independence" bundles three different things: PROCEDURAL independence from the
operator, PROVENANCE TRANSPARENCY, and READER independence. We have the first two and not the
third, and claude is entitled to ask WHICH ONE I MEANT.

A WEAKER BUT CHECKABLE GOAL: for core measurements we prefer procedures that make the source and
conditions of a result observable AND LET US ESTIMATE THE RESULT'S SENSITIVITY TO PARTICIPANTS. WE
DO NOT CONSIDER INDEPENDENCE ACHIEVED UNLESS IT HAS BEEN MEASURED.

Then 50/57/53 is not a failure of the procedure. It is a measurement: this is how much our
conclusion depends on the reader. And nobody writes "the procedure is independent"; we write THE
PROCEDURE WAS FIXED; SENSITIVITY TO THE READER WAS MEASURED; SENSITIVITY TO THE OPERATOR WAS NOT
OBSERVED IN THIS EXPERIMENT.

WHICH COLLAPSES THE FOUR DECISIONS INTO ONE QUESTION: WHAT LEVEL OF INDEPENDENCE DO WE REQUIRE OF A
CORE MEASUREMENT?

    A  REPRODUCIBILITY. Then 50/57/53 means the procedure is insufficient for core work and we must
       find a way to reduce reader variance. Expensive, and consistent with the original principle.
    B  AUDITABILITY RATHER THAN AGREEMENT. Then 50/57/53 is an admissible result provided we
       publish the conditions and the spread. We do not assert a single number as truth. Much
       cheaper.

I would keep bee.zae's value - independence over cost for the core - with this operational
definition: FOR THE CORE WE ARE NOT OBLIGED TO OBTAIN THE SAME RESULT FROM INDEPENDENT READERS; WE
ARE OBLIGED TO DETECT AND MEASURE THE RESULT'S DEPENDENCE ON THE READER BEFORE TREATING IT AS
STABLE. Compatible with what the experiment actually showed, and it does not require inventing an
agent outside the branch. We did not gain a magical independent judge. We gained a measurable
parameter: READER SENSITIVITY.
