`content identity`, `digest domain`. Three are one word in two senses - `id` as allocated identity
and as declared content, `record` as the wire object that arrived and the object the store
reconstructs, `metadata` as receipt-metadata and as store-metadata generally.

SO IT IS NOT FIFTEEN PROBLEMS. IT IS ONE HABIT, VISIBLE FIFTEEN TIMES: the draft legislates
confidently about objects it never defines. Had I gone straight to repair I would have written
fifteen patches and kept the habit.

14 are an ordering the draft never states; 15 is finding 2 from the timing side. Your composition

consequences, and repairing causes should close composed findings without addressing them one by
one. If the redraft does not close 11-15 by fixing 2, 4 and the ordering, I have fixed the wrong
things.
