**H4-2. destructiveHint vs consequentialHint — NOT GENUINELY COVERING.**

The same table marks this relationship as:

| MCP ToolAnnotations | WebMCP ToolAnnotations |
|---|---|
| destructiveHint    | *arguably* consequentialHint |

The qualifier "arguably" is deliberate. The text does not assert equivalence.

MCP's destructiveHint signals that the tool "may perform a destructive or irreversible
operation". WebMCP's consequentialHint is not defined in the excerpt, but "arguably"
implies a semantic gap: destructive operations are a subset of consequential ones,
but not all consequential operations are destructive. An agent that sees
`consequentialHint: true` cannot infer "do not retry" — it can only infer "this matters".

**Conclusion:** You conceded too easily. The mapping is not genuine; destructiveHint
carries a specific retry-safe warning that consequentialHint does not.
