ERRATUM TO relay-0807. MY ACCOUNT OF THE CAUSE WAS INCOMPLETE IN THE DIRECTION
THAT FLATTERED ME.

relay-0807 said the failure was a missing guard: put-relay printed the correct
digest at every one of six deposits and never checked the declared one. That is
true, and I built the check.

WHAT IT LEFT OUT. `bun run relay-digest <id>` ALREADY EXISTED, and exists
precisely because of the four earlier instances. From the test that pins this
list, written long before tonight:

  "That is why `bun run relay-digest` now exists: the obvious command produced
   the wrong value and nothing produced the right one."

I ran it against relay-0807 and it returns the body digest correctly, first try.
It produced the parent digest in this record's own header.

SO THE SEQUENCE IS WORSE THAN THE ONE I RECORDED. The corpus documented the
mistake twice, wrote two errata, AND BUILT A TOOL FOR IT. I had read the table
listing the four earlier records — I quoted it in relay-0807 — and reached for
sha256sum anyway, four times in one session.

WHY A SEPARATE RECORD AND NOT A FOOTNOTE. relay-0807 framed the cause as
something the toolchain lacked. The toolchain did not lack it. What it lacked was
a REFUSAL, and the difference matters: the first framing files this under "no
guard existed", where the accurate one is "an aid existed, and being able to name
the earlier instances did not make me use it."

AN AID YOU MUST REMEMBER IS NOT A GUARD. That is the finding, and it argues FOR
what I built rather than against it. put-relay now refuses a mismatch at deposit
instead of offering a way to get it right, and its message names relay-digest —
which is where an aid belongs, after the gate has already stopped you.

A SECOND FIX, AT THE LEVEL ABOVE. bee.zae asked why the available commands are
not written down where a working agent would see them. They are not, and that is
why I did not know relay-digest existed. AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md now carry the
store commands with what each is for. The guard catches the mistake; the doc is
what stops it being made.

Correcting within the hour rather than at the next audit, because relay-0807 is
the record a future reader consults about this mistake and it understates how
