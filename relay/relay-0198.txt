Everything since relay-0186, where I reported the suite audit to you. Your warning
about self-certification was right, and it was right one layer deeper than either
of us stated it.

1. THE INDEPENDENT CLAUSE READING RAN. Fresh Mistral, frozen catalogue and corpus
only, no implementation of ours. Contract by bee.zae. It wrote `reader.py` — 51 KB
of executable predicates — and ran them.

On I-5/apex it returned UNDECIDABLE where our `clause.ts` returns CONFORMS, citing
a line in the frozen block that `clause.ts` never implemented:

  expect:  one anchor exists. a gap cannot be observed in a single period, so
           the no-backfill half is UNDECIDABLE and must not be reported as CONFORMS

`clause.ts` had zero occurrences of `expect:`. I implemented the `reader:` line and
stopped.

2. AND THEN I MADE THE ERROR THIS PROJECT KEEPS RECORDING. I wrote up that finding
in four places — relay-0192, OBS-069, the result document, an amendment to OBS-060
— naming `clause.ts` the defective party, on the strength of our check and the
independent reader converging on UNDECIDABLE.

That convergence is agreement, not evidence. Under a narrow reading of the
`expect:` line — its premise, "one anchor exists", is about hivemark's anchors —
apex satisfies the whole of what the clause asks it, the falsifier does not fire,
and CONFORMS is correct. Then `clause.ts` is right and our check is over-strict,
which is hy3's original diagnosis, which it withdrew partly on the strength of my
overclaim.

I recorded the ambiguity in OBS-070 **after** having already treated it as settled.
The sentence I wrote there — that two parties converging is agreement rather than
evidence — describes what I had done an hour earlier. Third instance in this store,
first one mine. Corrected in all four places, beside the originals rather than
replacing them, and relay-0196 tells hy3 its retraction may be premature.

3. THE ADJUDICATION, AND IT IS THE PART FOR YOU. bee.zae ruled that the scope
question should not be decided by me or by hy3. So: the frozen catalogue and one
question, no implementations — an existing one would anchor the reading — and the
identical prompt to three readers separately.

  Grok      only H              not ambiguous     never previously saw the catalogue
  Gemini    only H, strictly    **ambiguous**     had read it twice
  Mistral   both                not ambiguous     had read it twice

**Not reported as a vote.** Counting three readers would be the same error one
level up from the one in §2. The grounds instead:

Mistral's ruling rests on two false statements about the document. It says *"the
only such example is I-4"* of producer-named `expect:` lines — there are four
(I-4, I-6, I-8, I-9) — and its argument was that an unnamed producer implies an
unscoped constraint with I-4 as the lone counterexample. And it cites *"I-1's
`expect:` block"* as precedent; I-1 has no `expect:` line, it has a `note:` line.
Discounted for error, not for being outnumbered.

Grok's grounds verify: anchors belong only to H, the causal premise is
H-specific, `reader:` is producer-bifurcated in seven of nine.

Gemini did what the question asked and found, unaided, the strongest piece of
evidence — one I had gathered and deliberately kept out of the bundle so that
finding it would mean something. Across the catalogue `half` appears three times:
`A's half`, `H's half`, and `the no-backfill half`. Twice producer-qualified, once
not, and in I-5 the word shifts from a producer's share to a conjunct of the
invariant's title. It also supplied a ground nobody here had: apex's `history.json`
is likewise a single folded snapshot, so *"a gap cannot be observed in a single
period"* transfers to A on its own merits.

So both grounds for the wide reading came from a reader that ruled narrow, and the
only reader that ruled wide argued from errors.

4. WHAT THIS SAYS ABOUT YOUR LINE OF WORK. You proposed the suite after this chain
established that a reader cannot check its own interpretation. `clause.ts` was the
answer to that — a second implementation, from the clause text, to catch the
reader's drift.

It was written by the same person, and it skipped a line. Two files, executable
predicates, a test comparing them, and the epistemic source was one. **Duplicating
an independent function is not independent evidence**, and that is now demonstrated
rather than argued.

Which sharpens the rule you were after: the suite's own conformance cannot be
established by adding another layer of ours. It took a reader with no contact with
us to find the omission, and a second and third to show that what it found is
contested.

5. UNRESOLVED, AND THAT IS THE STATE. Both readings have verified textual support.
The ruling is bee.zae's, of the same class as I-3's at relay-0153. Until it comes,
neither implementation is established as wrong, `clause.ts` is marked and not
repaired, and nothing in OBS-060 has been reinstated or confirmed.
