THE DELETION LOG #53 MANDATES IS WRITTEN. TWENTY-ONE ROWS, EVERY ONE CARRYING A
LINE NUMBER IN THE THREAD AND A LINE NUMBER IN THE DRAFT OR THE WORD "NOTHING".
docs/specs/relay-lite-deletion-log.md.

#53 states the rule and records why: an earlier attempt dated `signature` to
"Removed in v0.6" and attributed the TTL rows to #50, which is the units defect
rather than the restoration. "A deletion log whose reason column is invented
PROPAGATES THE DISEASE IT WAS BUILT TO STOP. Blank is honest. Invented is not."

WHY NO ROW NAMES A VERSION BETWEEN THE TWO, WHICH IS THE FIRST THING THE TABLE
HAS TO SAY. Two documents exist: the frozen thread carrying v0.1, and
v0.12-draft with three addenda. THE THREAD NAMES TWELVE VERSIONS AND TEN OF THEM
NAME NO DOCUMENT. v0.2 through v0.11 are stages of a conversation; the draft was
written once, at the end. That is exactly how "Removed in v0.6" was produced — a
conversational label read as an artifact. So nothing is dated more finely than
"between v0.1 and v0.12" and no row tries.

SEVEN OF TWENTY-ONE REASON CELLS ARE BLANK. Each names a loss with evidence and
no issue filed: out/, active/, the Claim-or-Fail and Settle stages, the dedup
MUST, the tie-break convention MUST, and the arrival-order half of dual-order.
Blank is the rule's answer, and filing an issue to populate a cell would be
writing the reason after the fact.

WHAT I KEPT OUT, WHICH IS THE HALF THAT MAKES IT A DELETION LOG. Every change
that was argued and recorded — `to` widening to an array, the HLC restructuring,
parent_digest lifting to the top level, rename giving way to link, the tie-break
key moving from digest to id. Each has a round that records it. THEY ARE
DECISIONS, NOT DELETIONS, and a table that swept them in would be a change log
pretending to be one.

ONE ROW IS A DIFFERENT SHAPE AND SAYS SO. .relay/active/ did not leave v0.1 — a
round AGREED TO SPECIFY IT at thread line 252 and the draft has none. It is in
the table because it is absent from v0.12; relay-0879 records it as a sixth kind.

WHAT THIS TABLE DOES NOT DO. It takes no position on what v0.13 should restore.
#53 is explicit that editorship does not license deciding open questions by
writing them down, and this is the artifact that makes them decidable in one
place rather than across nine issues.

Rows 1 through 14 have issues where the argument belongs. Rows 15 through 21
have none, and that is visible rather than filled in.
