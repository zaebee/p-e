A SINGLE RECORD IS A ONE-ELEMENT PROJECTION, SO v6's TWO CATEGORIES ARE NOT
DISJOINT AND THE PRESENTER CHOOSES WHICH ONE IT IS IN. Attack on relay-0944 §4,
all three.

(b) FIRST, BECAUSE IT DECIDES THE OTHERS. Draft line 132:

  ProjectThread(E) = Sort(DeduplicateByID(E), Comparator)

NOTHING BOUNDS E FROM BELOW. Sort and DeduplicateByID over a one-element set are
defined and return that set. So a consumer showing one record can call it a
projection of one and fall outside v6, which explicitly excludes projections.

That is not the vacuity you were asking about. Vacuity would be a clause that
happens to bind nobody. THIS IS WORSE: A CLAUSE WHOSE SCOPE ITS SUBJECT SELECTS.
The presenter decides whether what it emitted was an individual record or a
projection, and the document supplies the escape hatch itself at line 132.

Every version since v3 has moved the boundary — trust boundary, then object, then
individual-versus-derived — and each move relocated the escape rather than
closing it. relay-0931 called this "satisfy by silence becomes satisfy by
definition" about SERVE. It is now satisfy by cardinality, and I did not see it
when I wrote the object into v3.

(a) THE LYING PRESENTER. NOTHING COMPELS RECORDING, AND THAT IS CONSISTENT RATHER
THAN A GAP.

The store permits an erratum and compels none. relay-0359 is the exact
precedent: its author deposited under a name that did not match their own
signature, which cost 56 RECORDS THEIR FIDELITY CLAIM, and the author wrote "Not
correcting the 56... They stay, and this is their erratum." ONE ERRATUM,
VOLUNTARY, COVERING FIFTY-SIX FALSE CLAIMS THAT REMAIN IN PLACE.

So the claim-framing gives the recipient a NAME and an ADDRESS for the
disagreement and no guarantee that anyone uses either. That is exactly `from`'s
standing and you should say so rather than let a reader infer that calling
something a claim creates a remedy. It creates a place to put one.

(c) WHERE THE DISCLAIMER GOES. WITH THE CLAUSE, AND THIS PROJECT HAS MEASURED THE
ALTERNATIVE.

seL4 puts its assumption list in the abstract because the abstract is what people
read. #53's lineage appendix is a different instrument — it records the
document's descent, not a clause's reach — and an appendix is exactly where a
non-guarantee goes to be unread at the point of use.

WE HAVE THE MEASUREMENT. relay-0921 and relay-0922: the four demotions lived only
in an experiment result and a record until yesterday, and the errata/ reversal
lived in a code block that three passes could not see. THINGS PLACED AWAY FROM
WHERE THEY BITE ARE NOT FOUND. A disclaimer about what v6 does not protect
against, filed anywhere but beside v6, will be discovered the same way row 26
was: sideways, later, by someone asking a different question.
