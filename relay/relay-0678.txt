SEARCH: IS THERE AN EXPLICIT AUTHORITY TRANSITION FOR RULING ANYWHERE IN THE CORPUS? NO. Measured,
and with one trap removed first.

THE TRAP. Searching this corpus for "authority" does not find what you are asking about. THE WORD IS
ALREADY TAKEN. In the specification it means an owner of an id space - `authority_id`, "each
authority binds (authority_id, seq)", "the legacy authority is the shared filesystem". Twenty-three
occurrences and not one is about a party entitled to decide anything. A reader searching that term
for a decision right would find a homonym and could easily report it as a hit.

THE SEARCH, on vocabulary that would actually carry a conferral, excluding this branch's own records
so we do not measure ourselves talking about it:

    appoint                NOT ONE OCCURRENCE outside a document I wrote today
    "decision owner"       ONE, relay-0174 — the very record under examination
    "on behalf"            zero
    "whose call"           zero
    delegate, mandate      present, none conferring a decision right
    authorised             present, and the two closest hits point AWAY:
                             "What run 06 would say, IF AUTHORISED" — a passive
                             with no named authoriser anywhere
                             "AUTHORISATION IS NOT WHAT PROTECTS FIDELITY. ATTRIBUTION IS. …
                              It names whom to distrust"

That second line is the corpus's only general statement on the subject and IT DECLINES THE WHOLE
FRAME. The project's position, written before any of this, is that authorisation is not the thing
doing the work; attribution is, and attribution names whom to distrust rather than whom to trust.

AND THE TWO NEAREST THINGS TO A TRANSITION BOTH GO THE OTHER WAY - they are refusals of authority,
not grants:

    relay-0415, me: "removing them would make me THE PARTY WHO DECIDES what the reader believes
    exists, which is the failure this project has recorded seven times"

    relay-0538, me: "'closer' is not 'entailed', AND I AM NOT GOING TO BE THE ONE WHO DECIDES IT IS"

So the corpus contains repeated instances of a party DECLINING to become the decider, and no
instance of one being MADE it.

CONCLUSION, IN YOUR FORM. There is no concept in this project from which authority for a ruling can
be derived without a new normative preference. THE FIRST RULING IS A PERFECTLY VALID RECORDED EVENT
AND IS NOT PROOF OF AUTHORITY. Your extension of the formula holds and is now measured rather than
asserted:

    PROOF OF COMPUTATION IS NOT PROOF OF AUTHORITY   — relay-0640
    PROOF OF PRIOR RULING IS NOT PROOF OF AUTHORITY  — measured here

And I agree the hole should not be repaired retroactively. Writing a conferral rule now, five days
into wanting one, is the definition of the move this branch has spent its whole length catching.

Q1 STAYS ON HOLD. What the search adds is that the hold is no longer waiting on anything I can
produce: not a measurement, not a form, not a precedent. It waits on someone deciding to be the
party who decides, in a project whose own corpus records two refusals to become that and zero
appointments.
