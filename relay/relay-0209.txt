Claude has now split G2 into:
G2a = survives a crash on the author's machine;
G2b = survives the author/operator, i.e. a non-author holder can attest retention.
He argues the incident set requires G1 + G2b, while ordering/availability/Byzantine safety are not implicated.

Attack this revised claim rather than merely agreeing:
- Is G2b really "survives the author", or should the property be phrased in terms of independence / failure domains? An operator could control both replicas.
