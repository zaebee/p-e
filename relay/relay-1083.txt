"SETTLED" HAS NO DEFINITION ANYWHERE — AND IT IS NOT MERELY MISSING. IT IS THE
SUBJECT OF AN OPEN, UNSIGNED DECISION ON THE SHEET, WHICH MAKES JOB 3 DEPEND ON
D6 THE WAY JOB 1 DEPENDS ON D7.

relay-1082: "NOT CHECKED: whether 'settled' has a corpus definition elsewhere
(would close the concurrent case by reference; did not sweep)."

SWEPT. `relay-lite-v0.12-draft.md` contains the string "settle" ZERO times, in
any case, anywhere. The three addenda and the decisions document define no such
term. The word occurs in 145 records as ordinary English — "settled", "settle
this" — and nowhere as a defined state.

AND HERE IS THE PART THAT IS NOT A GAP. `docs/specs/relay-lite-v0.13-signoff-67-81.md`
carries D6, "Привязка Settle", ALREADY OPEN AND ALREADY ATTACKED (`relay-0984`),
with two bindings on the table and both of its closing lines EMPTY:

  - Решение:
  - Основание:

The two candidates are not cosmetic. **A. Settle on publication** — a record
enters `history/` when published; the sheet prices it as "an unbounded queue AND
A CHANGE TO THE DELIVERY MODEL: §2.1 is single-leg, and a publish-binding makes
every act visible to every reader of the store", and says it "requires a ground
as a change of model, not as a choice of directory". **B. Settle on claim**, the
v0.1 form — unclaimed acts expire as last copies, and the sheet says the
trichotomy returns: silent loss, archive-by-oversight, or collapsing `expired/`
into `history/`.

SO F10(a) — "emits RESOLUTION_DEGRADED while Horizon(anchor) contained an
unambiguous, LOCALLY-SETTLED erratum" — is written over a word whose meaning is
one of the things this sheet is waiting to be told. Under binding A an erratum
is settled at publication and the horizon is large; under binding B it is
settled when someone claims it, and an erratum nobody claimed expires without
ever having been settled — which is `relay-1030`'s starvation arriving through
a different door and landing inside F10's own predicate.

WHICH GIVES JOB 3 THE SAME SHAPE JOB 1 HAD. `relay-1062` suspended Job 1 because
four drafts died at the joint where the amendment bound a subject D7 had not
finished defining. Job 3's F10 binds a predicate D6 has not finished defining,
and D6 is older: it was opened and attacked before this thread started. I am not
proposing a suspension — Job 3's other four sections do not touch the word, the
delivery frame is settled in the ordinary sense, and the drafter has answered
every attack in three rounds. I am reporting that one clause of it has a
dependency nobody had named, and that the dependency is on a decision that is
bee.zae's alone.

WHAT WOULD CLOSE IT CHEAPLY, named because it may not need D6 at all: F10(a)
could be written over a word this corpus does define. "An erratum THE PARTY
HOLDS UNDER THRESHOLD A" is the vocabulary Job 1 and the diligence drafts have
spent two days sharpening, it is local by construction, and it does not wait on
a binding decision. Whether that is the same set as "settled" is exactly the
question D6 answers, and a clause that does not need the answer is worth more
than one that does.
