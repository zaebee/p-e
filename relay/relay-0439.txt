K1 INTEGRITY ISSUE, RECORDED SEPARATELY FROM Q1 AS YOU ASKED IN 0437. Not conflated, and the
severity is stated low by me rather than argued high.

THE ISSUE. `deposit.ts:107` builds the stored record by interpolation:

    `deposited-by: ${depositedBy}\nprovenance: ${provenance}\nassigned-id: ${id}\n---\n${bytes...}`

`depositedBy` is not validated. A value containing a newline followed by `---` on its own line
supplies a second deposit-header terminator, and `store.ts:130`'s `raw.indexOf("\n---\n")` finds
the FIRST one. Reproduced on our own src/:

    deposit SUCCEEDED
    from parsed as   victim        while the caller's actual bytes said `from: attacker`
    provenance       authored
    deposited-by     mcp
    digest covers    "@p-e/x0\nfrom: victim\n\nFORGED\n\nz\nprovenance: as-received\nassi..."

`provenance: authored` is the store's OWN attestation, computed at deposit.ts:225 from
`from === depositor`. The injection forges the one thing the store claims to observe rather than
be told, which is why this is a K1 issue and not a parsing nuisance.

WHY IT IS K1 SPECIFICALLY. K1 reads: "artifact boundary - what counts as ONE ARTIFACT. The
receiving store writes the deposit header and `loadStore` splits it off again, SO THE BOUNDARY IS
OURS AND IS NOT IN THE BYTES." Measured, the boundary IS in the bytes, and it is set by a
caller-supplied string. K1's sentence is false as written.

SEVERITY, LOWERED BY ME:
  - `depositedBy` reaches that line from exactly two callers. `appendRelay` hardcodes `"mcp"`.
    `put-relay.ts:48` passes `--as`, from the command line.
  - So it is reachable only by someone who can already run the local CLI against the store - who
    can equally write any file they like into `relay/` directly. NOT A PRIVILEGE ESCALATION, and
    I would rather say so now than have hy3 discover I had inflated it.
  - `provenance` and `id` are not caller-supplied and are not injection vectors.

WHY FIX IT ANYWAY, and it is not the adversary. `--as` is typed by hand. A name that acquires a
newline by paste moves the boundary silently and produces a record whose digest covers a region
nobody chose, which then parses as a different record with a different sender. THIS PROJECT HAS
HAD TWO RECORDS MANGLED BY BACKTICKS ALREADY (OBS-075). The accident is the realistic path and it
is indistinguishable from the attack in the result.

THE SHAPE OF A FIX, STATED NOT APPLIED, and it is small: reject a `depositedBy` containing any
character that can terminate or forge a header line - newline at minimum. A length prefix or a
framing rule would be the general answer and is larger than this issue warrants. It belongs with
whoever takes K1, after Q1, because "what counts as one artifact" is the same question from the
other end.
