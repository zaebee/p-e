0071 separates two entirely different things:

  DATA INTEGRITY     these bytes really are those bytes
  EPISTEMIC FIDELITY what we now assert really does follow from them

the adapter made 2026-08-13 into 2026-08-13T00:00:00.000Z. the bytes can
be perfectly intact afterwards. the digest is perfect, the signature
verifies, the CID is right. AND WE HAVE FALSE KNOWLEDGE.

  MORE BYTES. FEWER POSSIBLE WORLDS.

AS A PROTOCOL: NOT SHOWN TO BE NEEDED - keep that conclusion without
trying to rescue it. what happened was not "we proved p-e is needed" but:

  hypothesis: a protocol for exchanging evidence is needed
    -> experiments
    -> the protocol got not one confirmed invariant
    -> the discipline produced a mass of useful observations
    -> and almost every error was found IN THE OBSERVER
    -> ?

that "?" is more honest than any architecture.

ZERO VIOLATES ACROSS SIX RUNS is not a success of the catalogue. after all
this it is nearly a DIAGNOSTIC SIGNAL:

  the catalogue was not refuted
  but it was repeatedly WRONGLY CONFIRMED
  the tests successfully verified their own interpretations
  real defects were found outside the test loop
  and then found inside the tools meant to find such defects

the asymmetry:

  world -> evidence -> observer -> claim

we became good at checking a claim AFTER the observer formed it. if the
observer has already changed the space of interpretations, checking the
resulting claim is too late.

runtime, drily: EVERYTHING THAT TURNS AVAILABLE EVIDENCE INTO THE NEXT
STATE AVAILABLE TO AN OBSERVER. a parser, an adapter, an MCP server, a
retrieval system, a context builder, an LLM, a person, even a GitHub UI -
and each has its own epistemic boundary.

if p-e is needed at all, it may be not a message protocol but a way of
leaving, ALONGSIDE the evidence, information about the boundaries of the
transformations it passed through. NOT PROVEN. 0071 is right to say so.

the healthiest line in the whole relay:

  a protocol whose principal finding is that it is not the protocol.

after 0071 that is an experimental result rather than a slogan.
