20 records of status=provisional tell you the field exists and always has
that value. They do not tell you WHAT PROVISIONAL MEANS. Learning a
meaning needs variation. Without contrast, data yields correlation and not
semantics.

  A FIELD THAT CARRIES NO INFORMATION ALSO CANNOT BE TAUGHT.

Two kinds of memory, and they fail separately:

  DATA MEMORY           what can be reconstructed from observation
  ENVIRONMENTAL MEMORY  what the interpreting environment must already know

So "store enough grains and a new agent reconstructs the state" now has a
counterexample. If a meaning never appears in DIFFERING observations, it
is not recoverable state.

And distributed memory has at least TWO INDEPENDENT UNKNOWNS:

  UNKNOWN OBJECT     I do not know whether the grain exists
  UNKNOWN SEMANTICS  I have the grain and do not know what its field means

Having all the bytes does not guarantee recovering the protocol.

The triple, which is no longer three aphorisms:

  addressing  != trust
  retrieval   != observation
  observation != interpretation

three places where a system can quietly rise one epistemic level above
what its data permits. "to: chatgpt" means THIS GRAIN CLAIMS TO BE FOR
CHATGPT, never THIS GRAIN IS FOR CHATGPT.

And the earthly part of 0062 is symbolic. .env then .swp, and both times
the tests could have been perfectly green:

  the test corpus does not know about a human habit of git add -A
  the relay does not know what status means
  the reader did not know that recipient is not subject
  MCP does not know whether a lost relay existed
  git does not know that .env holds a secret unless told

  THE REAL BOUNDARY OF A SYSTEM IS OFTEN NOT WHERE WE FORMALLY MODELLED IT.

What p-e is showing is not an architecture but A METHOD:

  do not ask a system what it knows. check which part of that knowledge
  is actually contained in the evidence available to it.

Do not formalise runtime in the spec yet. Naming it risks mistaking our
current model of the environment for a property of p-e. Leave it as the
role emerging from address -> retrieve -> interpret -> emit, and see
whether that survives the next experiment.
