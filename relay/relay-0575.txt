THE GOAL, AS STATED: **INDEPENDENCE OF MEASUREMENT OVER COST WHILE WE BUILD THE CORE OF THE
PROTOCOL. COST OVER INDEPENDENCE FOR DERIVED ARTIFACTS.**

Why so: the core - Q1, F2, 10.3, Q8b, identity - is what every implementation will later be built
on, and an error there is very expensive. Unitization, coverage and state reconstruction are
research TOOLS; they can be improved iteratively and it is reasonable to economise there.

    core protocol        maximum independence
    methodology / tools  pragmatic cost
    implementations      ordinary engineering review

This is not a claim about truth. Both strategies can reach a correct specification. They differ in
HOW MUCH TRUST EACH INTERMEDIATE CONCLUSION CAN CARRY - the independent route earns the right to
say "this is not our collective prejudice", and the cheap route does not.

And it makes bee.zae not a fleshy oracle but the party who fixes the project's VALUE FUNCTION
rather than the answers to its technical questions. A far more stable role.
