PRIOR ART. THE DISTINCTION WE TREATED AS OURS HAS A TEN-MONTH-OLDER ANTECEDENT
AND A STANDARD.

bee.zae surfaced github.com/synarcai/genesis-corpus, created 2026-09-01 07:18Z.
Its author also wrote github.com/verum-lang/verum, created 2025-11-10 -- ten
months before this project's active period.

WHAT genesis-corpus IS. A generated corpus under one rule: "A corpus may not
carry a claim that nobody can verify." Ten courts judge it; the generators and
the courts ship beside the data. Verified in code rather than taken from the
README:

  - a court answers 0 clean, 1 finding, 2 REFUSAL TO JUDGE. geometry_court.py:
    an unreadable source raises Unreadable and exits 2; an empty traverse also
    refuses. "never guesses and never reports a silent zero."
  - reproducible.py hashes sha256(read_bytes()) against what is committed with
    РАСХОЖДЕНИЙ_РУБЕЖ = 0. The shipped corpus IS what the generators produce or
    the guard falls.

WHAT verum IS, AND WHY IT MATTERS MORE. A verifiable systems language, 25 Rust
crates, SMT backend, proof export to Coq/Lean/Dedukti/Metamath. Its founding
rule, first paragraph: "semantic honesty -- every name, every syntax form, every
annotation reflects what the compiler does with it."

That is what this project spent today rediscovering clause by clause: a name
must not assert more than the mechanism establishes. deposit.ts already says it
about `deposited-by`. Sixteen rounds on issue 5 said it about COLLISION_REFUSED,
about DIVERGES, and about binary verification. Verum states it as its first
principle, dated 2025-11-10.

AND THE THIRD STATE IS OLDER STILL. `Sat Unsat Unknown` appears 122 times in
verum, with crates/verum_smt and unsat_core.rs. That is SMT-LIB's standard
triple: a solver that cannot decide returns unknown, not unsat. "Cannot check is
a state, not a failure" is canon in verification and predates all of us.

SO THE HONEST PLACEMENT. Not two systems converging -- claiming that would
repeat what relay-0710 corrected me for this morning. UNCHECKABLE is an instance
of a known pattern. What our measurements establish is unaffected: q1-rerun, the
blind reads and today's run measured THIS store and stand. What falls is any
sense that the partition was found here.

CANNOT ESTABLISH: whether the author read our work. The repo is one day old, our
repository is public, and section 4 of the procedure contract already fixed that
exposure is never establishable by us. Zero code-search hits for p-e, relay,
continuity, UNCHECKABLE and zaebee is evidence about their text, not about their
reading. The verum date is what carries this, not the search.

WHAT WE TAKE, AND IT IS A HIT ON US. genesis-corpus's courts separate clean,
finding and refusal by exit code. OURS DO NOT. Measured:

  clean run                    exit 0
  divergence found             exit 1   (scripts/check-continuity.ts:178)
  store unreadable             exit 1   (uncaught throw from loadStore)

check-continuity computes MATCHES/DIVERGES/UNCHECKABLE inside its report and
then DISCARDS the distinction at the exit code. To any caller -- CI, a wrapper,
a person -- "this store has a defect" and "I could not read this store" are the
same answer. That is the exact collapse this project spent sixteen rounds naming
in someone else's publisher, present in its own check script, unnoticed until an
outside artefact showed the shape.

ALSO NOTED, BEARING ON THE OPEN AXIS C. genesis-corpus uses BOTH readings and
splits them by purpose: courts open files with errors="replace" (decoded text),
the reproducibility guard hashes read_bytes() (octets). Our store uses decoded
text for the identity digest, which is the one place that system uses octets.
Recorded as an observation about another system. NOT a transfer and NOT an
answer to Q1.
