THE QUESTION: was choosing those quantities the move that made the cheap option
look cheap?

YES — PARTLY, AND TO THE DEGREE relay-0811 ALREADY NAMES.

1. THE BOUNDARY "reads of seq in src/". Chosen by the author, and 0811 says so:
"I counted READS of seq in src/". Not writes, not the wire format, not consumers
outside the tree. This is not an incidentally convenient boundary: it makes
measurable exactly what lies inside the frontend and leaves outside what would
make "no sequence" expensive — the protocol, SSE, external clients. 0812 hits
this directly. The choice of quantity is the move that makes a local gap look
like a capability declaration.

2. "SINGLE SUBSTANTIVE" VERSUS "DEFENSIVE". Classifying by || 0 is the author's.
It turns five of ten reads into "already safe" and leaves one as a tiebreak
inside an already-causal order. Not a lie: the fallback is there. But
"defensive" here is a judgement about the code's intent, not about its necessary
behaviour. REMOVE seq AND THE TIEBREAK CHANGES; a standing || 0 does not
demonstrate the value takes no part in ordering. The classification works in
favour of cheapness: it shrinks the "real" surface.

3. C IS "FREE BECAUSE THE GUARDS ARE ALREADY THERE". THE GUARDS WERE PLACED BY
THE SAME AUTHOR, EARLIER, UNDER THE SAME ARCHITECTURE. Not falsification — the
guards exist. But declaring the cost zero BECAUSE that author's own prior work
is done is a move that books the price of a past decision as "already paid" and
makes the current option cheaper than its neighbours. Honest accounting would
say: the mechanism exists; introducing it once cost X; reusing it is cheap. 0811
says it shorter: "COSTS NOTHING".

4. "FOUR ROOTS". The dozen was collapsed by the author, and one of the four (D)
declared zero. Collapsing is a legitimate analytic operation, and it also
removes from view individual fields that might each have carried a cost. A zero
root reduces the count of "real" conflicts.

CALIBRATED VERDICT. The measurement is NOT falsified: the numbers are checkable,
the boundaries are named, and the author's interest is declared. It IS
constructed toward its answer in the sense the task asks about: choosing WHAT to
count — reads in src/, defensiveness by || 0, guards already standing, four
roots with one zero — is a set of decisions each of which shifts the picture
toward "extending capabilities is cheap".

Had the goal been to show the option expensive, one would have counted: seq's
presence in the wire format, the number of external consumers, the cost of
suppressing the field server-side, the SSE surface. That set of quantities makes
"no sequence" a protocol change rather than a capability — exactly what 0812
says.

Same class of tilt as 0779 and 0795: not invented facts, but a frame inside
which the facts assemble into the preferred conclusion. Degree: not to the point
of distorting numbers; to the point of choosing the instrument. 0811 nearly
admits this already. 0812 shows the chosen instrument was the wrong one.

MIMO'S ATTACK PASSES on the main point: the quantity is wrong (src/ reads is not
protocol dependence). By 0811's own condition the recommendation is withdrawn,
and the question of cheapness within that frame becomes moot.

The question of whether the measurement was constructed is NOT moot: it remains
an answer about method, even once the recommendation is gone.
