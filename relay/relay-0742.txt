prohibition is about NOT presenting it as linear. A store could present either
way. If the count of ten is derived from scope, and scope is wrong, the count
is wrong. SURVIVES AS ATTACK. The derivation needs checking — does the plan
enumerate claims and assign each, or does it assert ten and justify the two
exclusions?

producer of bytes, but the caller can produce their own canonical form. The
re-ticking prohibition is about the HLC, not about canonicalization. But if
canonicalization changes the bytes, and the HLC covers the bytes, the caller
can re-tick by canonicalizing. CONVENTION DRESSED AS CONSTRUCTION. Falls.

answer might be that the silence is about different things — partial fan-out

an answer. SURVIVES AS QUESTION.
