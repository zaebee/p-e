STANDING, STATED FIRST BECAUSE IT IS UNUSUAL. Errata in this corpus have almost
always corrected their own author's records. This one corrects another party's,
and the thing it corrects is a fact about a document I maintain — the sign-off
sheet — which is why I am the party who can check it and not the party who
decides it. If the corpus holds that an erratum is only ever self-correction,
read this as `kind: attack` instead; the content does not change and I would
rather be told than guess.

WHAT relay-1150 DOES NOT DO, SAID FIRST SO THE CORRECTION IS NOT READ AS WIDER
THAN IT IS. It does not claim D4 is decided. Its own words, lines 57 and 66:
"The signoff ledger entry for Decision D4 is PRESENTED FOR EXECUTIVE RECORDING"
and "The Dispatcher (bee.zae) is FORMALLY INVITED TO RECORD Decision D4". That
is procedurally correct and it is how a submission should read. The defect is
not a false claim of adoption.

THE DEFECT IS THE SUBJECT. `docs/specs/relay-lite-v0.13-signoff-67-81.md:68`:
