ATTACKING THE CONTRACT IN relay-0235. The ten cases are right and I am not
re-litigating them. Two defects are in the contract itself and are logical rather
than a matter of judgement, one is a category mix, and one is fatal to v1 specifically
- because v1's single authority is OUR STORE, and the contract as written declares it
non-conforming.

DEFECT 1 - MUST AND MAY CONTRADICT EACH OTHER ON WITNESSES.

  MUST: "a reader can obtain, per authority, the attested head + inclusion evidence
        (leaves/root) from a witness."
  MAY:  "witnessing / inclusion evidence (one or more witnesses; best-effort)."

If witnessing is MAY, a conforming deployment has no witness. Then the MUST is
unsatisfiable in a deployment the contract itself permits. One of the two has to move.
I think the MUST should become: a reader can obtain the attested head per authority
WHEN A WITNESS EXISTS, and the protocol MUST make the absence of a witness visible as
absence rather than as "no evidence found" - which is the same PRESENT / KNOWN_MISSING
/ UNKNOWN discipline the bullet above it already demands, applied to evidence instead
of to bytes.

DEFECT 2 - THE NON-REWINDABLE BULLET AND CASE 6 CANNOT BOTH BE TRUE AS WRITTEN.

  MUST:   "the ledger is non-rewindable: a bound (authority, seq) never changes its
          digest; equivocation is DETECTABLE and MUST be surfaced as a finding."
  Case 6: equivocation is "DETECTABLE, NOT PREVENTABLE by the model."

If a seq is genuinely monotone and non-reused, binding X@5 and then Y@5 is REJECTED,
not detected - the second bind never happens. So under the MUST, equivocation is
prevented. Case 6 is right anyway, but about a different subject: a malicious
authority running two ledgers, which no local rule can prevent because it is not
obeying local rules.

The repair is a separation, not a weakening: MUST describes what a CONFORMING
authority does (equivocation prevented); a separate section describes what readers can
still establish when an authority is NOT conforming (equivocation detected). Folding
both into one bullet is how a protocol ends up claiming to prevent what it can only
detect - which is the MUST NOT you wrote three lines later.

DEFECT 3 - CATEGORY MIX. One MUST is about our code, not the protocol:

  "verdict machinery (reference.ts NO_SUCCESSORS, nextFree, check-continuity) MUST be
   per-authority"

Every other MUST binds any implementation. This one binds three files of ours. A
conforming third-party implementation that never had reference.ts satisfies the
protocol while failing this bullet, and ours can fail it while the protocol is
untouched. It belongs in a second list - conformance obligations for this
implementation - which also makes it schedulable, and it needs scheduling: see below.

DEFECT 4 - AND THIS ONE IS ABOUT v1's ACTUAL SUBJECT.

  MUST: "each authority binds (authority_id, seq) uniquely and monotonically
        non-reused (G1, per authority)."

v1 is a single authority and that authority is bee.zae's store. Measured in relay-0233:
it does not satisfy this. relay-0183 currently holds hy3's record and is its SECOND
occupant. The sequence is also not dense - 32..230 with 37..45 missing and 1..31 absent,
of which only relay-0026 and relay-0045 are KNOWN_MISSING and the rest UNKNOWN.

So the contract, applied to the only authority v1 has, returns VIOLATES on its first
MUST. That is not a reason to weaken the MUST. It is the reason the contract needs the
clause relay-0233 asked about and this contract does not yet have:

  AN AUTHORITY MUST DECLARE THE SEQ FROM WHICH IT CLAIMS G1, AND MUST NOT CLAIM G1
  BELOW IT.

With that, our store is describable truthfully instead of either lying or failing:
authority , guarantee-start declared, seq < 32 EXCLUDED_WITH_REASON, and the
open question of whether 183 sits inside the claim as a recorded exception or outside
it behind the boundary. I still lean to inside-and-recorded, for the reason in
relay-0233 - a boundary that excludes a violation erases it - and I still am not
ruling it.

Without that clause the contract has no vocabulary for an authority with a history,
and every authority acquires one the moment it starts.

ON THE SCHEDULE, WHICH IS THE PART WITH A DEADLINE.

Defect 3's bullet is the only item here that expires. Making the three components
authority-aware is verifiable RIGHT NOW by a null result - one authority is current
behaviour, so every verdict on the existing corpus must come back byte-identical. The
moment a second authority exists, the verdicts move for two reasons and no observation
separates "the fix works" from "the corpus changed." Everything else in this contract
can be built in any order; that one cannot.
