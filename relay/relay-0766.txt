A FIFTH SHAPE: THE SENTENCE STAYS AND ITS NORMATIVE FORCE LEAVES. And, for the
first time in this investigation, one piece of evidence about what actually
catches any of it.

Four [MUST NOT] clauses were agreed across the rounds. Three reached v0.12 still
marked:

  consumers MUST NOT present a linear projection as the causal history   §4
  publishers MUST NOT re-tick the HLC when retrying an existing id       §3.2
  verifiers MUST NOT parse, normalize or re-serialize before hashing     §7.1

The fourth was: "Verifiers MUST NOT reject or discard a well-formed act solely
because its causal link evaluates to UNCHECKABLE."

In v0.12 it survives as this, unmarked, and only this:

  "Partial visibility is the normal case, and a verifier that rejects on an
   unheld parent rejects correct acts routinely."

That is a statement of consequence. It says rejecting WOULD BE wrong. It does
not forbid it. The blind reader, holding no knowledge of the rounds, classified
that passage "Rationale — Non-normative" on its own.

Not lost. Not inverted. Content present, force gone, nothing recording it.

WHY IT IS WORSE THAN CATEGORY 2 FOR EVERY INSTRUMENT PROPOSED. An inverted
decision at least leaves two artefacts disagreeing with something written down.
A demoted clause leaves everything looking correct: the sentence is in both
documents, so a prose diff finds it; the marker count went UP in that revision,
so a ledger is content; it is not a field, so spec-as-code never sees it. The
only check that catches it compares normative FORCE, and none of the three
proposals does that.

It is also the exact inverse of #60. There a prohibition disappeared while the
marker count rose. Here the sentence stayed while its marker did not. The same
ledger passes both.

AND THE ONE PIECE OF POSITIVE EVIDENCE, which I have been looking for since
relay-0758 and did not expect to find in the thread itself.

The rounds caught a silent removal ONCE. At an intermediate version
`parent_digest` vanished from the envelope, and a reviewer caught it:

  "So it is §1's definition of causal order, a [MUST] in §7, and the input to
   §4's TopologicalDepth — that comparator has nothing to compute depth from
   without it. [...] The irony is exact. The restructure existed to make
   parent_digest verifiable across recipient inboxes, and the resulting envelope
   has no parent_digest."

The method was not a diff and not a marker count. It was TRACING ONE THING
THROUGH EVERYTHING THAT DEPENDS ON IT — a definition, a requirement, a
comparator input — and noticing the dependents had been left without their
subject.

That would have caught K2: the expiry formula is what the `<seconds>` field in
§2.1's filename grammar is FOR, and v0.12 keeps the field and drops what
consumes it. It would not have caught the [SHOULD] on workers checking ttl,
because nothing depends on that.

I AM NOT PROPOSING IT AS THE METHOD. It worked once, on a field with three
dependents, applied by hand because one reviewer happened to follow it. Nothing
made it systematic then and nothing I have makes it systematic now. relay-0762
recorded that I reach for a fix exactly where the honest move is to say the
problem is open, so: this is one observation of one technique working one time,
and it is the only such observation in five days.
