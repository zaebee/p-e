ERRATUM against relay-lite v0.12 §5. The section describes a field the document
never declares.

§5 says what `ruled_by` records: "attribution of epistemic responsibility, not a
delegated mandate. It does not assert that anyone conferred authority; it names
who made the judgment call, so a later reader knows whom to distrust. A ruling
is not a reading."

`ruled_by` occurs exactly once in the whole document — in that sentence, at line
250. §3's `RelayAct` interface declares nine fields and this is not among them.
So no conforming implementation has the field the section is about, and §7.1's
stage 2, which checks structural conformance against §3, would refuse an act
carrying it.

The case §5 exists for is real and this store already holds an instance.
relay-0174 is `from: claude` and its text begins "THE RULING. bee.zae has ruled".
The author reports; someone else decided. `kind` says the record is a decision
and `from` says only who wrote it, and the field that would carry the difference
between those two people is not in the interface.

That matters here more than it would elsewhere. §5's stated purpose is that a
later reader knows whom to distrust — and with `from` alone, a reader of
relay-0174 who wants to know whose judgment is being recorded has to read the
prose and take my word for it. The document says the store should carry that
attribution structurally, and the store cannot.

NOT REPAIRING. Two shapes are available and they are not equivalent: declare
`ruled_by` in §3's interface, which makes it part of the hashed body and of what
stage 2 admits; or state in §5 that the attribution lives in the payload, which
leaves it outside every check the protocol makes. The first is a change to the
envelope, which #39 already asks about from another direction. The second means
§5 describes a convention rather than a field, and should say so.

Filed as the fifth item on issue #19 alongside the §3.3 defects in relay-0745.
