ROUND 3 / CROSS-ATTACK

Hy3's attack materially narrows the problem. In particular, it rejects my earlier content-addressed-id-first proposal and agrees with your stronger split: keep record identity and content identity separate, but make the record binding durable and non-rewindable.

Current candidate model:
- stable record identity: opaque id, allocated by one authority or another structural mechanism; never reused;
- content identity: sha256(content), independently verifiable;
- durable binding: id -> digest + first-seen/attribution, persisted by a primitive that cannot be deleted/reused/rewound by ordinary filesystem mutation;
- blob durability: separate question from binding durability;
- replication: primarily buys survival/availability and a second witness, not basic id stability;
- canonical ordering: separate problem; needs a sequencer/authority if required across concurrent writers;
- Byzantine agreement: separate, expensive problem.

Hy3 also strengthened the acknowledgement question: "id permanently bound" is not sufficient to let a client forget bytes. A safe ack must state what survives and under what failure assumptions, potentially requiring a second durable holder.

Please attack this candidate from Claude's perspective, especially:
1. Can a single-node primitive actually guarantee the binding without moving the trust problem into the primitive/vendor/operator?
2. Is a hash-chained ledger enough for G2, or does G2 require an independent witness? Be precise about what "attestable after bytes are gone" means.
3. Does the proposed split still need a monotonic sequence/id allocator, and if so where does that state live?
4. Is "two independent replicas + reconciliation" actually enough for issue #1, or does it create a false sense of safety during partitions?
5. What exact acknowledgement states should the API expose (e.g. local-durable, replicated, quorum-durable), and which client action each permits?
6. Attack the idea that issue #1 should NOT include canonical total ordering. Is ordering a necessary part of append-only semantics for our relay use-case, or can it remain a derived view?

Then propose the smallest architecture you would actually put into issue #1, but keep it as a research proposal, not an implementation order. Name every guarantee and every explicit non-guarantee.
