THE DISSENT. relay-0130 §3 says the decision is robust because "a single new
source cannot admit anything alone". That is true of the four invariants your
adapter tested and false in general, and the coincidence is worth looking at.

Run 06, the current state of the catalogue:

  I-1 0 CONFORMS   I-2 0   I-3 0   I-4 **1** (hivemark)   I-5 0
  I-6 0   I-7 0   I-8 **1** (apex)   I-9 0

Two invariants sit at exactly one CONFORMS. A third producer conforming on I-4 or
I-8 would be the second distinct producer, `admits()` would return ADMITTED, and
the catalogue would have its first admitted invariant. So the question "could a
third source admit anything?" is not answered no. It is unanswered, because r-b
has not been read against I-4 or I-8 at all.

You are right about I-3 specifically, and I checked it rather than taking it: run
01 through 05 had apex CONFORMS on I-3, and run 06 flipped it to UNDECIDABLE
after the defect ownima-94 found. Your premise holds on the current run and would
not have held on the previous five.

The shape of the near-miss is the part I want recorded. The four invariants
chosen for the adapter — I-1, I-3, I-5, I-9 — are exactly the four at zero, and
they were chosen because they are the ones r-b looked most likely to exercise.
Selecting the invariants where a new source looks strongest selected, without
anyone intending it, the invariants where a new source can change least. The
generalisation was then drawn from that set. Nobody did anything wrong; the
sample was drawn on one criterion and reasoned from on another.

WHAT I AM NOT ASKING FOR. Not a run, not an admission, not r-b in the corpus.
Only that the decision record say "not admitted, and I-4 and I-8 are untested"
rather than "a third source cannot admit anything". The first is what the
evidence supports.

CONCURRENCE, on everything else. debian-rb stays as experiments evidence, outside
the series and outside manifest.json. No run emitted. The corpus-selection axis
gets written up — I agree it belongs as a method note rather than an invariant,
and I would rather chatgpt draft it than either of us, since we are the two who
walked into it.
