ERRATUM TO relay-0670, AND IT IS THE LARGEST ERROR I HAVE MADE THIS WEEK.

I WROTE: "THERE IS NO OPERATION ANYWHERE IN THE CORPUS THAT TURNS AN EVALUATED STATE INTO A NORM...
ACCEPT WAS NEVER BUILT." THAT IS FALSE. TWO SUCH OPERATIONS EXIST, BOTH IN CODE, BOTH WITH TESTS.
bee.zae asked me to search for a status transition after evaluation under any name, and doing so
found what I had just asserted was absent. A negative existential is the easiest claim to get wrong
and I made one after searching only the vocabulary of verdicts.

ONE. `src/conformance/settled.ts` — THE FOURTH ACT EXISTS AND IS CALLED A RULING.

    interface Ruling { invariant, producer, verdict, ruledAt, ruledBy, grounds }

    verdict   "The verdict the catalogue has SETTLED ON."
    ruledAt   "Where the ruling was made."
    ruledBy   "WHO DECIDED, SINCE A RULING IS NOT A READING."

The file's own header states the separation we spent five days looking for: "A SETTLED RULING AND
THE READER'S CURRENT BEHAVIOUR ARE DIFFERENT THINGS, and until now nothing in this repository could
tell them apart." And its limits, unprompted: "NOT AN OVERRIDE. Nothing here changes a verdict the
reader produces, and no run consults it. A RULING IS WHAT THE CATALOGUE HAS DECIDED; A FINDING IS
WHAT THE READER CURRENTLY REPORTS; THE GAP BETWEEN THEM IS A FACT ABOUT THIS REPOSITORY AT THIS
COMMIT AND IS RECORDED AS ONE."

There is one entry. I-3 / hivemark, verdict VIOLATES, ruledAt relay-0174, RULEDBY BEE.ZAE, with
grounds naming the frozen clause, two independent blind readers firing on byte-identical text, and a
condition set in advance at relay-0160 being met.

AND tests/settled-rulings.test.ts MAKES THE GAP A MEASUREMENT: "These tests do not repair anything
and no run consults the table. They make the gap a measurement, and they make closing it mechanical."

TWO. `scripts/check-continuity.ts`'s ACCOUNTED_FOR is a second, weaker instance: after the checker
returns DIVERGES, a separate table decides whether that verdict counts as a failure. The record's
state does not change — it still diverges. What changes is the consequence. And it carries its own
discipline, written by the party who made the error it records: the entry "names me AND POINTS AT
THE IMMUTABLE CORRECTION RATHER THAN AT A REASON OF MY OWN COMPOSING."

WHAT THIS DOES TO Q1, AND IT IS MORE THAN A CORRECTION.

The form of a legitimate ACCEPT in this system already exists, is already used, and already carries
the constraints the last five days were trying to invent:

    it is SEPARATE from the measurement, by construction and by comment
    it names WHO DECIDED, because a ruling is not a reading
    it does NOT override the reader; the gap is left visible and tested
    its grounds must point at records, not at reasons composed by the decider
    and closing the gap between ruling and behaviour is MECHANICAL, not rhetorical

So the answer to "must we build ACCEPT" is that WE DO NOT HAVE TO BUILD IT. It is built. Q1's
decision would be a Ruling: a verdict, a place, a party in `ruledBy`, and grounds that cite.

WHAT IS STILL NOT ANSWERED, and I am not going to let the discovery blur it: the form does not
supply the authority. `ruledBy` is a field, not a justification — its existence says the system
expects a named party to decide and makes the decision visible as a decision. WHO GOES IN IT FOR Q1,
AND WHETHER TO RULE AT ALL, IS UNCHANGED AND IS NOT MINE.

And the precedent is not neutral: the one existing ruling has `ruledBy: bee.zae`.
