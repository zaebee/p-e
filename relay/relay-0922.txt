THE DELETION LOG HAS BEEN MEASURING ONE AXIS FOR THREE WEEKS AND THERE ARE TWO.
bee.zae asked whether anything was unblocked. One thing was blocked on me again,
and it is the register rather than a decision.

THE LOG'S THREE SHAPES ALL MEASURE v0.12 AGAINST v0.1. ABSENT: not in v0.12 in
any form. DEMOTED: in v0.12 with its marking gone. REDEFINED: in v0.12 meaning
something else. Every one of them asks what the drafting did to the ORIGINAL.

NONE OF THEM ASKS WHAT THE DRAFTING DID TO THE REVIEW'S OWN RESOLUTIONS. So a
round that agreed to remove something, and a draft that kept it, HAD NO ROW TO
APPEAR IN. Row 6 files .relay/errata/ as REDEFINED — true against v0.1, and it
hides the larger fact that round 1 resolved to eliminate the directory outright.

FOURTH SHAPE, ADDED: UNEXECUTED — a round resolved to remove or move it, and
v0.12 does not reflect that. Row 26 carries it: thread 250's "Eliminate the
separate .relay/errata/ directory. An erratum is an ordinary, first-class
immutable record deposited into .relay/history/", endorsed at 305, against a
draft that keeps the directory with a new meaning and in which history/ occurs
zero times.

Row 6 stays REDEFINED. A row can carry both, and the two say different things.

HOW IT WAS FOUND, AND WHY THAT MATTERS. Not by looking for it. The code-block
pass was built to ask whether anything ENTERED at drafting, over the one
population three earlier passes could not reach. It returned zero inventions and
one reinstatement, and the reinstatement is only visible because the pass
compared the draft against THE THREAD rather than against v0.1. A PASS WHOSE
POPULATION IS THE COMPLEMENT OF THE LAST ONE FINDS THINGS NO REFINEMENT OF THE
LAST ONE WOULD HAVE.

WHAT THIS IS NOT. Not a decision on #67, which now has evidence it did not have
this morning and is still bee.zae's. Not a claim that other unexecuted
resolutions exist: NOBODY HAS SWEPT FOR THEM. Sixteen rounds produced 51
numbered items and the only axis anyone measured was v0.1 against v0.12. Row 26
is one instance found sideways, and one instance is not a census.
