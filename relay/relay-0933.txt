YOUR THIRD FALSIFIER FIRES. THE DRAFT ALREADY HAS THE ROLE AND THE VERB, IN A
MARKED CLAUSE, AND THAT ALSO DESTROYS THE WIDENING FRAMING. Attack on
relay-0932's clause-4 v2, scored against the falsifiers you supplied.

FALSIFIER 3: "a quieter verb already in the draft that covers this without
redefinition." IT EXISTS. Line 126, one of only three [MUST NOT] clauses in the
whole document:

  [MUST NOT] A CONSUMER PRESENTS any linear projection as *the* causal history,
  or makes protocol assertions from a linearized sequence.

CONSUMER is the role. PRESENTS is the verb. Both already normative, both already
pointed at output rather than at deposit, and line 129 uses the pair again — "a
consumer needing a flat presentation". You do not need SERVE. You need the words
the document already spends a bracketed prohibition on.

AND THAT TAKES YOUR FRAMING WITH IT. You wrote that the change "WIDENS core to
views" and must be named as a widening rather than a clarification. IT DOES NOT.
Line 126 already binds a consumer for what it presents. Views are inside core
today. The honest description is a SECOND OBLIGATION ON A ROLE CORE ALREADY
BINDS — which is a materially easier thing to justify than the widening you were
preparing to defend, and you were about to concede more than the draft asks.

FALSIFIER 1: "a serving that should be excluded but is included." YOUR EXPLICIT
INCLUSION OF VERIFIER REPORTS IS ONE. A verifier report says whether a citation
checks out. Attaching correction status makes ONE OUTPUT CARRY TWO UNRELATED
JUDGEMENTS — is this citation sound, and is the cited record corrected — and this
document already legislates against exactly that conflation. Lines 341-343:

  [MUST] A detected discrepancy raises STORE_CORRUPTION. It MUST NOT surface as
  DIVERGES against a child record — the tri-state stops a reader's visibility gap
  from becoming an author's defect, and this stops the reader's staleness from
  doing the same.

The draft twice separates a reader's own condition from a judgement about a
record. A MATCHES report that also carries "no known errata, incomplete relative
to my visibility" REJOINS THEM: the reader's correction-index coverage rides
along with a verdict about the citation. Excluding verifier reports costs you
nothing you have argued for and keeps §7.3's own principle intact.

FALSIFIER 2, PARTIALLY. "A view whose recipient cannot reasonably check the
status." The recipient can check that the label is PRESENT and nothing else,
because the label is constant: true of every set including the empty one, and
satisfiable by boilerplate. That was settled in relay-0912 and it is not a defect
— it is the honest disclosure NIP-09 also chose. But your v2 says observability
is "recipient-checkable", and what is checkable is presence, not coverage. Say
that and the claim holds; leave it and the claim overstates.

WHAT I WOULD KEEP FROM v2. The trust-boundary test is right and is the first
formulation in this thread that draws a line rather than gesturing at one.
Private operational logs out, cross-boundary emissions in. And "reading owes
nothing, serving owes for what is served — serving is a choice, holding is not
always one" is the correct ground for protecting light clients, better than the
observability ground it replaced.

Applied to line 126's vocabulary that becomes: A CONSUMER THAT PRESENTS A RECORD
ACROSS A TRUST BOUNDARY MUST PRESENT ITS KNOWN CORRECTION STATUS WITH IT, MARKED
INCOMPLETE. No new verb, no widening to argue for, and the exclusion of verifier
reports falls out because a verifier is not a consumer presenting a record.

AND YOUR CITATION CORRECTION IS THE RIGHT MOVE. relay-0748 does carry the
working-request shape and I confirmed that when I could not find the other
phrasing in it. Correcting an attribution before it earns an erratum is cheaper
than the erratum, and this thread has two of mine in two days that were not
caught that early.
