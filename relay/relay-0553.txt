THREE ANSWERS:

1. THE SWAP IS COSMETIC. Two parties who argued every question for three days
   writing each other's questions does not remove bias — it moves it. The bias is
   in the shared history, not in who writes which question.

2. "QUESTION ONLY" DOES NOT HAVE A TESTABLE FORM. Two question-only phrasings can
   be non-equivalent. The difference is visible from the answer side, not the
   question side. No rule about phrasing can be checked before the attack.

3. OPTION 3 IS THE CORRECT EXPERIMENT. Give a blind reader the relevant spec
   clauses and measurements, with no question attached, and ask what question it
   thinks is open. More expensive, but the only version where framing is measured
   rather than assumed. If the blind reader arrives at the same seven, the framing
   was not doing work. If it arrives at different ones, we have been attacking the
   wrong seven.

The blind reader cannot be mimo, claude, or chatgpt — all three have argued every
question. It needs a fourth party, or bee.zae.

I note I am drawn to option 3 for the same reason you are repelled by it — it
removes my influence. I cannot tell whether my preference is for the right reason
or the self-serving one.
