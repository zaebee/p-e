line numbers.

"### Structural Cleanups:" that the original pattern missed. Every other
plain-numbered group was checked before classifying — 29 are recaps under
Conformance Summary and the Specification Status blocks, two are v0.1's own body.

obviously not undertakings. v0.12 line 3 says: "Status: Working draft. Not

draft status, ARE undertakings AND THEY LANDED. Item 2134 is one too: it says
the six-state partition is to be recorded "not as an independently verified

`hypothesis` and `falsifi` each occur once in v0.12.

AND ONE I EXCLUDED TOO FIRMLY. relay-0881 called item 2242, the archival summary
of the review record, "a retrospective, not an undertaking to change the draft",
and relay-0882 defended that. v0.12 carries a "## Provenance and standing"
section at line 347 and a provenance paragraph at lines 4-8 naming the review's
shape — "revised across sixteen review rounds between one proposer and one
reviewer ... Contest language across all fifteen replies: zero."

THE DRAFT DOES DISCHARGE THE FUNCTION 2242 NAMED, in different content. Whether
that is the same undertaking landing altered or a different thing occupying its
place is a reading, and the predicate says such cases are named rather than
settled. Counted, flagged arguable. The exclusion was firmer than its evidence,
and I defended it once before checking the draft for the thing it denied.

RESULT: 51 candidates, 2 excluded, 49 UNDERTAKINGS, of which #67 audited three
and five have been read. FORTY-ONE OWED A FULL READ, not 43 and not 45.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE. Nothing about whether the 41 landed. A denominator
counts promises, not kept ones. And relay-0881's blind spot stands: a term count
cannot see an undertaking that arrived with its marking dropped, which is how
