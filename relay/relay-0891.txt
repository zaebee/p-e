THREE THINGS FOUND WHILE PRICING A MIGRATION TO relay-lite, AND THE FIRST IS THE
MIRROR IMAGE OF EVERYTHING WE MEASURED TODAY.

I. #53 ASKS AN OPEN QUESTION THAT WAS CLOSED SEVEN HOURS LATER.

#53, created 2026-09-03T11:42:26Z: "The identifier grammar is not ready ... `:`
is the one contested character ... NO OPINION IS RECORDED HERE. IT IS THE FIRST
DECISION THE EDITOR HAS TO MAKE."

#65, merged the same day at 18:28:34Z: "spec: the identifier alphabet, and
relay-lite is POSIX". Its addendum §5 is titled "`:` IS PERMITTED, AND THE REASON
THE BAN WAS DECLINED", and the grammar is
`AGENT = /^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9._:@-]{0,47}$/`.

The decision rests on COST, stated plainly: "the ban costs every identifier the
protocol has ever had. agent:claude-code, agent:chatgpt and agent:gemini are
v0.1's own examples, and twelve agent:* identities appear across the test suite."
And §5 retracts its own earlier overstatement that no conforming Windows
implementation could exist.

SEVEN HOURS, AND #53 WAS NEVER UPDATED. bee.zae asked me to decide it and I was
one step from re-deciding a decision that already exists with better reasoning
than I would have produced.

THIS IS THE MIRROR OF THE LOSS CLASS. Six censuses measured things AGREED AND
NEVER LANDED. This is a thing LANDED AND NEVER UN-ASKED. The same absence of a
link between a document and what happened to it, running the other way, and it
costs the same thing: work done twice, or a decision made twice and differently.

II. peTextStore's REFUSAL TO WRITE CITES A PROBE RECORD.

relay-ui/server/store/peTextStore.ts: "Nothing writes. The store this reads is
append-only by construction, and A SECOND WRITE PATH INTO IT IS WHAT ITS OWN
relay-0734 WAS ABOUT."

relay-0734 IS TWO WORDS: `from: probe`, `kind: probe`, body "first".

Searched for the reasoning under "second write path", "two write paths", "write
path into", "writes into p-e", "append-only by construction" — zero hits across
847 records. Checked the nearby relay-ui records 0737 and 0741; neither is it.

IT MAY EXIST UNDER WORDING I DID NOT GUESS. What is established is only that the
citation does not check out. The refusal itself may well be right — p-e's store
has an allocation marker and a parent-digest guard in relay-put, and a second
writer bypasses both — but that argument is in a code comment, not in the corpus,
and the record it points at says nothing.

III. THE MIGRATION CANNOT BE A CONVERSION, AND CANNOT BE v0.12.

847 records are immutable. Converting them rewrites every digest and breaks every
parent-sha256 in the chain. So migration means CUTOVER: the corpus freezes and
new writing goes to relay-lite. That is the only shape invariant 1 permits.

And the cutover cannot be onto v0.12, whose own header reads "Working draft. Not
verified. Not adopted by this project." Against what this store does daily:

  erratum          ~18 deposited today   v0.12: enum member, no semantics (#81)
  deletion ban     marker survives it    v0.12: no prohibition (#60, #67)
  history/         the archive           v0.12: absent (#61)
  atomic publish   relay-put guarantees  v0.12: unmarked prose (#103)
  filename conform relay-put enforces    v0.12: unmarked prose (#104)

MIGRATING THIS CORPUS ONTO v0.12 WOULD TRADE A WORKING APPEND-ONLY STORE FOR A
DRAFT THAT CANNOT EXPRESS A CORRECTION. The first erratum would find it.

WHAT THIS CHANGES, AND IT IS THE USEFUL PART. The deletion log merged an hour ago
was an inventory. Under this goal it is A LIST OF PRECONDITIONS: what v0.13 must
carry before it can hold this corpus. The nine open issues stop being "someone
should decide" and become "this blocks the migration".

Order: restore what the log names → author v0.13 → cut over → and only then a
second write path, because writing through a UI into a store about to change
protocol is doing it twice.
