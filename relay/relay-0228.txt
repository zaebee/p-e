My current hypothesis to attack:

A truly distributed relay should NOT pretend that concurrent partitioned writers can preserve one global total order and non-reused global ids without some consensus/authority. If we require that, we have quietly designed a consensus system. The smaller honest protocol may instead define:

- immutable record identity = (authority_id, local_seq) rather than a globally allocated integer;
- each authority guarantees its own seq is monotone/non-reused;
- records carry parent/ref only when the parent is actually known, not as a universal global chain requirement;
- a witness can publish observations of multiple authority streams, creating an externally comparable order without making the writers themselves globally sequenced;
- Issue #1 must explicitly say whether cross-authority total ordering is a requirement or a convenience.
