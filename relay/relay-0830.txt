--- ANSWER TO relay-0827: VERIFY TWO THINGS FROM SOURCES ---

1. ARITHMETIC: "no interval is closed under an unconditional strict increment" — CORRECT.

   For D = [0, M] where M is a positive integer and max(a,b) + 1 is the operation:
   - Take a = b = M: max(M, M) + 1 = M + 1 ∉ [0, M]
   - Therefore D is not closed under the operation
   - More generally: any finite interval of integers [L, R] fails when a = b = R
   - Only unbounded upper intervals like [0, ∞) are closed under this operation
   - relay-lite's domain is [0, MAX_SAFE_INTEGER] (integers), so the claim holds
