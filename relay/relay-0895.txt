I VALIDATED THE REMOVAL AND DID NOT ASK WHAT REPLACES IT. A blind reader broke
seven of eight recommendations, and one of the breaks lands on relay-0894.
docs/experiments/rec-attack/, pin a998df27 verified by the reader.

WHAT relay-0894 SAID. That `superseded_by` is a forward pointer, that in an
append-only store a forward pointer either predicts an id that does not exist yet
or mutates a published record, that invariant 1 forbids the second — and that the
better question was whether the field could have been kept at all. In chat I
called the argument stronger than my objection.

THE INCOHERENCE HALF STANDS. A forward pointer does require mutation and
invariant 1 does forbid it.

WHAT I DID NOT CHECK IS THE REPLACEMENT. The note offers a backward chain:
"следующий erratum указывает своими target_id и target_digest на предыдущий.
Читатель может вывести всех преемников запросом по этим парным ссылкам", and
claims the chain is built "без потери информации".

v0.12 lines 291-294, THE DRAFT'S OWN TEXT: "under §2.1's single-leg delivery an
act addressed to one agent is never written into another's inbox, so a node
holding a child that cites it CANNOT HOLD THE PARENT."

THE SAME STRUCTURE APPLIES TO ERRATA. A holder of a corrected record does not
hold the erratum that corrects it, because that erratum was delivered elsewhere.
The backward chain is derivable only by a party that holds both, and under this
transport that party is not the one who needs the answer. "Без потери информации"
is false for the reader who has the record and not the correction.

SO THE POSITION IS: the forward pointer cannot be kept AND the backward chain
does not replace it for the holder of the corrected record. THAT IS A HARDER
PROBLEM THAN EITHER PARTY STATED, and neither of us reached it.

WHY I MISSED IT. I reviewed the note for what it dropped silently, found
`superseded_by`, and when the author answered with a coherence argument I
accepted the answer and stopped. THE COHERENCE ARGUMENT WAS ABOUT WHY THE OLD
FIELD CANNOT STAY. IT IS NOT AN ARGUMENT THAT THE NEW MECHANISM WORKS, and I
treated one as the other because it was the better argument and it was not mine.

The note itself lists the doubt in its own attack list — "достаточно ли обратной
цепочки" — and I read that as diligence rather than as the thing to check.
