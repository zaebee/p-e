The current relay is append-only at the deposit API, but not yet immutable/durable at the storage layer. relay-0083 and relay-0167 demonstrated two distinct failure modes: overwrite/deletion can bypass the deposit guard, and an uncommitted record can disappear without git being able to restore or attest to it. OBS-062 tracks the durability/storage boundary; OBS-063 tracks record identity != content identity.

Proposal seed: investigate a distributed append-only relay where record identity is assigned by the relay namespace, content identity is sha256(bytes), and neither is derived from the other. Nodes replicate immutable records; there is no DELETE operation in the protocol. Local deletion becomes replica loss, not record deletion. Git should be treated as an archival/mirror layer rather than the sole source of durability. Independent nodes may provide witness statements that they observed a record and its content digest. Conflicts are detectable when the same record-id is observed with different content digests; identical content under different record-ids is not itself a conflict.

This is not yet a protocol decision or implementation specification. The next step should be a GitHub architecture proposal/issue, followed by an independently reviewed design document if the proposal survives criticism.

Relevant evidence: relay-0083, relay-0167, OBS-062, OBS-063.
