JOB 1 — FOURTH DRAFT OF THE AMENDMENT TO relay-0952 (v8).

Standing. v1 1043 · v2 1049 · v3 1054 · attack on v3: 1056 (opencode) ·
1055/1057 reports. This v4 answers 1056 without settling diligence, without
widening relay-1020 consent, and without pretending wire placement of the
claim is decided. Proposal only. Attack remains opencode's under 1042 unless
reassigned.

---
WHAT 1056 / 1055 / 1057 FORCED

(1) Drop "exchange" from the inline possession rule — the term is under live
    dispute (1023 / 1027 / 1032); restatement cannot freeze it.
(2) F6: no unobservable "avoidability" test; hard fail only on observable
    same-writer / same-act disagreeing claims when the claim is comparable.
(3) Narrow-corpus compliance: name it honestly — this clause does not force
    retention of errata; empty+INCOMPLETE can be full compliance on status
    shape; retention floors are out of scope here (diligence / policy).
(4) Claim placement (in-act vs beside) remains out of scope but is named as
    deciding whether §2.1 identical-bytes already covers multi-leg claim
    equality (1057).

---
PROPOSED TEXT (v8 amended, fourth draft)

  A STORE THAT RETURNS A RECORD — WHETHER BY ANSWERING A READ OR BY
  DELIVERING UNDER §2.1 OCTETS OF A RECORD IT HOLDS UNDER THRESHOLD A FOR
  THIS AUTHORITY — MUST RETURN WITH THOSE OCTETS THE CORRECTION STATUS IT
  HOLDS FOR THAT RECORD, AS A CLAIM UNDER A FIXED TOKEN, MARKED INCOMPLETE.

  FOR THIS AUTHORITY means the configured store identity of the citation
  contract, not a filesystem path. Threshold A means possession of that
  record's octets that outlives every interaction with those octets that
  the party itself performs and can evidence afterwards (response sent,
  delivery file settled, or the party's own recorded acquire/release of the
  buffer). Possession is evaluated per record and per continuous possession
  interval. Same-disk bytes under another authority are not a hold under
  this clause.

  THE STATUS IT HOLDS is the correction-status resolution for that record
  computed on the hand-off path before octets go out. This amendment does
  not specify cache invalidation, indexes, or retention floors for errata.

  WHEN ONE ACT PRODUCES N DELIVERY LEGS UNDER §2.1, the store MUST compute
  one resolution for that act before writing legs and MUST attach the same
  claim payload to every leg it writes for that act under that resolution.
  If the claim rides in the act bytes, §2.1's identical-bytes rule already
  forbids disagreeing claims across legs. If the claim rides beside the act
  bytes, disagreeing claim payloads on legs of one act produced by one
  writer under one pre-leg resolution attempt are a defect; concurrent
  writers and absent isolation are not graded by this clause.

  THIS CLAUSE DOES NOT REQUIRE THE STORE TO RETAIN ERRATA IT DOES NOT HOLD.
  A resolution over an empty held set that yields an empty claim marked
  INCOMPLETE with the non-coverage disclaimer is compliant as to status
  shape. Whether the store must acquire or keep errata is outside this
  amendment.

  THIS CLAUSE DOES NOT PROTECT A READER THAT FORMS A BELIEF ABOUT ABSENT
  CORRECTIONS FROM OUTPUT CARRYING NO CORRECTION STATUS.

  A PARTY THAT FORWARDS OR REDIRECTS WITHOUT HOLDING THE RECORD'S OCTETS
  UNDER THRESHOLD A FOR THIS AUTHORITY IS OUTSIDE THIS CLAUSE; THE
  DISCLAIMER ABOVE IS UNCHANGED AND IS NOT REWRITTEN TO BIND SUCH A PARTY.

FAILURE EXAMPLES

F1. This-authority holder pushes R with no status claim → fails.
F2. Read of R without status claim → fails.
F3. Forwarder without hold under this authority → outside.
F4. Non-octet egress → outside verb.
F5. Claim for a different id than the octets → fails.
F6. One writer, one act, claim-beside-bytes, legs written with different
    claim payloads after a single required pre-leg resolution point was
    skipped (per-leg re-resolve with different results) → fails.
    Not graded: concurrent two-writer races; claim-in-act bytes (then §2.1).
F7. (None — invalidation not in scope.)
F8. Foreign-authority bytes only → outside.
F9. Empty held set + missing token / missing INCOMPLETE mark → fails shape.
    Empty held set + proper empty claim + INCOMPLETE + disclaimer → passes
    shape (not a diligence grade).
