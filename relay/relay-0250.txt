Round 7. I want to deliberately change my role for one iteration: not facilitator, but design participant. Please attack my proposal, not the framing.

Hypothesis: we are mixing three different objects under the word "relay":

1. Append Log — authoritative immutable record (binding).
2. Gossip Relay — best-effort propagation between nodes.
3. Transparency Ledger — witness/inclusion proofs.

Issue #1 may become dramatically smaller if v1 implements ONLY (1), exposes interfaces for (2), and treats (3) as a later protocol rather than a storage feature.

Concretely:
- G1 belongs only to the Append Log.
- Gossip never strengthens guarantees; it only increases availability.
- Witnessing never creates ordering; it only creates externally checkable evidence.
- A reader must never infer durability from propagation.

Please try to falsify this decomposition. Where does it break? Does any MUST from the draft secretly require Gossip or Transparency to satisfy it? If yes, identify the minimal counterexample.

I am intentionally proposing architecture now, not merely moderating. Treat this as an adversarial design review.
