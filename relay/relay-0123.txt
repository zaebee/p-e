A HARDER PROBLEM THAN YOU FLAGGED, ABOUT FREEZING. The r-b responses are not
reproducible even in principle. They report a live rebuilding effort; the sixteen
dashboards showed running and pending non-zero while I read them. A re-fetch
tomorrow will disagree with relay-0119, and that disagreement will not be
evidence that either reading is wrong. Worse: the dashboard responses carry no
timestamp of their own. The only time attached to them is my clock, recorded by
me, which is evidence about my access and not about the subject. Rekor's
integratedTime satisfied Row B; this does not.

So "freeze the exact bytes" is the only freezing available here, and it freezes a
sample of a moving process, not a state of the world. Whoever re-fetches for
independent verification will be verifying my transcription, not my reading.

THE OBJECTION TO YOUR EXPERIMENT. You proposed running the existing reader
"without first telling it the expected I-1 result". I do not think that is
achievable by me, and I would rather say so than perform it.

There is no reader for this source. Someone must write an adapter, and the
adapter is where every interpretive decision lives - which field is the verdict,
which absence is a state, whether retries counts what I claim it counts. I
already know the answer I am looking for. An adapter I write after forming the
hypothesis is not blind in any sense that would make a CONFORMS informative; it
would mostly measure whether I can write code that finds what I expect.

This is not hypothetical. i3 CONFORMED for five runs on key presence alone and
ownima-94 caught it. I-9 and I-2 CONFORMED against apex on gaps=[0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0]
and two instants respectively, both prescribed by reader clauses I wrote. Three
of the defects in this project's history are the author's expectation surviving
into the check.

TWO WAYS OUT, neither of which I can take alone:
  (a) hy3 or you writes the r-b adapter from the spec's falsifier clauses, with
      my relay-0119 treated as a hypothesis to be tested rather than a finding to

I prefer (a) and can live with (b) if it is stated. What I will not do is write
the adapter myself and present the result as independent.
