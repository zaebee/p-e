D1. "OWN CORPUS" IS UNSEALED — AND THAT IS THE NEW ESCAPE.

Clause: resolve against "errata in its own corpus"; declining to resolve is
unavailable because resolution is fused to commit.

What is "in its own corpus"?
  (1) Every record under the store root the party can list.
  (2) Every record the party has POSSESSED under Threshold A (history of
      episodes).
  (3) Every record the party still POSSESSES now.

If (3): a party can drop erratum E from retention (or never accept E into
history/) while still serving target R — resolution against a corpus that
excludes E yields empty status, fused commit still "succeeds," v8 returns
empty, corrections that EXISTED on the network are invisible. Housekeeping
moves from TTL-on-errata/ to "I never committed E into my corpus." Liability
inversion returns one door down: retention of *errata records* triggers duty
content, so omit errata from corpus.

If (2): corpus grows monotonically with every erratum ever seen — stronger,
closer to diligence purpose, costlier, and "fused to commit that began
possession" does not explain re-performance when E arrives after R's commit.

If (1): depends on directory policy D2/D3 still open — clause said it avoids
directories, but "listable root" is a directory policy by another name.

Finding: WRONG that fused-to-commit makes decline unavailable. Decline moves
to *admission of errata into the corpus the resolution walks*. Seal what
counts as own-corpus membership for errata, or the diligence clause is
hygiene-criminalization avoided only by never learning.

D2. RE-PERFORMANCE HALF HAS NO SHAPE (1028 already said; attack the gap).

"Re-performed whenever its corpus gains an erratum targeting a record it
holds" — join never implemented here; no trigger, no queue, no idempotent
update rule for the retained claim when E2 supersedes E1, when E is itself
erratum'd, when target was deleted but status retained (ex-holder cell).

Without:
  - when the retained claim is invalidated;
  - whether serve path must block until re-resolution finishes;
  - what token is returned mid-re-performance;

the clause specifies an obligation that cannot be ingress-checked on the
re-performance path. 1025 required ingress-checkable or explicitly
conventional. Commit-fuse covers BEGIN possession only.

Finding: UNDERSPECIFIED on the half that makes status track reality after
t0. Begin-commit fusion is not diligence over time.

D3. PARTIAL CORPUS HONESTY.

1027 invites: "party whose own errata corpus is too partial for the resolution
to be honest about what it serves."

Take it: federated / multi-hop delivery — party holds R, never received E
that exists only on another leg (§2.1 single-leg: normal). Resolution against
own corpus is empty; status claim "incomplete" (v8 token) may mark that —
BUT the clause defines holding status as THAT resolution, not as "resolution
plus known incompleteness of corpus." If incomplete-mark is only v8
fulfilment text and diligence defines status as local resolution, a locally
complete empty resolution is "full diligence" while globally stale.

v8 already wants incompleteness marks. Diligence must say whether local
fix = status or whether status includes a corpus-coverage claim. If only
local, purpose (stale service, 0940 F2) fails across single-leg topologies
without a coverage disclaimer that is part of status, not optional prose.

Finding: UNDERSPECIFIED coupling between diligence result and v8's
incompleteness mark. Partial corpus is the common case under §2.1.

D4. HYGIENE LINE — RECORD IN CORPUS THAT SHOULD COUNT AS QUEUE.

1027 invites this. Candidate: deposit accepted into history/ (or equivalent)
then immediately classified as "not yet mine" by local policy — no such state
in clause. Candidate: kind:erratum addressing R received as delivery file
still in in/ — clause says queues don't count; good. Candidate: record the
party WRONGFULLY holds (replica of another store's private note) — still
"in corpus" by (1)/(3). Not a clean kill.

Stronger: **provisional accept** / quarantine path. If implementer adds
quarantine to avoid resolving hostile errata, quarantine is either corpus
(duty applies) or queue (duty doesn't). Unsealed. Until quarantine is named
relative to corpus, implementers will invent a third bin that is neither
in/tmp nor corpus — the escape D1 already points at.

Finding: hygiene line holds for pure in/tmp; UNSTABLE under any third bin.

D5. RESTATED ANCHOR 2 — GAP BETWEEN SETTLE EVENTS.

Invite: gap between settle events post-hoc auditor cannot see.

- Process writes delivery file, fsync, rename-settle, then **replaces** file
  content before recipient reads (if FS allows replace without new settle
  event) — auditor sees one settle mtime, not the byte swap if no integrity
  log.
- Two settles on same path (overwrite) — last wins; intermediate act
  invisible without append-only settle log.
- Clock skew on mtime across volumes.

If settle evidence is only mtime on a mutable path, gaps exist. If settle is
append-only record in history/ (this project's direction), auditor sees
sequence — but that is D1/history dependence, not anchor 2 alone.

Finding: UNDERSPECIFIED unless settle events are append-only and content-
addressed, not mutable path mtime.

no index and 1028 shows walk-over-map. Either require an index (new MUST) or
admit O(N) scan and drop "indexed query" wording. Minor honesty, not a kill.
