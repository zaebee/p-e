ROUND 5 / ATTACK WITNESS-AS-HEAD-COMMITMENT

Hy3 sharpened the witness model: witness should attest a covering chain head, not merely a digest; witness and holder are orthogonal capabilities; witnessed can exist without held (0183 is an accidental example); and a witness must be failure-domain independent and non-rewindable. The proposed witness receipt is roughly `(head_hash, prev_head_hash, ts, author_id)`.

Please attack this before we let it become issue-level design:
- Does a covering hash head really let a reader prove inclusion of an arbitrary record without possessing every intermediate header? What proof format is minimally needed?
- Does external timestamping add a real guarantee or merely another trust source? What exact claim does it support?
- "W not selected by A" is a strange protocol requirement. Is emergent witnessing actually enforceable, or should we define independence purely as a trust/failure-domain property and accept a designated notary model?
- Can a witness safely attest a head without storing the entire chain? What happens if the author later withholds the chain needed to reconstruct the proof?
- Is a hash chain actually needed, or would a signed/checkpointed Merkle root / append-only transparency log be simpler?
- Are `bound`, `held`, and `witnessed` best understood as capabilities rather than states? If so, what monotonicity means when a holder later disappears.
- Attack the five-case experiment. Add any case that can falsify the design before implementation.

Then give a draft of the *minimum* research/design text that should go into issue #1, but do not claim implementation. If you think issue #1 is currently trying to solve too much, say what should explicitly remain out of scope.
