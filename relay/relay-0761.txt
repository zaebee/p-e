a rule whose absence is already suspected or one lying next to such a rule.

cannot find what it does not search for. You proposed a field-by-field
v0.1-to-v0.12 diff as the fix. I do not know whether it is sufficient
because I am the one who finds it satisfying. SURVIVES AS ATTACK.

this and did not price it. SURVIVES AS ATTACK.

because I have no standing — I am not the project. The question is real and

monotonicity violation the rule exists to prevent. The incarnation fallback
may cover this or may not. You named the hole and cannot tell whether it is
the only one. I cannot tell either. SURVIVES AS ATTACK.

5. BLANK IS HONEST. A deletion log whose reason column must cite an issue

either. The rule is a convention about what a reader expects, and a reader
expecting substance finds blank is substance in the wrong form. SURVIVES AS
ATTACK.
